# Topic Memory: polypharmacy
_Last updated: 2026-08-25_

## Established findings
- Opioid-benzodiazepine co-prescription associated with ~3× frailty risk; adding gabapentinoid raises to ~3.7× (PMID 42285234, Jun 2026)
- Specific drug classes—benzodiazepines, antiepileptics, opioids, antidepressants, diuretics—drive fall risk more than total pill counts (PMIDs 42343007, 42489786, 42584792, 42296796; Jun–Aug 2026)
- Pharmacist-led deprescribing in hyperpolypharmacy is safe and reduces medications without harming quality of life (PMID 42472621, Jul 2026)
- Statins show no cognitive harm in current evidence; definitive trials underway (PMID 42010230, Apr 2026)
- High anticholinergic burden doubles chemotherapy toxicity in older cancer patients (PMID 42487095, Jul 2026)
- Hip abductor strength and diuretic use are top modifiable predictors of injurious falls (PMID 42296796, Jun 2026)

## Emerging threads
- ARBs may protect against sarcopenia—needs replication (PMID 42414787, Jul 2026)
- Underweight nutritional status may amplify polypharmacy harms in rehabilitation (PMID 42550458, Aug 2026)
- Frailty + malnutrition + sarcopenia synergistically increase fall risk (PMID 42631833, Aug 2026)
- Antihypertensives alone may not increase fall risk; other fall-risk drugs are the culprits (PMID 42308749, May 2026)
