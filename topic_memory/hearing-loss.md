# Topic Memory: hearing loss
_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

## Established findings
- Hearing loss consistently associated with depression across China, U.S., England, Mexico (PMIDs 42119796, 42065922; replicated July and August 2026 runs)
- Hearing aid use linked to reduced frailty risk (NHANES, PMID 42423142; replicated both runs)
- Cognitive decline accelerates after hearing loss onset (HRS 24-year data, PMID 42083497)
- Flavonoid-rich diet associated with lower incident hearing loss (UK Biobank, PMID 42235109; replicated both runs)
- Hearing loss as marker of accelerated biological aging tied to dementia, CVD, diabetes (epigenetic clock, PMID 42419408; replicated both runs)
- Hearing loss identified as global modifiable dementia risk factor clustering with other risks (14-country Lancet analysis, PMID 42437564)

## Emerging threads
- Public awareness of hearing-dementia link remains low despite strong evidence (meta-analysis, PMID 41966599)
- Osteoporosis medications (zoledronic acid, denosumab) may protect hearing (Taiwan, PMID 42574660)
- Racial/ethnic variation in social isolation and loneliness among adults with hearing loss (PMID 40645228)
- Brain network reorganization beyond auditory cortex in presbycusis (EEG/MRI, PMIDs 42609411, 42568626)

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Your Ears as a Window to Aging" – hearing loss as systemic aging sentinel, gateway to integrated geriatric assessment (July 2026 run)
- "The Dementia Risk You Can Actually Do Something About—If Only You Knew" – awareness gap between evidence and public knowledge on hearing-dementia link (August 2026 run)
