# Topic Memory: dementia
_Last updated: 2026-11-05_

## Established findings
- Frailty predicts dementia across four international cohorts; worsening frailty raises risk further, improvement lowers it in some cohorts, and depressive symptoms mediate ~29-45% of the link (PMID 42600788, recurring Sep-Nov 2026); multidomain intrinsic capacity scoring reaches the same conclusion with psychological function the strongest single domain (PMID 42727174, Nov 2026)
- Delirium in hospital is common, frequently missed, and consequential: 55.6% of hospitalized nonagenarians with dementia arrive delirious (PMID 42840854), and a delirium episode carries 3.7x MCI and 5.4x dementia risk over ~7 years (PMID 42970282); live AI alerting doubled detection rates but with a 74% false positive rate (PMID 42981091)
- Caregiver self-report understates real harm; caregiver burden tracks with snoring, sleep latency and sleep-medication use almost entirely among women (PMID 42580292, recurring), and Hispanic/Latino caregivers report full coverage for the patient and nothing for themselves (PMID 42981258)
- Blood biomarkers work in ordinary clinics and earlier disclosure speeds diagnosis without raising distress (PMID 42832237); the frontier is now delivery — nurse-led workups with blood tests and portable MRI in rural practice (PMID 42982740)
- Physical capacity tracks with brain outcomes at scale: cardiorespiratory fitness halved dementia risk across 2.3 million adults with a 7% reduction per MET (PMID 42980900), consistent with gait speed and leg power findings (PMID 42833623)
- Widely used dementia symptom drugs rest on thin or stage-dependent evidence: scheduled acetaminophen no better than placebo (PMID 42843009), RBD drugs mostly subjective small studies (PMID 42623795), memantine modest with no caregiver relief (PMID 42838860), and extended-release amantadine may worsen hallucinations and cognition (PMID 42985541)

## Emerging threads
- Diagnostic overshadowing is becoming measurable: dementia independently predicted fewer heart failure drugs at discharge (PMID 42849399) and cognitive impairment predicted absence of advance care planning (PMID 42586324)
- Ownership and business structure now part of the dementia care story — private equity hospices enrolling more dementia patients, longer stays, more live discharges, higher Medicare spend (PMID 42970733)
- AI as both tool and hazard in geriatric care: documented failure modes including ageist bias and missed brief safety events (PMID 42786110) alongside successful live delirium detection (PMID 42981091)
- Screening infrastructure is the weak link: brief cognitive test sensitivity in primary care ranges 0.17-0.98 (PMID 42983912); community screening can flag one in three but needs a referral pathway (PMID 42985109)
- Cognitive reserve is being refined, not confirmed: bilingualism did not preserve brain structure but loosened the brain-cognition link (PMID 42766942), while out-educating one's parents tracked with lower risk (PMID 42982757); commercial brain training remains far ahead of its evidence (PMID 42587262)
- Mood and distress as dementia-adjacent risk: sleep disturbance tied to suicide risk independent of depression (PMID 42790647); optimism lowers depression risk but does not buffer stressful events (PMID 42567210)
- Palliative and caregiver support gaps outside hospice eligibility — nurse practitioner telehealth reduced caregiver burden in a small pilot (PMID 42631925)
- Tau gains causal-inference support for Alzheimer's and vascular dementia while amyloid results stay inconsistent (PMID 42979808)
