# Topic Memory: cardiovascular disease
_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## Established findings
- Mood is a cardiovascular variable: depression independently raises CVD events and death in diabetes beyond glycemic control, lifetime depression history raises cardiometabolic risk 15 years out with no distinguishing symptom subtype, and depression plus frailty clusters carry steep risk (PMIDs 42624218, 42826766, 42679503, 42821655; Oct 2026–Jan 2027)
- Cardiovascular risk scores track brain health: ASCVD/PREVENT scores correlate with amyloid, tau, cortical thinning, white matter damage and cognitive decline (PMIDs 42898366, 42561811; Oct–Nov 2026)
- CVD's share of health burden in the 80+ population is falling (42.3%→28.6%) while disability's share rises; demand shifts toward function-preserving care, and palliative care is now argued into cardiology itself (PMIDs 42594758, 41879158; recurring through Nov 2026)
- Atherosclerosis is systemic and silent: 57% of asymptomatic adults had plaque, typically femoral/carotid before coronary, echoing the underdiagnosed-PAD literature (PMIDs 42670946, 42772316; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Social and structural exposures carry measurable cardiovascular weight: financial strain and discrimination linked to inflammatory protein shifts in older Black adults with heart disease; men's excess mortality driven largely by modifiable CVD (PMIDs 42843435, 42956774; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Nocturia and sleep-disordered breathing flag cardiovascular risk, consistently stronger in men (PMIDs 42600420, 42969922; Nov–Dec 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Standard risk calculators underperform in older women, especially with CKD, while PREVENT simultaneously anchors large policy projections — a tension worth watching (PMIDs 42934356, 42975393; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Trajectories beat snapshots: cumulative inflammation/insulin-resistance and TyG-obesity scores, plus CGM patterns missed by fasting glucose and HbA1c, outperform single values (PMIDs 42994151, 42988750, 42752803; Nov 2026)
- Reversibility evidence: frailty recovery and depression remission tracked with lower CVD risk; intensive lifestyle programs cut IL-6; exercise type can be matched to a specific risk factor (PMIDs 42679503, 42687462, 42822168; Nov 2026–Jan 2027)
- Implementation over prediction: sequential digital nudges restored statin refills, with persistence beating channel choice (PMID 42667613; Nov 2026)
- Shared-mechanism framing across specialties: osteoporosis and atherosclerosis proposed as linked processes; senescence blood markers tested as surgical risk refiners (PMIDs 42749103, 42993449; Nov 2026–Jan 2027)
- Groups missing from guidance: fewer than 2% of cardiology guidelines mention LGBTQ+ patients (PMID 42870187; Oct 2026)
