# Topic Memory: hearing loss
_Last updated: 2026-07-12_

## Established findings
- Hearing loss consistently associated with depression across China, U.S., England, Mexico (PMIDs 42119796, 42065922; multiple runs)
- Hearing aid use linked to better cognition and reduced dementia risk (meta-analysis PMID 41803081; frailty studies PMIDs 42423142, 41942921)
- Cognitive decline accelerates after hearing loss onset (HRS 24-year data, PMID 42083497)
- Flavonoid-rich diet associated with lower incident hearing loss (UK Biobank, PMID 42235109)
- Social/financial support reduces hearing aid abandonment (Chile, PMID 42045825)

## Emerging threads
- Hearing loss as marker of accelerated biological aging (epigenetic clock, PMID 42419408)
- Chronic pain predicts incident hearing difficulty (six-cohort analysis, PMID 42090330)
- Sensory impairment linked to elder mistreatment risk (PMID 41981819)
- Attention-switching deficits persist beyond peripheral hearing correction (PMID 41525395)
