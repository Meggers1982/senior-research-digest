# Topic Memory: vision loss
_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

## Established findings
- Vision loss is a modifiable dementia risk factor: confirmed across multiple populations and runs (PMID 42437564, 42371784, 40583126, 42212629)
- Older adults often don't recognize treatable vision loss when overshadowed by other health problems (PMID 42271249—repeated across runs)
- Photobiomodulation shows promise for early/intermediate dry AMD (PMID 42259594—repeated across runs)
- Home-based sensory support for people with dementia is feasible with caregiver involvement (PMID 42212629—repeated across runs)

## Emerging threads
- Multiple concurrent eye diseases compound vision loss risk; integrated screening needed (PMID 41249002)
- Type 2 diabetes driving global rise in diabetic retinopathy; type 1-related burden stable/declining (PMID 42476825)
- Gender disparities in vision loss persist globally, tied to structural inequality (PMID 42521465)
- Contrast sensitivity declines early in AMD, before standard acuity tests detect change (PMID 42386395)
- Audiobook transition after vision loss requires active support, not just access (PMID 42500929)
