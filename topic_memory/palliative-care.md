# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

## Established findings
- Palliative referrals arrive too late, and the effective fixes are structural: embedded referral triggers, co-location, routine screening, named accountability, renaming the service (PMID 42504041, 42714185, Oct 2026; PMID 42580497, 42853191, Oct 2026)
- Screening alone changes nothing without an attached pathway — identical consult rates for positive and negative ED screens (PMID 42860763, Oct 2026)
- Only specific written instructions (DNR/do-not-hospitalize) track with fewer hospital transfers; residents are still moved against documented wishes (PMID 42714187, Oct 2026)
- Whole-system nursing home pathways can keep frail residents out of hospital at the end of life (98% died in facility; PMID 42580497, Nov 2026)
- Family caregiver burden shapes how families experience and remember a death, and is rarely screened (PMID 42804262, Sept 2026)
- Preventive drugs linger past usefulness; deprescribing stays reactive and depends on an owned, scheduled conversation (PMID 42519959, 42533730, Oct 2026)
- The pharmacologic evidence base at end of life is thin: null lidocaine trial (42536632), low-certainty propofol sedation (42533745), 50-day median survival after chemo for malignant bowel obstruction (42843988)

## Emerging threads
- Identity and coverage predict end-of-life care intensity: dharmic faiths (42556759), Chinese immigrant elders (42833695), Latino pancreatic cancer families (42842360), and commercial vs Medicaid insurance (42492600) — the last counterintuitive, with better insurance linked to more aggressive care and less hospice
- Prognostic accuracy is population-dependent: 0.90 in nursing home intake data (42748398) vs 0.55–0.76 in advanced dementia (42533729); clinician judgment at heart failure referral also discriminates well (42841449)
- Quantified workforce shortfalls replace anecdote: 106,470 Australians a year unserved by 2045, regional areas worst hit (42546987); rural solo APRNs (42406410)
- Loneliness as an untreated end-of-life symptom — 32.2% in the final year (42492599)
- Opioid policy cuts both ways: age-tailored lower doses gave equal relief (42853544), while China met only ~26% of population pain-relief need (42567370)
- Palliative care expanding beyond oncology into neurology (42714185), cardiac ICU (42844232), and psychedelic-assisted depression treatment during active cancer therapy (42546985)
