# Topic Memory: osteoporosis
_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

## Established findings
- Post-fracture care collapses at the handoff in every health system examined: wrist/forearm breaks rarely coded as osteoporosis, <20% of men treated after fragility fracture, 29.5% of Japanese hip fracture patients starting medication by 2023, and osteoporosis documented in only 19.2% of Croatian post-fracture rehab admissions (PMIDs 42448012, 42735505, 42288172, 42930018; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Screening, knowledge and diagnosis are heavily sex-skewed against men: DXA ordered at 0.04% of eligible men's visits vs 5.1% of women's; men scored worst on osteoporosis knowledge and had the higher silent vertebral fracture rate (PMIDs 42493193, 42600719, 42624233, 42735505)
- Bone and muscle decline as one system; BMI and body weight mislead in both directions, with visceral fat tracking lower density and low BMI predicting sarcopenia (PMIDs 42811242, 42641825, 42928822, 42631701)
- Exercise is the most consistent non-drug lever, with site-specific small-to-moderate density gains across 162 RCTs; chair-based or purely aerobic programming is unlikely to move bone (PMID 42575297)
- High-dose vitamin D in already-sufficient older adults did not prevent fractures over five years — a deprescribing candidate, alongside routine post-hip-fracture X-rays that changed no decisions (PMIDs 42704968, 42732263)
- Vertebral Fracture Assessment finds silent spine fractures in ~16% of a primary care population aged 40–80, most never diagnosed (PMID 42624233)

## Emerging threads
- Detection is outrunning follow-through: AI on existing chest X-rays, urine albumin as a fracture flag, and evidence-graded ultrasound alternatives to DXA, with modeled benefit hinging on referral and medication persistence (PMIDs 42732841, 42928907, 42822751)
- Osteoporosis as a multi-system signal: shared mechanisms with atherosclerosis, compounded risk in women with epilepsy, salt intake linked to both bone and cardiovascular outcomes (PMIDs 42749103, 42810186, 42700476)
- Menopausal hormone therapy re-entering bone care: transdermal/spray delivery and HT-plus-bisphosphonate combinations raise density, but fracture benefit and long-term safety remain unproven, with heavy industry authorship (PMIDs 42753514, 42868070)
- Diet pattern over single nutrients: Mediterranean adherence linked to lower fracture risk; table-salt habit linked to higher osteoporosis risk (PMIDs 42641790, 42700476)
- Bone quality matters for orthopedic hardware, not just fracture risk (PMID 42733408)
