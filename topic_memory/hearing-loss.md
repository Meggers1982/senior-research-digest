## Topic Memory: hearing loss
_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

## Established findings
- Hearing loss tracks with worse memory and faster cognitive decline across large cohorts, but causal evidence keeps weakening: negative primary outcome in the first RCT in people with MCI, and little Mendelian randomization signal for dementia (PMIDs 42804222, 42753934, 42949226, 42447643)
- Hearing loss belongs to a cluster of aging processes — multimorbidity, mortality, immune aging, cerebrovascular risk, weaker brain connectivity (PMIDs 42728658, 42756103, 42864571, 42961136)
- Delivery and access, not technology, is the bottleneck: unfound hospital amplifiers, patchy Medicaid coverage, unmade implant referrals, too few trained providers, undetected loss in underserved communities (PMIDs 42677253, 42172530, 42685269, 42418229, 42446280 — recurring across four runs)
- Self-report is an unreliable diagnostic but a useful alarm: 70% wrong about ear asymmetry; 47% of care recipients had measurable loss they did not recognize; felt listening effort carries information beyond the audiogram (PMIDs 42467662, 42861992, 42348455)
- Popular and marketed remedies show no hearing benefit: seafood/omega-3 (42697173), systemic stem cell infusions (42819495), hair cell regeneration still preclinical only (42868226)
- Speech-in-noise difficulty is the functionally and prognostically important measure, yet it is underused clinically (PMIDs 42735379, 42961136, 42932290, 42420817)

## Emerging threads
- Hearing loss as a two-person problem: caregiver depressive symptoms, conversation partners' speech and repair behavior measurably change (PMIDs 42861992, 42370824)
- Cognition contaminates hearing testing — reasoning and memory predict scores on speech-in-noise and ripple tests independent of thresholds, with implications for device candidacy (PMIDs 42900575, 42932290)
- Device trade-offs, not just device access: directional microphones hurt localization and quiet the wearer's own speech (PMIDs 42507806, 42370824); comfort-in-noise fitting (42760273)
- Shared vascular biology may underlie part of the hearing–brain link (PMIDs 42864571, 42961136) — watch for replication and external validation
- Uptake is driven by traits and information, not severity: boredom proneness and mind-wandering predicted not buying aids over five years (PMIDs 42244439, 42707027); health literacy mediates worse health days (42386205)
- Peripheral mechanisms and exposures: middle-ear aging (42635394); early-life farm noise/chemical exposure and Alzheimer's pathology (42851117)
