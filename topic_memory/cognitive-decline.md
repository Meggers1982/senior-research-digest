# Topic Memory: cognitive decline
_Last updated: 2026-09-05_

## Established findings
- Resistance training improves executive function, working memory, and processing speed—not episodic memory (PMID 42508500; recurred Aug & Sep 2026)
- Multidomain lifestyle interventions (capability + opportunity + motivation) most effective for behavior change in MCI/SCD (PMID 42586247; recurred Aug & Sep 2026)
- Persistent/worsening loneliness accelerates cognitive decline; intermittent confidants more protective than daily contacts (PMIDs 42480060, 42478745)
- Elevated resting heart rate (≥80 bpm) linked to faster cognitive decline (ASPREE; PMID 42329536)
- Bilingualism slows episodic memory decline; effects vary by ethnicity (PMID 42500984)

## Emerging threads
- Sleep disturbance trajectories (not single snapshots) predict Alzheimer's risk; sleep spindle-slow oscillation disruption mediates tau-cognition link (PMIDs 42542107, 42673553)
- Brain-derived p-tau217 ratio refines Alzheimer's blood testing beyond p-tau217 alone (PMID 42677463)
- Sociocultural diversity shapes SCD prevalence, risk factors, and dementia prediction—instruments need cultural validation (PMID 42683545)
- Premature/early menopause linked to Alzheimer's-related neuroimaging changes; women show earlier microvascular dysfunction (PMIDs 42656334, 42675347)
- Oral health mediates cognition-mortality link; dental care as intervention target (PMID 42550368)
