# Topic Memory: broad senior living
_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

## Established findings
- Mixed brain pathology is the norm, not the exception, in late-life dementia: Alzheimer's biology inside Lewy body dementia (42841041, Oct 2026), mineralized small vessels in AD brains (42805035), and TDP-43/hippocampal sclerosis/vascular disease dominating in centenarians (42777343, Nov 2026)
- Depression and physical decline travel together; frailty amplifies the consequences, including fewer outpatient visits but more hospital admissions (42749164, Oct 2026; 42849853, Nov 2026)
- Progressive lower-body strength training reliably improves balance; add-on neurotechnology does not beat sham (42753671, Oct and Nov 2026)
- Technology and devices in aging care are routinely adopted ahead of evidence — bed alarms (42833806), personalized TMS (42749167), AI assessment tools (42786110), wearable gait-variability metrics (42759222)
- Routine lab values are repurposed as prognostic markers with only moderate accuracy; vitamin D deficiency (not borderline-low) doubles frailty odds with a U-shaped curve (42777518, Oct 2026; 42886648, Nov 2026)
- Communication gaps, not drug choice, are the recurring weak point in older adults' care (42640817, 42785158, 42762629; extended by post-surgical self-report findings, 42855369)

## Emerging threads
- Fall risk relocating to overlooked places: forefoot deformity (42766945), gaze fixation on the next step (42753670), gait transitions rather than straight-line walking (42762698), Charles Bonnet hallucinations (42833804)
- Palliative care access as a payer- and staffing-design problem rather than a clinician-persuasion problem: Medicare Advantage incentives (42773642) and community health worker navigation (42681826)
- Brain structure explains less of cognitive performance with advancing age, pointing toward reserve, sensory health and behavior (42784930)
- Veteran-specific late-life brain health: cumulative mild TBI burden and accelerated white matter aging (42832957)
- Long-term care operations and financing: multi-year Medicaid HCBS enrollment (42834564), flawed nursing home calorie equations (42759166)
- Early ("pre-frail") social isolation carries measurable mortality risk, with sex and education differences (42762630)

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Whole lifespan" feature: how childhood circumstances shape late-life health (Aug 2026)
- Falls prevention evidence audit: bed alarms and balance gadgets fail while feet, vision, lighting and transitions go unchecked (Oct 2026)
- Mixed pathology feature: memory loss at advanced ages is rarely just Alzheimer's, and vascular risk is the actionable part (Nov 2026)
