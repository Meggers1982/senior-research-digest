## Established findings
- Risk scores and biomarkers perform at the group level and stall at the individual level: dementia risk calculators topping out at AUC 0.74-0.76 with wide variation by tool (42934475, 2026-10-27), PD CSF progression signature not clinically validated (42920057), no justified universal REM Atonia Index cutoff (42895952), expected practice gains on repeat digital cognitive tests (42896150), epigenetic clocks (42926120).
- Under-detection, not disease frequency, is the dominant care failure: dementia undiagnosed in over half of Medicare records (42923120), late-stage cancer in intellectual disability (42934045, 2026-10-27), lupus cognitive impairment found in 46% once anyone tested (42913450, 2026-10-27).
- Mood and sleep predict physical outcomes: Life's Crucial 9 beating Life's Essential 8 after hip fracture (42928905), circadian syndrome predicting new-onset pain across three national cohorts (42933335, 2026-10-27), low mood + poor recall doubling disability risk (42925897).
- Visible signs and frailty outperform disease labels as vulnerability markers: frailty not HIV for bladder symptoms (42929288), action tremor tied to mortality in three Danish cohorts (42935472, 2026-10-27).
- Plan design, court process and administrative gates act as clinical variables: MA transplant gap (42766312), MA home-visit diagnoses without treatment (42896184), 99% guardianship approval (42929333), AI in prior authorization now under study (42932180, 2026-10-27).
- Popular supplement and gut-health claims keep failing clean tests: probiotics adding nothing to iron for anemia (42933603, 2026-10-27), PD methane overgrowth explained by constipation rather than the disease (42934967, 2026-10-27).

## Emerging threads
- Deprescribing logic shifting from pill count to drug class and age-based caution weakening: anticholinergic burden beating polypharmacy for hip fracture and dementia (42931882), SGLT2 inhibitors safe and linked to fewer hospitalizations in adults 75+ (42937517) — both 2026-10-27.
- Low-tech bedside screening as a prevention lever: nurse-led swallowing screen halving aspiration pneumonia (42934351), GNRI flagging diabetic foot disease and death in dialysis (42933604) — 2026-10-27.
- Drug-free neuromodulation advancing from feasibility to efficacy: at-home remote ischemic conditioning adherence trial (42921033) to randomized Hi-tACS cognitive gains after stroke (42933772, 2026-10-27).
- Global aging evidence base widening: first comparable multimorbidity picture across The Gambia, South Africa and Zimbabwe (42933453, 2026-10-27).
- Preclinical findings arriving pre-loaded with a supplement headline (ABCA1/magnesium citrate, 42901980, 2026-10-27).
