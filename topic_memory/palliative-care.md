# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

## Established findings
- Palliative care needs extend beyond cancer; majority of hospitalized/NH older adults have unmet needs (PMID 42366281, June 2026)
- Palliative referrals for oldest-old (90+) come just days before death (PMID 42580255, August 2026)
- Families and clinicians perceive dying differently — systematic bedside communication gaps (PMID 42530751, July 2026)
- Living alone or having dementia predicts unmet social/emotional/spiritual needs at end of life (PMID 42407095, July 2026)
- ACP improves goal-concordant care but needs cultural adaptation (PMID 42085825, April 2026)
- NP-led home dementia care reduces ED visits and supports aging in place (PMID 42343547, June 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Coordination failures persist even after palliative consult — families feel care is reactive with poor discharge planning (PMID 41247831, Nov 2025; PMID 42663268, Aug 2026)
- Cachexia as distinct syndrome affecting 70%+ of advanced cancer deaths (PMID 42674857, Aug 2026)
- CHW-led task-shifting models extend palliative reach (PMID 42681826, Sept 2026)
- Telehealth as access enabler for patients who wouldn't access palliative care otherwise (PMID 42661449, Aug 2026)
- Sleep hygiene as modifiable target in palliative symptom management (PMID 42668859, Aug 2026)
