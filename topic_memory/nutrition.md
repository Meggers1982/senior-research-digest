# Topic Memory: nutrition
_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

## Established findings
- Gut-friendly diet (high DI-GM) associated with lower mortality and slower biological aging (PMID 42030815; covered Jun & Aug 2026)
- Early-life sugar restriction linked to lower frailty decades later—UK Biobank natural experiment (PMID 42214193; Jun & Aug 2026)
- Protein intake alone does not prevent frailty progression over 8 years—Norwegian cohort (PMID 41999683; Jun & Aug 2026)
- Oral-health + nutrition education reduces oral frailty and care-dependency risk—Japan CAMCAM-P (PMID 42128471; Jun & Aug 2026)
- Ergothioneine emerging as candidate "longevity vitamin"; mushrooms primary dietary source (PMID 42107722; Jun & Aug 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Social/household structure shapes long-term nutrient trajectories (spouse cohabitation → higher protein; PMID 42385655)
- Nutrition–sleep–circadian integration as unified framework for brain-health interventions (PMID 42612710)
- Periodontal disease linked to mobility, vision, hearing—not just chewing/nutrition (PMID 40971116)
- Policy gaps in integrating nutrition into aging strategies—Iran analysis, Cameroon ICOPE screening (PMIDs 42445616, 42334923)
- Resistance training superior to nutrition supplementation for muscle-specific strength (PMID 42570706)
