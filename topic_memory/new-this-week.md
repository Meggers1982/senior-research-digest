# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-21_

## Established findings
- Marketed anti-aging metrics and shortcuts keep failing scrutiny: supplements and lab ratios (42746141, 42744962, 42741023), single-score "biological age" epigenetic clocks (42755069), testosterone as the explanation for midlife strength/cognitive decline (42763064).
- Everyday function and cheap routine signals predict hard outcomes better than test scores: IADL decline (42753032), mobility in free-text notes (42760637), chewing/swallowing items (42754543), BNP and intrinsic capacity (42764591), multimorbidity and incident sarcopenia (42764080), 72-hour morning accelerometry (42754817).
- Large analyses keep undercutting assumed benefit — or assumed futility — in older patients: expanded-criteria kidneys at 75 (42759976), ascites reinfusion (42759903), and now dementia as a reason to withhold STEMI care (42754545). Age and diagnosis are poor standalone decision rules.
- Non-drug and low-tech interventions carry real weight: group programs cutting controlled pain medication and falls (42755166), group PT extending therapist reach (42760622), post-neurosurgery breathing/mobilization bundles (42754223).
- Symptoms and priorities that nobody asks about go unrecorded: "What Matters" most-skipped 4M (42760752), ED communication as top driver of bad experience (42762629), early satiety never asked in cancer care (42751765).
- Integration alone does not produce good dementia care — VA clinicians report persistent gaps in screening, training, pathways and access despite a unified record (42750559).

## Emerging threads
- Measurement instability as its own story: claims frailty indexes labeling 10–71% of the same people frail (42760259), claims algorithms missing most dementia and missing more in Medicare Advantage (42760257), eyeball frailty with 49% positive predictive value (42750643), face-valid clinic tasks predicting real-world function worse than abstract executive testing (42765144).
- Alzheimer's detection shifting to who is missed: atypical non-amnestic variants and a proposed four-axis framework (42760345), blood markers needing imaging to hold up across cohorts (42758365), digital pre-screen before blood testing (42757183).
- Whose reference data are we using — U.S. disease onset ages diverging from U.K. figures across 274 conditions (42745562).
- Restraint and sedation of older adults outside the hospital: benzodiazepine-heavy EMS chemical sedation (42754548).
- Risk stratification at intake to long-term care, with vendor involvement to disclose (42748398).
- Under-studied populations: aging with intellectual disability and Down syndrome (42753686); falling female enrollment in stroke rehab trials (42759356).
- Environment and building conditions as clinical variables: heat, cold and PM2.5 driving nursing home ED visits (42760629).
