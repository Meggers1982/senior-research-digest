## Study 2: Childhood Trauma Linked to Heart Disease and Faster Biological Aging Decades Later
**PMID:** 42208056 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Imprecise biological aging estimate** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Forced childhood migration was linked to biological aging acceleration of 1.5 to 2.7 years, with stronger effects in women."
- **Abstract says:** "Forced childhood migration was also associated with age acceleration (∆KDMAge β =1.52), with a stronger association in women (∆KDMAge β =2.67)."
- **Problem:** The digest presents 1.5 and 2.7 as a range, but the abstract reports 1.52 as the overall effect and 2.67 specifically for women—these are not a range but separate estimates for different groups.
- **Suggested fix:** "Forced childhood migration was linked to biological aging acceleration of about 1.5 years overall, with a stronger effect in women (approximately 2.7 years)."

## Study 5: Frailty Index Strongly Predicts Death in People with Early Metabolic and Kidney Problems
**PMID:** 41615808 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline and framing suggest "early" CKM but study includes all stages** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "early-stage cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome" (headline says "Early Metabolic and Kidney Problems")
- **Abstract says:** "7049 participants with complete information for CKM staging (stages 0-4)"
- **Problem:** The study includes all CKM stages (0-4), not just early stages. The digest's framing as "early-stage" is not fully accurate.
- **Suggested fix:** Change "early-stage" to "stages 0-4" or simply "cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome" throughout.

## Study 10: New Insulin Resistance Measure Helps Predict Heart Disease in Early Metabolic Syndrome
**PMID:** 42101474 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Model accuracy slightly overstated** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "achieved 93% accuracy in predicting CVD"
- **Abstract says:** "achieving an accuracy (ACC) of 0.986 and an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) of 0.932"
- **Problem:** The digest conflates AUC (0.932) with accuracy. The actual accuracy reported is 98.6%, not 93%. The 93% figure appears to be the AUC, which is a different metric.
- **Suggested fix:** "achieved 98.6% accuracy and an AUC of 0.93 in predicting CVD" or simply "demonstrated strong predictive performance (AUC 0.93)"

**Issue B. Headline says "Early Metabolic Syndrome" but study covers stages 0-3** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "early-stage cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome"
- **Abstract says:** "CKM stage 0-3"
- **Problem:** While stages 0-3 could reasonably be called "early" (excluding stage 4), this should be clarified.
- **Suggested fix:** "stage 0-3 cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic syndrome"
