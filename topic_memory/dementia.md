# Topic Memory: dementia
_Last updated: 2026-09-10_

## Established findings
- Modifiable lifestyle factors (activity, diet, sleep, cognitive engagement) cumulatively slow cognitive decline; sustained (6+ month), socially supported, skill-building programs change behavior, but the link from behavior change to protected cognition remains low-certainty (PMIDs 42566156, 42645764, 42586247 — recurring Aug/Sep 2026)
- Frailty predicts dementia across cohorts and countries; worsening frailty raises risk further, improvement may lower it, and depressive symptoms mediate roughly a third of the link (PMIDs 42616643, 42600788)
- Socioeconomic/educational disadvantage and depression modify APOE ε4 risk, especially in women (PMIDs 42538506, 42551464)
- Caregiver burden is driven by family dynamics and system fragmentation, not only patient symptoms, and does not end at placement — post-move-in distress and depressive symptoms are near-universal in small samples (PMIDs 42607070, 42606574, 42720084)
- Young-onset dementia families face higher strain and lower formal service use; existing programs are a poor age fit (PMIDs 42546122, 42594757)

## Emerging threads
- Mood/psychiatric history as a load-bearing dementia pathway: diabetes+depression multiplicative risk, bipolar cognitive profiles, mood stabilizer choice, optimism failing to buffer stress (PMIDs 42556583, 42456815, 42442665, 42567210)
- Blood biomarkers (p-tau217) entering clinic with genotype-dependent interpretation; raises risk-disclosure and advance-planning questions for cognitively healthy people (PMID 42716078)
- Behavioral crisis as a housing-decision trigger: agitation-driven psychiatric hospitalization ends home care ~30% of the time, often without medication change (PMID 42414168; follows antipsychotic awareness gaps, PMID 42640817)
- End-of-life dementia decisions: minimal comfort feeding acceptability among clinicians, structured hospice conversation tools with lopsided clinician-vs-family uptake (PMIDs 42331265, 42713768)
- Built environment and access equity: WHO age-friendly neighborhood scores and pre-dementia syndrome risk; audio-only telehealth reliance in rural/Black beneficiaries; social vulnerability and delirium predicting long hospital stays (PMIDs 42712112, 42705121, 42686639)
- Preserved procedural/motor learning in Alzheimer's and MCI, with rehab implications (PMID 42709508)
