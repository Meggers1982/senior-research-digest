# Topic Memory: fall prevention
_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

## Established findings
- Exercise + protein (≥1.2 g/kg/day) + vitamin D (≥700 IU/day) reduces sarcopenia-related falls (PMID 42208412, Jun 2026)
- Hip abductor strength and diuretic use are top modifiable predictors of injurious falls; polypharmacy counts less useful (PMID 42296796, Jun 2026)
- Abdominal obesity + muscle weakness dramatically elevates fall risk in men (PMID 42223007, Jun 2026)
- STEADI telemedicine is cost-effective for population-based fall prevention (PMID 42315165, Jun 2026)
- Parks/walking paths reduce falls; moderate terrain may be protective (PMID 42025148, Apr 2026)
- Exercise variety (not walking alone) reduces fall risk in women (PMID 40992429, Sep 2025)
- Cardiovascular assessment gaps persist in fall clinics (PMID 42230523, Jun 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Falls resilience framework: shift from risk reduction to resilience building (PMID 42508443, Jul 2026)
- Explosive strength (rate of force development) declines steeply after 90—training implications (PMID 42498271, Jul 2026)
- Perceived fatigability as novel fall risk marker (PMID 42605068, Aug 2026)
- Task-shifting models (village doctors, community health workers) effective in underserved settings (PMID 42580695, Aug 2026)
- Social media fall prevention content quality highly variable; verification matters (PMID 42531750, Jul 2026)
