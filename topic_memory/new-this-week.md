# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

## Established findings
- Exercise modality, load, timing and format barely matter for older adults; volume you will sustain does — resistance training tying with all other exercise for cognition (42623933, 2026-10-02) and aerobic/tai chi/dance all improving frailty, mood and function with no winner (42617397, 2026-10-03).
- Longevity supplements and "anti-aging" nutrition keep failing on their own claims: 27-trial senescence review with no change in core markers (42401265), 42 HSF-1 plant-compound studies all in worms with zero mammalian data (42472607), polyphenol null at muscle level (42788414).
- Age and frailty labels, not clinical need, decide who gets offered treatment — anticoagulants withheld from frail AF patients (41345290), ablation after stroke (42821842), hospice in dementia (41176320) — while pooled trial data show the same patients benefit: thrombectomy over 70 and 80 (41706582), iNPH shunting (42825924).
- Short, cheap functional measures carry real prognostic weight: grip strength over muscle mass (41369831), sarcopenia and stroke recovery (42811735), VES-13 (42628443), severity of fall-related fear (42628444).
- Services already in wide delivery rest on very thin evidence: post-delirium recovery (42612611), stroke navigation (42140823), meaning-centered palliative support (41762175), online dementia education with no retention data (40831193).
- Self-guided digital tools for caregivers perform better than the "needs a human attached" rule predicted: automated caregiver platform (41644333), companion chatbots (42623930), e-learning courses completed across the full adult age range (40831193).

## Emerging threads
- Procedures in the oldest-old are expanding while risk shifts to the home: 13-fold rise in same-day lumbar fusion, highest share at 80+ (42364622); 30% reoperation rate after subdural drainage, worst on dual antithrombotics (42424791); recovery capacity (sarcopenia) driving outcome (42811735).
- The anti-amyloid era creates a new gatekeeping layer: ARIA in 1 in 10 with APOE4 homozygotes at ~5x risk (42825842), p-tau217 best blood marker but weakest in the pre-symptomatic group where it is most wanted (42631717), CSF tau predicting early lecanemab trajectory (42819539).
- Vascular function as the first measurable step in dementia risk, with a sex difference: APOE4 carriers losing cerebrovascular reactivity while cognition tests normal (41353310).
- Mood, meaning and framing as clinical variables: persistent depression preceding frailty (42822543), pre-DBS mood burden predicting gait recovery (42823526), meaning tracking with reduced wish to hasten death (41762175), biological-cause framing raising willingness to take gout medication (41931035).
- Bone fragility may be the wrong target in diabetes — identical bone loss rates across glycemic groups over 8 years (42555304) — while drug continuity (denosumab rebound) is a sharper fracture lever (42825930).
- Non-surgical adjustment of existing implants as a mobility lever: low-frequency STN-DBS improving balance with a tremor trade-off (42240331).
