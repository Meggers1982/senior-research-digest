# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- Exercise modality and format matter far less than sustained volume: resistance training tying with other exercise for cognition (42623933), aerobic/tai chi/dance all improving frailty with no winner (42617397), and one-minute "exercise snacks" improving peak power at every age (42571752, 2026-10-04).
- Brain devices and supplements marketed to older adults routinely outrun their human evidence: rTMS/tDCS umbrella review (42398685), 42 longevity plant-compound studies all in worms (42472607), 40 Hz GENUS with no cognitive benefit across eight studies (42828668), NAC for Parkinson's resting on two open-label trials of 65 people (40988585).
- Supplements layered onto training add little beyond the training or adequate total intake: plant vs animal protein a wash (42617803), fortified beverage shifting blood amino acids with no reported strength or function outcomes (42617474).
- Age and frailty labels, not clinical need, gate treatment that works — thrombectomy over 70/80 (41706582), iNPH shunting (42825924) — with embolization-alone for meningioma (41660991) now extending the "less invasive option for frail patients" line.
- Self-guided digital tools for older adults and caregivers keep matching or beating human-supported versions: caregiver platform (41644333), e-learning courses (40831193), and a randomized memory-app trial where a clinician coach added nothing (42043435, 2026-10-04).
- Short functional and cognitive measures carry prognostic weight but are taken under flattering conditions: grip strength (41369831), sarcopenia after stroke (42811735), and clinic gait speed averaging 0.22 m/s faster than real-world walking (42296946, 2026-10-04).

## Emerging threads
- The clinic snapshot vs. real life gap: lab-vs-home gait speed (42296946), intraindividual test variability predicting dementia beyond average score (40847579), insomnia-related cognitive vulnerability appearing only under inflammatory challenge (42264275).
- Dementia detection broadening and narrowing at once: p-tau217 blood marker (42631717), glymphatic/perivascular MRI indices (42828568), performance variability (40847579) — against targeted de-escalation of genetic testing (42802826).
- Parkinson's non-motor symptoms as core disease features: pain on brain scans (42641775), neuropsychiatric symptoms clustering with worse cognition pre-dementia (41888329), exergaming helping anxiety but not cognition (42576230).
- Sleep as a systemic aging variable, not a comfort issue: insomnia plus inflammation hitting spatial mapping (42264275), U-shaped sleep duration and chronic lung disease over 9 years (42246285), weaker ASV benefit in older cardiovascular patients (42296895).
- Access and culture as determinants of older-adult care: urban hospital closures concentrating harm on elderly, uninsured and minority residents (42343058); minority caregiving framed around preserving cultural identity rather than relieving burden (41781013).
- Biology of recovery variation after stroke: sarcopenia (42811735) and BDNF Val66Met genotype (42828579) both explaining why similar strokes diverge.
