# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

## Established findings
- Biomarkers and scores routinely hit a ceiling at the individual level: dementia blood tests (42913444, 42909513), epigenetic clocks (42926120), neuromelanin MRI prediction intervals crossing zero (42911266), no justified universal REM Atonia Index cutoff (42895952, 2026-10-26), and expected practice gains on repeat digital cognitive tests (42896150, 2026-10-26).
- Social disconnection and absent advocacy are durable risk markers: loneliness+inactivity as the top late-life dementia cluster (42911078), low mood+poor recall doubling disability risk (42925897), no caregiver predicting far lower hospice use (42897085, 2026-10-26).
- Mood and psychological health predict physical outcomes, not just quality of life: Life's Crucial 9 outperforming Life's Essential 8 for hip fracture survival and walking recovery (42928905, 2026-10-26).
- Frailty outperforms disease labels as a predictor: frailty, not HIV status, tied to urinary symptoms (42929288, 2026-10-26); RAI frailty score still valid after a decade (42902074).
- Popular programs are well-liked but underdeliver on measured outcomes: dementia ECHO confidence up, knowledge patchy, steep attrition (42920885, 2026-10-26); phone coaching (42895589); mindfulness for kinesiophobia with an improving control group (42927343, 2026-10-26).
- Caregiver-facing qualitative work keeps finding interpretation, not information, as the barrier: stigma driving disclosure and care-setting timing (42923468, 2026-10-26), apraxia misread as stubbornness (42925366), hospital delirium friction in Parkinson's (42924868).

## Emerging threads
- Plan design, court process and documentation as clinical determinants: MA transplant gap (42766312), MA home-visit diagnoses without treatment (42896184), 99% guardianship approval with Medicaid/education disparities (42929333), dementia detection under 50% and varying by plan and region (42923120) — all 2026-10-26.
- Vascular health as modifiable brain-risk territory: ePWV dose-response to Alzheimer's with no safe threshold (42924501), first adherence trial of at-home remote ischemic conditioning (42921033) — 2026-10-26.
- Population gains masking left-behind subgroups: older women with diabetes in Taiwan's falling stroke rates (42927802, 2026-10-26).
- Bone and fracture risk resisting simple numbers: metabolic syndrome sex split in BMD (42927503), hip fracture risk persisting after Cushing's remission (42911249) — 2026-10-26.
- Trajectory beating snapshot in prognosis: serial weight and CRP/albumin in advanced pancreatic cancer (42918737, 2026-10-26).
- Aging with HIV still has almost no geriatric evidence base (42921758, 42900505).
