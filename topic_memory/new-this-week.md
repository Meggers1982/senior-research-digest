# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

## Established findings
- Cheap, already-collected measures carry real prognostic signal but add little to existing prediction models: CRP-TyG frailty trajectory (42805127), preoperative sodium (42802297), admission mobility tests (42806440), pre-crisis vulnerability checklist (42804380), self-rated near/distance vision predicting IADL loss (42810001, 2026-09-30).
- Where you are cared for, and what label you carry, changes what treatment you are offered: regional dementia coding (42804729), VA vs fee-for-service PSA (42804174), dementia and cancer-specific mortality (42810378, 2026-09-30), income gradient in postoperative radiation for grade 3 meningioma (42809476, 2026-09-30).
- Subjective report captures what objective instruments miss: felt loneliness vs counted isolation (42805125), self-reported arm symptoms vs volume/bioimpedance after breast cancer (42778350, 2026-09-30), imaging failing to predict post-stroke cognitive impairment (42805023).
- Cognitive impairment is partly reversible and partly modifiable: hearing aids reverting 15.3% vs 2.4% to normal cognition (42804222), sleep/mood/social participation (42802990).
- Caregiver burden is the modifiable lever in dementia and end-of-life care: burden as strongest predictor of a "good death" (42804262), pooled RCT evidence that psychoeducation cuts Zarit burden (42811273, 2026-09-30).
- Low-cost process and training changes outperform their price tag in serious illness: Surprise Question (42806888), mandated reflective writing in palliative training (42810835, 2026-09-30).

## Emerging threads
- Frailty shifting from gatekeeping criterion to dosing variable — cognitive-optimal systolic BP moving from ~120 to ~131 mmHg in prefrail adults (42808497, 2026-09-30).
- Pushback against reflexive de-escalation in older adults: DOACs still net-beneficial with cerebral microbleeds and anemia (42807144), procedure-driven mortality in unruptured small aneurysms (42809132), age not reducing radiation receipt in meningioma (42809476).
- Noninvasive neuromodulation for memory — small, short-lived, age-dependent effects: tACS meta-analysis (42809063), single-arm ultrasound (42808496), taVNS (42803727).
- Exercise benefits extending beyond muscle: miR-29/Klotho cartilage pathway (42811122), variable-priority dual-task training in Parkinson's (42809128), on top of the sarcopenia-as-energy-failure reframing (42805229).
- Supplement and early-pilot enthusiasm outrunning evidence, often with commercial author ties: pectin in frailty (42803883), beetroot juice pilot (42810600), PAM/adrenomedullin assay cohort with patent-holding authors (42811373).
- Culturally specific and geographically thin dementia care: Indigenous Great Lakes provider interviews (42809225).
- Diagnostic anchoring in older adults — late-onset NMOSD misread as stroke, with delay tied to worse disability (42810047).
