# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

## Established findings
- U-shaped sleep duration risk holds across cohorts: very short and especially 9+ hours track with mortality and weaker cognition, while a solid eight hours tracks with more activity and stronger grip; napping repeatedly shows no signal (PMIDs 42648048, 42755049, 42821756; earlier 42690762, 42628578)
- Sleep and mood are tightly coupled in later life, and sleep is often the residual symptom when depression is treated (PMIDs 42648548, 42850158, 42579898, 42857391, 42800142)
- Objectively measured presurgical sleep — efficiency, rest-activity rhythm, apnea severity — predicts postoperative delirium and its severity (PMIDs 42665464, 42600483)
- Disrupted sleep is biologically entangled with Alzheimer's pathology, with stage-specific resolution (low REM predicting amyloid trajectory) and effect modification by APOE ε4 and vascular burden; no interventional proof yet that fixing sleep changes disease course (PMIDs 42851166, 42857391, 42861684, 42780441)
- Sedative prescribing is risky and demonstrably changeable: benzodiazepines after hip fracture fell 33.7%→13.3% over 16 years while opioids stayed near 45% (PMIDs 42852585, 42727946, 42746961)
- Orexin agonists produce large wakefulness gains in narcolepsy type 1 across two compounds, but trial populations are decades younger than this audience (PMIDs 42636845, 42714024)

## Emerging threads
- Parkinson's as a sleep condition: ~43% carry a psychiatric or sleep disorder, symptoms accumulate predictably over a decade, RBD plus smell loss may mark subtypes, and commercial "hypoxic training" is marketed well ahead of evidence (PMIDs 42579898, 42839627, 42840315, 42574864)
- Non-drug insomnia options now have dosing and durability numbers: ~6 weeks of acupuncture as the plateau, acupuncture and massage equivalent in advanced cancer, 8-week MBCT effective but fading by 3 months, 40 Hz tACS positive in a small AD trial (PMIDs 42697250, 42471129, 42648548, 42858052)
- Menopause evidence matured from "nobody asks" to comparative data on which treatments also improve sleep and at what cost (PMIDs 42832800, 42837121, 42770459)
- Sleep spreading into new outcome domains: incontinence risk, daytime activity and grip, CPAP adherence and post-stroke mood, loneliness programs, household ventilation (PMIDs 42748569, 42821756, 42800142, 42850158, 42860785)
- Process note: the Oct 13 2026 run returned the same 23 studies as the prior run (minus PMID 42821755) — the 90-day window has been fully mined; next run should widen the window or journal set before assuming new signal
- Dormant, worth watching for follow-up: night-shift staff circadian health (42624217), bereavement as a sleep/cognition event (42791223), sleep patterns as dementia-subtype clues (42768715)
