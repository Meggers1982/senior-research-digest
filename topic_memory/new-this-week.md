# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

## Established findings
- Age alone is a weak basis for withholding or stopping chronic treatment: anticoagulation after 80 linked to 25% lower mortality (42946923, 2026-10-28), SGLT2 inhibitors safe in 75+ (42937517), restraint minimization safe in older ICU patients (42944623, 2026-10-28), drug class beating pill count for deprescribing (42931882). Exception: acute catastrophic events, where age 80 split outcomes sharply after brain bleed (42946922).
- Under-detection, not disease frequency, is the dominant care failure: dementia missing from Medicare records (42923120), late-stage cancer in intellectual disability (42934045), lupus cognitive impairment (42913450), apathy misread as depression or personality (42949266, 2026-10-28), late-onset muscle disease mistaken for aging (42934973).
- Biomarkers and risk scores work at group level and stall at individual level, often with industry authorship: dementia calculators AUC 0.74-0.76 (42934475), PD CSF signature (42920057), p-tau217 stable but 8% shifting category (42947225), neuromelanin MRI with no clinical threshold (42949087), CADi2 timing data (42948203).
- Popular, intuitive interventions keep failing randomized tests: probiotics plus iron (42933603), gut methane in Parkinson's as plain constipation (42934967), food aroma raising saliva but not intake (42933112).
- Low-tech, system-level fixes outperform single campaigns: nurse-led swallowing screen (42934351), GNRI nutrition scoring (42933604), stacked workflow/data/training in seven health systems raising vaccination rates against a falling national trend (42947551).
- Mood, sleep and social contact function as physical risk factors: circadian syndrome predicting new pain (42933335), bidirectional isolation-illness-depression loop over 16 years (42945538, 2026-10-28).

## Emerging threads
- Mental speed as the early signal, distinct from memory: processing speed first in small-vessel disease (42946888), response time outperforming accuracy on digital screens (42948203), sleep apnea tied to glymphatic/perivascular markers even in mild cases (42946145).
- Place of care shifting without loss of function: home-based geriatric rehabilitation matching inpatient outcomes (42945046) — watch for replication and longer follow-up.
- Parkinson's prodrome timing: initially negative alpha-synuclein seed tests converting over ~3 years in RBD (42947475).
- Acute post-surgical muscle loss as a recovery lever in older patients, still very low certainty (42946212).
- Aging-in-place policy lacking any equity-sensitive evaluation framework (42933452), following the global multimorbidity picture (42933453).
- Repurposed drug signals arriving mechanism-first: statins and bone formation after menopause (42946859), ABCA1/magnesium citrate in Parkinson's (42901980).
