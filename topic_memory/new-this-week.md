# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

## Established findings
- Heavily promoted add-ons and devices keep underperforming in controlled comparison, while the shared baseline therapy does the work: TPS vs sham (42975199), high-frequency DBS (42972941), trans-spinal stimulation added to treadmill training (42664922), diet counseling vs waitlist monitoring (42815344), tavapadon's doubled adverse events (42822261), cannabinoids (42966680).
- Exercise works beyond its target outcome; combination and dose are the active ingredients: cardio+strength for sleep (42970245), structured exercise in diabetes (42973264), exercise+nutrition for sarcopenia with exercise alone best for mobility (42980314), VR only as an add-on at adequate dose (41819338).
- Late-life dementia is usually mixed pathology, not one disease: alpha-synuclein co-pathology and faster p-tau217 rise (42970748), 27.3% Lewy body pathology across 7,309 autopsies with sex-differentiated distribution (41885166).
- Under-detection and misattribution remain the core care failure: oral care as optional extra (42971073), palliative involvement invisible in records (41980783), Parkinson's diagnosis only 57% sensitive at first visit (42759118).
- Depression, isolation and physical illness are one intertwined problem — but "lives alone" is not a proxy for isolated: shrinking social life before decline (42974667), solo dwellers more socially active except in organized groups (42974955), digital tools that move depression but not loneliness (42976720).
- Evidence is routinely built without the people who will use it: hip telerehab (42307257), sarcopenia calculators (42790129), frailty-specific vaccine data scarce (42976900), screened-in psychedelic trial samples (42975895).

## Emerging threads
- Remote delivery maturing from workaround to validated method, with caveats: 27,144-visit cognitive testing comparison showing slightly higher remote scores (42979031), rural digital mental health with a loneliness ceiling (42976720).
- Infection as a cardiovascular event in older adults, not just a respiratory one (42976901).
- Demand-side framing of aging: frailty prevalence flat while head count climbs toward 5.2 million by 2040 (42976199); healthy aging traced to childhood circumstances (42974810).
- Nutrition in later life as circumstance, habit and meaning rather than knowledge deficit (42977035).
- Psychedelic-assisted therapy entering palliative care with a six-cohort evidence base (42975895).
- Low-cost, low-risk group activities gaining legitimacy as adjunct therapy: singing in Parkinson's (42975677), multidomain MCI bundles (42966490).
- Conflict-of-interest density in aging research reviews worth flagging routinely (42976901, 42976900).
