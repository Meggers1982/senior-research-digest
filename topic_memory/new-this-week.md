_Last updated: 2026-11-02_

## Established findings
- Under-detection and misattribution is the recurring care failure across runs: swallowing problems in bvFTD unmeasured and caregiver report contradicted by imaging (41989822), incontinence absorbed into laundry routines rather than worked up (41947693), Parkinson's attention loss read as inattentiveness (42492675), FTD seizures read as progression (42664548), endometriosis after menopause (42887272).
- Appealing or heavily marketed interventions repeatedly fail clean tests: cannabinoids in Parkinson's with 4x confusion risk (42966680), erector spinae block vs physical therapy alone (42131955), vaginal radiofrequency vs sham (42403115).
- Exercise reaches beyond its target outcome, and type/duration/pairing matter: resistance+balance improving mood and cognition in Parkinson's (41144919), exercise+psychoeducation in nursing home sarcopenia (42966207), mind-body exercise in MCI past 12 weeks (42951347), domain-specific rankings across 110 trials (42696867).
- Depression and physical illness in later life are one intertwined problem, and age itself is not the driver: childhood violence predicting both depression and multimorbidity in CHARLS and ELSA (42248533), nine depression trajectories shaped by widowhood and thinning ties (42362063).
- Cheap, fast bedside measures keep outpredicting expensive ones: dual-task TUG as cognitive flag (41201919), grip + metabolic score (42956513), face-name recall (42961537).
- What you choose to measure determines what you find: frailty subtypes appear only if cognition and mood are tested (42537597), agitation scales counting outbursts not distress (42425806).
- Industry and developer authorship needs routine flagging: pharma speaker fees in epilepsy atrophy work (42638126), cocoa-manufacturer authorship (42679502), device-maker consulting in DBS work (42537546).

## Emerging threads
- Caregiver support as clinical treatment, now with design specifics: resilience program ingredients and the need for sustained follow-up (42966208), mechanisms of online self-help (41925437), spillover to care-recipient frailty (42288393).
- Where people die is driven by professional supply, not family willingness: home visits beat informal care (42966200), county palliative physician density tracks non-hospital deaths (42965848).
- Alzheimer's pipeline moving from detection to decision-making: anti-amyloid consent as an ongoing relational conversation (42969459), building on two-marker blood testing (42601653).
- Psychosocial factors outranking physiology in who actually participates: motivation and social support over lung function in COPD (42635504), stigma and caregiver burden blocking Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (41512238).
- Non-motor Parkinson's as the underserved evidence gap: GBA1 cognitive trajectories (42969188), attention under load (42492675), no head-to-head bladder data (42226719).
- Device-based options for people who cannot train: NMES for sarcopenia with unstandardized protocols (42698133).
- Deprescribing has a closing window — sedative-hypnotic success halves after 12 months (42470902).
