### Study 5: Dementia risk remains elevated in people over 90, with disparities by race and sex
**PMID:** 42551464 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Dementia incidence percentage** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "About 17% developed dementia during follow-up."
- **Abstract says:** "138 (17%) developed dementia"
- **Problem:** This is accurate. However, the digest states the comparison was "Black participants had higher risk than Asian participants" without noting this was the only significant racial comparison reported.
- **Suggested fix:** No fix needed; the comparison stated is accurate per the abstract (sHR 1.75, 1.07-2.88 for Black vs Asian).

**Issue B. APOE ε4 finding characterization** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "the ε4 variant showed a trend toward higher risk but did not reach statistical significance"
- **Abstract says:** "not significantly higher in APOE ε4 carriers than in non-carriers (sHR 1.51, 0.92-2.47)"
- **Problem:** Using "trend" is reasonable interpretation but slightly editorial; the abstract simply states it was "not significant."
- **Suggested fix:** "the ε4 variant was associated with higher point estimates but did not reach statistical significance"

### Study 10: Sleep quality and housework linked to memory in older adults
**PMID:** 42587261 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Plant protein ratio specificity** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "A moderate proportion of protein from plant sources (about 35–40%) was associated with the best memory performance."
- **Abstract says:** "plant protein-to-total protein ratio retained a significant nonlinear (inverted U-shaped) association with Recognition, with performance peaking at a moderate proportion of plant protein (approximately 0.35-0.40)"
- **Problem:** The digest correctly reports this but doesn't specify this was for Recognition specifically, not all memory measures.
- **Suggested fix:** "A moderate proportion of protein from plant sources (about 35–40%) was associated with the best memory recognition performance."

### Study 17: Digital mindfulness app helps dementia caregivers reduce stress
**PMID:** 42478935 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. App benefit timing** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Stress and mindfulness improved most during the first 3–4 weeks."
- **Abstract says:** "Stress had the greatest decrease between baseline and week 3 (-2 points on average), whereas mindfulness had the greatest increase between baseline and week 4 (+2.5 points on average)."
- **Problem:** Minor—digest correctly captures the 3-4 week window but combines the two measures.
- **Suggested fix:** No change needed; this is an acceptable summary.

**Issue B. Per 10 minutes specification** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Each 10 minutes of weekly app use was associated with higher mindfulness and lower stress"
- **Abstract says:** "For every 10 minutes of HMP-C utilization between baseline and week 1, mindfulness and stress ratings were 0.14 points higher (P<.001) and 0.14 points lower (P<.001), respectively"
- **Problem:** The abstract specifies this was for "between baseline and week 1" utilization, not ongoing weekly use throughout. The relationship persisted but with smaller effect sizes over time.
- **Suggested fix:** "Each 10 minutes of app use in the first week was associated with 0.14 points higher mindfulness and 0.14 points lower stress, and this benefit persisted throughout the trial."

### Study 26: Nutritional problems in frontotemporal dementia linked to depression, not just eating behavior changes
**PMID:** 42552670 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size discrepancy** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Turkish researchers assessed nutritional status in 68 patients with behavioral variant frontotemporal dementia"
- **Abstract says:** "94 patients with bvFTD were included... Of these, 68 patients with available MNA-SF data were included in the final analyses"
- **Problem:** The digest reports the final analytic sample (N=68) correctly but doesn't mention the initial sample of 94.
- **Suggested fix:** No change strictly needed; reporting the analytic sample is appropriate.

**Issue B. Nutritional impairment percentage** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "About 62% had nutritional impairment"
- **Abstract says:** "Nutritional impairment was identified in 61.8% of patients"
- **Problem:** Rounding 61.8% to "about 62%" is acceptable.
- **Suggested fix:** None needed.
