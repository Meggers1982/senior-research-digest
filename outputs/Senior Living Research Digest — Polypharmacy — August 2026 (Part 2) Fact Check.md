# Fact-Check Report

### Study 1: Korea's Hospital-Based Polypharmacy Management Program Cuts Readmissions and Healthcare Costs
**PMID:** 42570168 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Cost difference amount** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "incurred roughly $340 less in hospitalization costs per person"
- **Abstract says:** "the intervention group incurred significantly lower hospitalization costs (USD$6400 vs USD$6740, P = 0.005)"
- **Problem:** The difference is $340, which matches, but the digest says "roughly $340" when the abstract gives an exact figure of $340.
- **Suggested fix:** "incurred $340 less in hospitalization costs per person"

No other factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

### Study 2: Pharmacist-Led Deprescribing Triples Medication Reductions in Patients Taking Many Drugs
**PMID:** 42472621 | **Verdict:** ❌ Significant issues

**Issue A. Headline misrepresents the finding** — Severity: Major

- **As written:** "Pharmacist-Led Deprescribing Triples Medication Reductions in Patients Taking Many Drugs"
- **Abstract says:** "mean 2.5 vs. 1.7 per patient; mean difference 0.79"
- **Problem:** The headline claims deprescribing "triples" medication reductions, but 2.5 vs. 1.7 is approximately a 47% increase (or 1.47-fold), not a tripling. A tripling would require 5.1 reductions (3 × 1.7).
- **Suggested fix:** "Pharmacist-Led Deprescribing Increases Medication Reductions by Nearly 50% in Patients Taking Many Drugs"

**Issue B. Body text overstates comparison** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "intervention patients had 2.5 medications reduced or stopped on average, compared with 1.7 in usual-care controls"
- **Abstract says:** Same figures, but context matters given the misleading headline framing.
- **Problem:** While the numbers are accurate, presenting them directly after a "triples" headline reinforces the misinterpretation. The text itself is accurate but needs a headline correction.
- **Suggested fix:** Body text is accurate; headline requires correction as noted above.

### Study 3: Diuretics, Antipsychotics, and Polypharmacy Linked to Injurious Falls in Australian Older Adults
**PMID:** 42489786 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Slightly imprecise effect size for diuretics** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "diuretic use carried a nearly three-fold risk"
- **Abstract says:** "diuretic use (aSHR = 2.98, 95% CI: 1.81-4.92)"
- **Problem:** 2.98 is very close to three-fold, so "nearly three-fold" is acceptable but could be more precise.
- **Suggested fix:** "diuretic use carried a nearly three-fold higher risk (2.98-fold)"

No other factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

### Study 6: Opioid-Benzodiazepine Co-Prescription Linked to Three-Fold Higher Frailty in Older Americans
**PMID:** 42285234 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Age threshold difference** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "U.S. adults aged 50 and older"
- **Abstract says:** "adults aged ≥50 years"
- **Problem:** No error here—these are equivalent.

**Issue B. Odds ratio precision** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "2.88 times the odds of frailty"
- **Abstract says:** "odds ratio for frailty of 2.88 (95% CI 2.47-3.35)"
- **Problem:** The digest correctly states 2.88. However, the headline says "Three-Fold Higher Frailty" which is a slight overstatement of 2.88.
- **Suggested fix:** Headline could read "Nearly Three-Fold Higher Frailty" for precision, though this is a minor rounding issue.

No other factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

### Study 10: Pharmacist-Led Medication Review Uncovers Drug Problems in Older Cancer Patients Before Treatment
**PMID:** 42364399 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Mean medications rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Patients averaged 11.5 medications"
- **Abstract says:** "taking a mean 11.5 medications (SD = 4.8)"
- **Problem:** Accurate—no issue.

**Issue B. Review time description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "The average review took roughly 1.5 hours per patient" and in caveats "over 100 minutes per patient on average"
- **Abstract says:** "mean pharmacist review time was 103.1 (SD = 60.3) minutes per patient"
- **Problem:** The body says "roughly 1.5 hours" (90 minutes) while the abstract says 103.1 minutes. The caveat correctly says "over 100 minutes." The body text is slightly inaccurate.
- **Suggested fix:** "The average review took roughly 1 hour 43 minutes (about 103 minutes) per patient"

- **As written:** "median age 83"
- **Abstract says:** "median age = 83 ± 4 years"
- **Problem:** The abstract appears to report this as median ± something (possibly IQR or SD), written as "83 ± 4 years." The digest drops the variability measure, but the central estimate is correct.

- **Problem:** Accurate translation of OR 1.35 to "35% higher risk."

### Study 15: Frailty, Malnutrition Risk, and Sarcopenia Together Multiply Fall Odds
**PMID:** 42631833 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Odds ratio for two conditions** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Having any two of the three conditions more than doubled the odds of reporting a fall (adjusted odds ratio 1.94)"
- **Abstract says:** "the adjusted odds of reporting a previous-year fall were higher among those with... two conditions (OR 2.22, 95% CI 1.56-3.16)"
- **Problem:** The digest says OR 1.94 for two conditions, but the abstract reports OR 2.22 for two conditions. The OR 1.94 in the abstract refers to "participants with two or three conditions" compared to "zero or one condition."
- **Suggested fix:** "Having any two of the three conditions more than doubled the odds of reporting a fall (adjusted odds ratio 2.22)"

**Issue B. Odds ratio for three conditions** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "having all three nearly tripled the odds"
- **Abstract says:** "three conditions (OR 2.41, 95% CI 1.34-4.34)"
- **Problem:** 2.41 is not "nearly tripled" (which would suggest close to 3.0). It's more accurately described as "more than doubled."
- **Suggested fix:** "having all three more than doubled the odds (OR 2.41)"
