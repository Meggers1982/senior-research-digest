### Study 2: Having multiple age-related eye diseases dramatically increases risk of severe vision loss
**PMID:** 41249002 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Imprecise description of risk increase** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "having two conditions nearly doubled the risk compared to having one"
- **Abstract says:** "For patients with one AROD, the risk of severe VI (aHR 4.96) increased 1.93-fold with a second AROD (aHR 9.54)"
- **Problem:** The 1.93-fold increase is close to "nearly doubled" but the digest implies doubling the absolute risk rather than the hazard ratio multiplier. This is a minor imprecision in framing but doesn't fundamentally misrepresent the finding.
- **Suggested fix:** "having two conditions increased the risk about two-fold compared to having one (aHR 9.54 vs 4.96)"

**Issue B. Follow-up period rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "median follow-up ~15 years"
- **Abstract says:** "followed over 7 495 390 person-years" for 0.5 million participants, but does not state "median follow-up ~15 years"
- **Problem:** The digest states a median follow-up that cannot be verified from the abstract. The abstract gives total person-years (7,495,390) for 500,000 participants, which averages to ~15 years, but "median" is not stated.
- **Suggested fix:** "mean follow-up of approximately 15 years" or flag as derived calculation rather than stated median.

### Study 7: Photobiomodulation shows promise for slowing early dry macular degeneration
**PMID:** 42259594 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study location incomplete** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "across multiple centres in Europe and the UK"
- **Abstract says:** Author affiliations include centres in Italy, Turkey, France, and UK
- **Problem:** Turkey is not in Europe (geographically mostly in Asia). The digest's phrasing is slightly imprecise but not materially misleading for the audience.
- **Suggested fix:** "across multiple centres in Europe, Turkey, and the UK" or simply "international multicentre trial"

**Issue B. Industry honoraria disclosure** — Severity: Minor

- **As written (in Caveats):** "Some study authors reported industry honoraria."
- **Abstract says:** "Claudio Iovino, Enrico Borrelli and Sarah Farrant have received honoraria for speaking engagements from Espansione Group."
- **Problem:** The disclosure is accurate but could be more specific about the nature (speaking honoraria from a specific company). However, the caveat does flag industry ties appropriately.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; current disclosure is adequate.

### Study 8: Diabetic eye disease–related vision loss is rising globally — especially in middle-income countries
**PMID:** 42476825 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Percentage increase imprecise** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "the age-standardised prevalence rose nearly 40%"
- **Abstract says:** ASPR rose from 48.8 per 100,000 in 1990 to 67.3 per 100,000 in 2021
- **Problem:** The actual increase is (67.3-48.8)/48.8 = 37.9%, which is indeed "nearly 40%." This is acceptable rounding.
- **Suggested fix:** None required.

**Issue B. "Middle-income countries" vs SDI groups** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "The steepest increases occurred in middle-income countries"
- **Abstract says:** "High-middle SDI and middle SDI groups showed the most notable increase in period and cohort effect"
- **Problem:** The abstract discusses SDI (Socio-Demographic Index) groups, not income classifications directly. High-middle SDI roughly corresponds to upper-middle income, and middle SDI to middle income. The simplification is reasonable for the audience but slightly imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** "The steepest increases occurred in middle and high-middle SDI regions"

### Study 11: Contrast sensitivity testing may detect vision changes in macular degeneration earlier than standard eye charts
**PMID:** 42386395 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. "Half of eyes" slightly imprecise** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Half of eyes with intermediate AMD showed deficits in contrast sensitivity even when standard acuity was still normal"
- **Abstract says:** "Proportions iAMD eyes breaching reference limits: 50% for AULCSF, 36% for BCVA"
- **Problem:** The 50% refers to AULCSF (area under log CSF) breaching reference limits. The digest implies these are the eyes with "normal" standard acuity, but the abstract doesn't explicitly state that the 50% with CSF deficits had normal BCVA — these are separate statistics. The implication is reasonable but not directly stated.
- **Suggested fix:** "50% of eyes with intermediate AMD showed deficits in contrast sensitivity measures, compared with 36% breaching standard acuity limits"

**Issue B. Single-centre study** — Severity: Minor

- **As written (in Caveats):** "Single-centre study"
- **Abstract says:** Author affiliations are all from Massachusetts Eye and Ear/Harvard
- **Problem:** This appears correct based on affiliations; the abstract doesn't explicitly state single-centre but the institutional uniformity suggests it. Reasonable inference.
- **Suggested fix:** None required.
