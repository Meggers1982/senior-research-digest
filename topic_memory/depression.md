# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-09-27_

## Established findings
- Loneliness, not contact frequency, drives the path from social connection to depression; reachable services — not program counts — are what track with less loneliness and less depression (PMIDs 42667132, 42678139, 42789932; Aug–Sep 2026)
- Depression clusters with hearing loss, vision loss, dental/swallowing problems, low education and cognitive decline in national samples, with sharp Hispanic/White disparities (PMIDs 42731172, 42645764, 42437564; Jun–Sep 2026)
- Depression and physical decline move together: frailty, liver disease risk even at normal weight, and loss of function predicting distress better than pain (PMIDs 42599324, 42667890, 42713765; Jul–Oct 2026)
- Family caregivers carry independent, screenable depression risk that patient-focused services do not resolve (PMIDs 42791221, 42749467, 42728769; Sep 2026)
- Emotional suffering outranks pain in serious illness, and the most impaired patients cannot self-report it (PMIDs 42724296, 42362166, 42444638; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Generic exercise improves physical function but not mood in MCI; social, choreographed movement (dance) is the exception (PMIDs 42545663, 42709503; Aug–Sep 2026)

## Emerging threads
- The gap is at the handoff, not the screen: a third of older Americans with major depression untreated, 65+ least likely to get outpatient care, carers trained but with no referral pathway (PMIDs 42624700, 42788366; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Symptom pattern, not just severity, carries predictive information about later cognition and daily-living decline; low-energy presentations get missed (PMIDs 42799655, 42727201; Sep 2026)
- Mood costs and cardiac profiles of drugs older adults already take — dementia drugs linked to more depression diagnoses and antipsychotic use; QTc differs by antidepressant while class-level cardiac events stayed rare (PMIDs 42781277, 42778211; Sep 2026)
- Treating depression at end of life: methylphenidate and propofol both show promise on very low certainty evidence (PMIDs 42762836, 42533745; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Neurostimulation hype outpacing evidence — personalized accelerated TMS failed to beat sham (PMID 42749167, Dec 2026), against earlier case-report enthusiasm for palliative ECT (PMID 42716350)
- Mechanism threads worth watching: FGF21 sweet-craving uncoupling (PMID 42632431), fatigue-first symptom cascade in rheumatoid arthritis (PMID 42775296), clinician empathy tracking with lower depression a year later (PMID 42741899)
- Cannabis in later life — smoking and edibles tied to problem use, no mode tied to depression (PMID 42799588, Sep 2026)
- Depression precedes Parkinson's diagnosis but does not predict faster progression (PMID 42766893, Sep 2026)
