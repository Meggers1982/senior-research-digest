# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

## Established findings
- Products marketed for brain, muscle or longevity benefit routinely outrun human evidence, and the failures are now reaching phase 3: nicotinamide riboside slightly worsened early Parkinson's (42848371, 2026-10-09), hyperbaric oxygen rests on 7 studies/502 people over 50 (42849526), PRP knee injections faded and didn't improve gait (42847270); earlier: Ginkgo (42810842), fisetin in mice (42836501), CB-839 in progeroid mice (42841610).
- Measurement is the recurring weak link: DXA scanners from one manufacturer differ by up to 19% against international norms (42841935), loneliness results flipped by choice of scale (42850158), gait-quality gains invisible to step counts (42849064), unadapted activity questionnaires (41638194), amyloid gray zone (42441390), p-tau217 wrong ~1 in 12 (42821845).
- Function, not symptom intensity, carries the prognostic and clinical signal: activity-limiting back pain predicted frailty (42844230), opioid users' deficits largest in physical functioning (42853040), and guideline care for back pain centers on PT yet 78.4% got low-value care instead (42852584).
- Non-drug programs produce real but fragile gains, and the failure point varies: adherence faded sharply by 12 months in vestibular exercise (42844416) while an 8-week program with 81% attendance produced no strength gain at all (42849527) — dose and intensity, not engagement.
- Alarming drug-risk associations shrink substantially under adjustment for comorbidity; group-level averages are not personal risk (benzodiazepine-dementia 43%→15%, 42853249; white-matter/BP ecological signal, 42843461).
- Palliative care access is a delivery-system problem: uptake responds to simply being asked (42842242), and whether support is sustained depends on team staffing and who makes first contact (42849667), screening prompts (42853125), nurse-led lab-trend review (42850936) and plain-language explanation (42842360).

## Emerging threads
- Medication review as the highest-leverage conversation in later life: orexin antagonists vs Z-drugs on hip fracture (42850802), benzodiazepine-dementia reframe (42853249), opioid quality-of-life deficits (42853040), dose-by-dementia-severity for brexpiprazole (42847294), and an honest account of why deprescribing trials stall (42848531).
- Geography and logistics as hidden determinants of care: transportation/housing vulnerability steering post-joint-replacement rehab (42667652), rural and Hispanic gaps in high-value back pain care (42852584), out-of-pocket eyewear costs concentrated in ages 65-74 (42848386).
- Osteoporosis diagnostics under scrutiny — scanner-dependent T-scores (42841935) and persistently low ALP as a missed hypophosphatasia clue, industry-funded (42844428).
- Repurposing and risk stratification in Alzheimer's: EHR-nominated existing drugs plus African-ancestry genetic data (42850312), longitudinal-only APOE ε4/ε4 and female hippocampal loss (42853241), routine-bloodwork discordance index (42847329).
- Lay-delivered, low-cost psychosocial interventions with durable effects — telephone narrative therapy holding at 12 months (42850158); qigong for MCI (42847483).
- Serious neurological disease and complications mistaken for ordinary aging or recovery: very late-onset myasthenia gravis (42842206), hemichorea-ballism (42469138), post-stroke epilepsy flagged by aphasia (42848724).
