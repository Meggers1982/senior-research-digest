# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

## Established findings
- Products and compounds marketed or hyped for brain, muscle or longevity benefit routinely outrun human evidence: Ginkgo (42810842), prebiotics (42028746), cricket protein (42813244), redesigned fisetin senolytics in mice (42836501), and GLS1 inhibitor CB-839 restoring strength in progeroid mice (42841610, 2026-10-08). Preclinical results need expectation-setting before the headlines reach residents.
- Biological-age and organ-clock products predict survival at best, not cognitive health (42679383, 42782071).
- Measurement is the recurring weak link across fields: amyloid PET gray zone 11-26 Centiloids (42441390), p-tau217 wrong ~1 in 12 (42821845), and physical activity in older adults measured with unadapted questionnaires and inconsistent device thresholds (41638194, 2026-10-08).
- Caregiver outcomes and patient outcomes decouple in both directions (42834794, 42838860).
- Non-drug group and exercise programs produce real but fragile gains — benefit while done, with adherence fading sharply after the first follow-up (42838128, 42844416 2026-10-08).
- Late-life depression tracks with circumstance — functional limitation, multimorbidity, education (42839307) — and with community conditions beyond the household (42842074, 2026-10-08).
- Function, not symptom intensity, carries the prognostic signal: activity-limiting back pain predicted frailty seven years later while pain severity and frequency did not (42844230, 2026-10-08); echoes earlier clinic-test-vs-everyday-function thread.

## Emerging threads
- End-of-life decision quality as the intervention: 69% of seriously ill older adults acted when simply offered advance care planning, format mattering more than facilitator race-matching (42842242); one-in-three relief rate and 50-day median survival in refractory malignant bowel obstruction (42843988); low-dose methadone add-on resting on a single RCT (42843430). All 2026-10-08.
- Parkinson's moves from pre-symptomatic prediction (42836749) toward modifiable targets: total daily activity time beating intensity, recent activity counting most (42842855); intermittent hypoxia at the safety-check stage (42574864).
- "Lower is always better" blood pressure challenged for small-vessel brain disease at the group level, with explicit ecological-fallacy caveats (42843461); pairs with the first solid animal model of cerebral microbleeds plus TIMP2 variants (42225261).
- Serious neurological disease mistaken for ordinary aging: very late-onset myasthenia gravis with early bulbar/respiratory involvement (42842206); hemichorea-ballism as an emergency sign, subthalamic nucleus implicated in only 24.6% of cases (42469138).
- Aging with Down syndrome: language and communication decline as an understudied early Alzheimer's marker, with no population-appropriate tools (42844026).
- Generational health decline as a service-demand forecast (42837509).
