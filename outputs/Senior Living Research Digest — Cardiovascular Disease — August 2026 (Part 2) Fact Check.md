## Study 1: Stopping Statins After 75 May Be Safe When No Heart Disease Is Present, Large Trial Finds
**PMID:** 42580354 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Percentage of women slightly rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "67% women"
- **Abstract says:** "775 (66·8%) of 1160 participants were women"
- **Problem:** The digest rounds 66.8% to 67%, which is acceptable rounding but technically imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** "67% women" is acceptable, or use "approximately 67% women" for precision.

No other factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract, appropriately noting the open-label design and non-inferiority result without overstating causation.

## Study 3: Aortic Calcification Detected by Machine Learning Tied to Frailty Risk
**PMID:** 42334801 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size range calculation** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "27–58% greater odds of being physically frail"
- **Abstract says:** "ORs 1.27 95%CI 1.12-1.44 moderate; 1.58 95%CI 1.31-1.91 high"
- **Problem:** The digest reports 27–58% based on the point estimates for frailty (1.27 and 1.58), which is correct. However, the abstract also reports pre-frail odds (1.06 and 1.14). The digest focuses only on frailty, which is reasonable but could be more complete.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; the digest accurately represents the frailty findings.

**Issue B. Mean age approximation** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "mean age ~65"
- **Abstract says:** No specific mean age is provided in the abstract.
- **Problem:** The abstract does not specify the mean age. This appears to be an inference, potentially from knowledge of UK Biobank demographics.
- **Suggested fix:** Flag as "Unverifiable from abstract only" or remove the specific age reference.

## Study 5: Lipid Signatures in Midlife May Predict Future Heart Disease Decades Later
**PMID:** 42387180 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size interpretation** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "some lipids reducing risk by up to 79%"
- **Abstract says:** "HR range: 0.21-0.86"
- **Problem:** An HR of 0.21 represents a 79% risk reduction, which is correctly calculated. However, the abstract notes these are associations, and the digest's phrasing could be interpreted as suggesting a causal protective effect. The observational caveat is included, mitigating this concern.
- **Suggested fix:** Consider rewording to "associated with up to 79% lower risk" to maintain consistent associational language.

No other factual errors found. Study details (N=1,801, mean age 40, 20-year follow-up, American Indian population) are accurately reported.

- **Abstract says:** "953,793 participants"
- **Problem:** The digest rounds down slightly; "nearly one million" in the headline and "over 950,000" in the body are both acceptable approximations.

**Issue B. Meta-analysis count** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "104 meta-analyses"
- **Abstract says:** "104 meta-analyses including 2,110 studies and 953,793 participants were analyzed, covering 126 outcomes"
- **Problem:** The digest omits the number of underlying studies (2,110) and outcomes (126), but this is acceptable summarization.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required.
