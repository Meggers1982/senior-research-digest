## Topic Memory: vision loss
_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

## Established findings
- Vision loss is a modifiable dementia risk factor that clusters with cardiometabolic, mood and social risks; cumulative burden tracks with worse cognition and MRI changes including hippocampal atrophy (PMIDs 42645764, 42371784, 42716809; recurring through Oct 2026)
- Two or more age-related eye diseases compound the risk of severe visual impairment far beyond any single diagnosis (PMID 41249002 — recurring, Korean NHIS cohort)
- Women carry a higher global burden of vision-related disability, largest for treatable cataract and tied to national gender inequality (PMID 42521465 — recurring)
- Type 2 diabetes drives a rising global burden of diabetic retinopathy blindness, steepest in high-income countries where screening exists but isn't used (PMID 42476825 — recurring)
- Audiobooks require training and accessible browsing to restore reading; a device handoff alone fails (PMID 42500929 — recurring)
- Low-contrast obstacles reduce foot clearance in all older walkers, not only those with diagnosed vision loss — contrast marking belongs in fall-prevention audits (PMID 42501620 — recurring)

## Emerging threads
- Near vision impairment, the cheapest to correct, may be the stronger cognitive correlate versus distance vision (PMID 42716809) — still cross-sectional, needs longitudinal replication
- How glaucoma is treated, not just whether, may track with dementia risk (PMID 42693831) — confounding by indication unresolved
- Reducing the anti-VEGF injection burden: relapse risk after pausing (PMID 42752539), OCT-based progression prediction (PMID 42752554), implants/oral TKIs/gene therapy pipeline (PMID 41076606)
- Drug-induced vision problems in older adults: ocular surface and corneal nerve damage in ~86% of patients on HER2-targeted antibody-drug conjugates, dose-related and partly reversible (PMID 42331364) — new to Oct 2026, watch for larger cohorts
- Salvage options for "untreatable" conditions: mitomycin C for corneal neovascularization (PMID 42215273), gel injection for chronic hypotony (PMID 41521029) — both tiny single-center series
- Population-specific risk hierarchies challenge universal prevention lists; depression dominant in American Indian elders, with vision and hearing unmeasured (PMID 42635108)
- Dropped from active watch unless new data appear: contrast-sensitivity vs. acuity in AMD (42386395), AMD vs. myopic CNV proteomics (42599028), inherited retinal disease pipeline (42409034, 42269152)
