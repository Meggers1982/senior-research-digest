### Study 1: Common Blood Pressure Medication May Help Protect Muscles in Hospitalized Older Adults
**PMID:** 42414787 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. The study facts (N=206, 60% female, median age 78, Turkish hospital, cross-sectional design), statistical findings (15% vs 40% ARB use in sarcopenic vs non-sarcopenic, p=0.005), and caveats are accurately reported. The "Why it matters" section appropriately hedges with "suggests" rather than claiming causation.

### Study 2: Sarcopenia Linked to Higher Risk of Lung Disease, Including COPD and Asthma
**PMID:** 42391675 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Incorrect COPD hazard ratio** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Those with confirmed sarcopenia faced 37% higher COPD risk"
- **Abstract says:** For probable sarcopenia: "COPD (HR = 1.37; 95% CI: 1.24, 1.50)"
- **Problem:** The digest attributes the 37% figure to "confirmed sarcopenia," but the abstract reports this HR for "probable sarcopenia." The abstract does not provide separate HRs for confirmed sarcopenia for COPD.
- **Suggested fix:** "Those with probable sarcopenia faced 37% higher COPD risk, 35% higher asthma risk, and 74% higher risk of interstitial lung disease."

### Study 8: Sarcopenia Doubles Risk of Cognitive Impairment in Latin American Older Adults
**PMID:** 42155950 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Imprecise characterization of EWGSOP2 effect size** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "When using stricter sarcopenia definitions (EWGSOP2 criteria), the association was nearly threefold."
- **Abstract says:** "diagnosis criteria of sarcopenia by EWGSOP2 (OR = 2.96; 95% CI: 2.15-4.08)"
- **Problem:** The digest says "nearly threefold" but the OR is 2.96, which is very close to threefold. This is technically accurate but slightly imprecise. More importantly, the abstract also reports a separate finding for "probable cognitive impairment (OR = 2.56)" which the digest doesn't clearly distinguish.
- **Suggested fix:** No revision required; the characterization is acceptable.

### Study 14: Declining Respiratory and Muscle Function Together Signal Higher Chronic Disease Risk
**PMID:** 42275840 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Incorrect hazard ratio for asthma** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "4.3 times higher for asthma"
- **Abstract says:** "asthma (HR = 4.31, 95% CI: 2.11-8.81)"
- **Problem:** The digest says "4.3 times higher" which would imply HR=5.3. An HR of 4.31 means 3.31 times higher risk (or 331% higher), not 4.3 times higher. The correct phrasing should be "4.3-fold risk" or "more than 4 times the risk."
- **Suggested fix:** "Those with persistently low values in both measures had dramatically elevated risks: more than 4-fold higher for asthma (HR=4.31), 2.3 times higher for chronic lung disease, and nearly double for arthritis and hypertension."

**Issue B. Arthritis HR mischaracterized** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** Not explicitly stated, but the 4.3x is attributed to asthma
- **Abstract says:** "arthritis (HR = 1.95, 95% CI: 1.54-2.47)" — this is "nearly double," not mentioned separately in the digest's specific numbers
- **Problem:** The digest structure implies the numbers apply to different conditions than they actually do.

### Study 17: Thigh Muscle Loss Best Predicts Strength and Mobility Decline Over Five Years
**PMID:** 42287341 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size interpretation** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Each square centimeter of thigh muscle loss doubled the odds of strength decline and mobility impairment."
- **Abstract says:** "each decrease (per cm2) of anterior compartment of thigh muscle size was associated with a 101% decrease of HGS (95% confidence interval [CI], 1.10-3.67; P = 0.02) and an 121% longer TUG"
- **Problem:** The abstract language is confusing ("101% decrease of HGS" with OR 1.10-3.67), but the digest's interpretation of "doubled the odds" appears to align with ORs around 2.0-2.2 (101% and 121% increased odds). This is a reasonable interpretation.
