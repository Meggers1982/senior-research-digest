# Fact-Check Report

### Study 1: CPAP Machines May Protect Hearts Best in Older Adults With Significant Nighttime Oxygen Drops
**PMID:** 42403561 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. The digest correctly reports the 62% reduction (HR=0.377), the propensity score matching approach, the 4-5 year follow-up, the 350 patients (70 CPAP, 280 non-CPAP), and the key finding that benefits were concentrated in patients with high nocturnal hypoxia burden (T90 ≥1.5%) while those with mild hypoxia (T90 <1.5%) showed no significant benefit.

### Study 4: Smartphone App Shows Promise for Testing Memory Consolidation During Sleep at Home
**PMID:** 42275527 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Completion rate wording** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "While 57% completed both tests"
- **Abstract says:** "There was 57% (39/68) completion of both evening and morning delayed recall tests"
- **Problem:** The digest says 57% "completed both tests" but doesn't clarify this is 39 out of the 68 who completed at least one test, not 57% of the original 76 participants.
- **Suggested fix:** "While 57% of those who engaged with the app (39 of 68) completed both evening and morning tests"

### Study 6: Eight Common Factors Can Help Identify Depressed Older Hospital Patients
**PMID:** 42165005 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Accuracy statistic** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "predicted depressive symptoms with high accuracy (93%)"
- **Abstract says:** "The development set AUC was 0.930, and the corrected AUC after internal validation was 0.832"
- **Problem:** The 93% refers to the development set AUC, but after internal validation the corrected AUC was 0.832 (83%). Using only the higher pre-validation figure is slightly misleading.
- **Suggested fix:** "predicted depressive symptoms with high accuracy (AUC of 0.93 in development, 0.83 after internal validation)"

### Study 7: Music Therapy Reduces Depression and Supports Cognition in Older Adults Without Dementia
**PMID:** 42161569 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Age range reporting** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "average age 74-82 across groups"
- **Abstract says:** "one-to-one (Mage = 79.71), small-group (Mage = 82.00), or large-group (Mage = 79.83) conditions... passive control group (Mage = 74.56)"
- **Problem:** The control group's mean age (74.56) is notably lower than intervention groups. Presenting this as "74-82 across groups" obscures that the control group was younger on average.
- **Suggested fix:** "average ages ranging from 75 in the control group to 82 in the small-group condition"

### Study 10: Combining Antioxidant-Rich Diet with Proper Sleep Duration May Protect Cognition
**PMID:** 42241923 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study design characterization** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Researchers followed 4,988 Chinese adults aged 60 and older for 10 years"
- **Abstract says:** "4,988 eligible participants aged 60 years or older at the 10-year follow-up were included" and "cognitive impairment was assessed using the Mini-Mental State Examination at the 10-year follow-up"
- **Problem:** The cognitive assessment was done at a single time point (10-year follow-up), not tracked over 10 years. The caveats note this correctly, but the main text implies longitudinal cognitive tracking.
- **Suggested fix:** "Researchers analyzed data from 4,988 Chinese adults aged 60 and older from a 10-year cohort study, assessing cognitive function at the final follow-up"

### Study 23: Cognitive Behavioral Group Therapy Plus Lavender Aromatherapy Improves Sleep in Menopausal Women
**PMID:** 41968827 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Age description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "135 menopausal women (average age 60)"
- **Abstract says:** "mean age of 60.1 ± 5.9 years and a menopause duration of 3.1 ± 1.2 years"
- **Problem:** While the age is correct, the abstract indicates these are relatively recent post-menopausal women (3.1 years average). The digest could better characterize this population.
- **Suggested fix:** "135 post-menopausal women (average age 60, about 3 years post-menopause)"

**Issue B. Placebo group finding** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "79% of women in the lavender-plus-therapy group achieved good sleep quality... compared to no significant improvement in the other groups"
- **Abstract says:** "No significant improvements were observed in the CBT with placebo or control groups (p > 0.05)"
- **Problem:** The digest accurately notes the caveat about "placebo group showed no benefit, raising questions about therapy alone," but this is a notable finding that therapy alone (without lavender) showed no benefit.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; caveat addresses this appropriately.
