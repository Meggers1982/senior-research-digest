# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-08-05_

## Established findings
- Palliative care needs extend far beyond cancer; majority of hospitalized/nursing home older adults have unmet needs (PMID 42366281, June 2026)
- Dementia significantly undermines end-of-life planning, communication, and quality of death (PMID 41856050, June 2026)
- Living alone or having dementia predicts unmet social/emotional/spiritual needs at end of life (PMID 42407095, July 2026)
- Polypharmacy and potentially inappropriate medications persist even in late-stage palliative care (PMID 41950773, June 2026)
- Brief clinician training improves POLST documentation and end-of-life conversation comfort (PMID 41932056, June 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Families and clinicians perceive dying differently — systematic communication gaps at bedside (PMID 42530751, July 2026)
- Elective procedures continue near end of life; opportunity for earlier goals-of-care conversations (PMID 42545407, August 2026)
- Oral health is a neglected domain in palliative care settings (PMID 41404855, June 2026)
- Economic evidence for nursing home palliative care remains weak despite promising pilot data (PMID 42497095, PMID 42315168)
- Virtual reality emerging as non-pharmacological palliative intervention (PMID 42490252, July 2026)
