# Topic Memory: nutrition
_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

## Established findings
- Whole dietary patterns outperform isolated supplements across outcomes — diabetes, depression, muscle, eye and bone health (PMIDs 42679454, 42586246, 42476484, 42208816, 42570706, 42912690; Jul–Oct 2026 runs)
- Protein plus resistance exercise, not supplements alone and not protein restriction, is the defensible muscle strategy after 60 (PMIDs 42547949, 42721581, 42570706)
- Delivery, not content, is the weak link: printed guides and one-off counseling fail; sustained dietitian contact closes intake gaps (PMIDs 42660495, 42906880, 42674351)
- Malnutrition and calorie shortfall are survival- and function-relevant, not side effects — cancer admissions, stroke's first week (PMIDs 42668014, 42926059)
- Function beats imaging and beats the scale: chair stand and grip strength predicted what CT muscle size and composite metabolic indices did not (PMIDs 42782098, 42692360, 42753933, 42537115, 42731357)
- After 65, low body mass and unintended weight loss track with higher mortality; weight-reduction targets from midlife don't transfer (PMID 42753933, repeated across two runs)
- Evidence base is still dominated by cross-sectional NHANES/CHARLS analyses; causal claims remain rare

## Emerging threads
- Nutrition as a leading fall-risk marker, ahead of mobility, mood and vision in ICOPE-style screening (PMID 42869421)
- Blood-based "biological age" is informative at population level but unproven for individuals, and contradicts itself across studies (PMIDs 42727262, 42912690 vs 42753933)
- Feeding-tube decisions as a time-limited window, with patients and families frequently misaligned (PMIDs 42922280, 42791516)
- Stigma, not availability, as the barrier to food assistance — self-reliance, embarrassment, fear of being seen (PMID 42848400), layered on earlier geography/gender access findings (42776721, 42776185)
- Naming a specific dietary pattern (DASH4D) as a counterweight to vague "individualize" guidance (PMID 42900851)
- Oral health, swallowing and gut flora as one post-stroke system; vitamin D status entering the sleep conversation (PMIDs 42854972, 42696426)
- Polypharmacy rising fastest where frailty is greatest, with appetite- and fall-affecting drug classes concentrated in the frailest (PMID 42870717)
