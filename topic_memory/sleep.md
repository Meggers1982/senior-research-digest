# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

## Established findings
- U-shaped sleep duration risk holds across cohorts: very short and especially 9+ hours track with mortality and weaker cognition, while a solid eight hours tracks with more activity and stronger grip; napping repeatedly shows no signal (PMIDs 42648048, 42755049, 42821756; earlier 42690762, 42628578)
- Sleep and mood are tightly coupled in later life, and sleep is the residual symptom most likely to persist when depression is treated (PMIDs 42665164, 42648548, 42850158, 42579898, 42857391)
- Objectively measured presurgical sleep — efficiency, rest-activity rhythm, apnea severity — predicts postoperative delirium; sleep belongs in pre-surgery geriatric assessment (PMIDs 42665464, 42600483)
- Disrupted sleep is biologically entangled with Alzheimer's pathology, now at stage-specific resolution (low REM predicting amyloid trajectory) and with effect modification by APOE ε4 and vascular burden; still no interventional proof that fixing sleep changes disease course (PMIDs 42851166, 42857391, 42861684, 42780441, 42798625)
- Sedative prescribing in older adults is risky and changeable: benzodiazepine use after hip fracture fell from 33.7% to 13.3% over 16 years while opioid use stayed near 45%; cascades from dementia drugs to sleep agents remain common (PMIDs 42852585, 42727946, 42746961)
- Orexin receptor agonists produce large, consistent wakefulness gains in narcolepsy type 1 across two compounds, but trial populations are decades younger than this audience (PMIDs 42636845, 42714024)

## Emerging threads
- Parkinson's as a sleep condition: ~43% carry a psychiatric or sleep disorder, symptoms accumulate predictably over a decade, and RBD plus smell loss may mark subtypes; commercial "hypoxic training" is being marketed far ahead of evidence (PMIDs 42579898, 42839627, 42840315, 42574864)
- Non-drug insomnia options now have dosing and durability data: ~6 weeks of thrice-weekly acupuncture as the plateau; acupuncture and massage equivalent in advanced cancer; 8-week MBCT effective but fading by 3 months; 40 Hz tACS positive in a small AD trial (PMIDs 42697250, 42471129, 42648548, 42858052)
- Menopause evidence has matured from "nobody asks" to comparative data on which treatments also improve sleep and at what cost (PMIDs 42832800, 42837121, 42770459)
- Sleep spreading into new outcome domains: incontinence risk, daytime activity and grip, CPAP adherence and post-stroke mood, loneliness interventions, household ventilation (PMIDs 42748569, 42821756, 42800142, 42850158, 42860785)
- Dormant from prior runs, worth watching for follow-up: night-shift staff circadian health (42624217), bereavement as a sleep/cognition event (42791223), sleep patterns as dementia-subtype clues (42768715)
