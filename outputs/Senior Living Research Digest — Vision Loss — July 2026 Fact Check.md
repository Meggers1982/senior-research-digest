### Study 2: Light Therapy Shows Promise for Slowing Early Macular Degeneration in Year-Long Trial
**PMID:** 42259594 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Imprecise visual acuity gain/loss figures** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "gained about 1.3 letters of visual acuity on average, while the sham group lost about 2.6 letters"
- **Abstract says:** "+1.31 ± 6.7 letters" and "-2.62±7.1 letters"
- **Problem:** The digest rounds correctly but omits the large standard deviations, which are important context for understanding variability.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required, but could add "(with wide individual variation)" if space permits.

**Issue B. Conversion rate to wet AMD slightly imprecise** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Four eyes in the sham group developed the more severe 'wet' form of AMD; none in the treatment group did."
- **Abstract says:** "Four eyes in the sham group developed macular neovascularisation, compared with none in the PBM group (p=0.044)"
- **Problem:** "Macular neovascularisation" is technically the term used; "wet AMD" is an acceptable lay translation. No substantive error.
- **Suggested fix:** None required.

### Study 5: Transient Vision Loss Signals Elevated Heart Attack and Stroke Risk for Years
**PMID:** 42365525 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "over 75,500 matched patients"
- **Abstract says:** "37 750 patients were included in each cohort" (totaling 75,500)
- **Problem:** "Over 75,500" is slightly imprecise; the actual total is exactly 75,500 (37,750 × 2).
- **Suggested fix:** "75,500 matched patients (37,750 in each group)"

All other statistics (21-fold stroke risk, 5-fold MI risk at 14 days, 10-year persistence) match the abstract accurately.
