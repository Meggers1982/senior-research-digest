## Study 6: Lifetime Trauma Raises Risk of Mild Cognitive Impairment and Dementia—Resilience Helps, Especially for Men
**PMID:** 42261258 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size for dementia risk** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "18% higher dementia risk"
- **Abstract says:** "OR = 1.18, p = 0.02" for dementia risk
- **Problem:** The abstract reports an odds ratio of 1.18, which the digest converted to "18% higher risk." However, the abstract also reports MCI as OR = 1.78, which the digest correctly states as "78% higher odds." The 18% figure is technically correct but the magnitude difference between MCI (78%) and dementia (18%) is notable and accurately represented.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; this is accurate.

**Issue B. Resilience and executive function** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Psychological resilience—the ability to adapt to adversity—was linked to better executive function"
- **Abstract says:** "Psychological resilience was more strongly associated with better executive function in men than in women"
- **Problem:** The digest states resilience was "linked to better executive function" generally, but the abstract specifically notes this association was stronger in men. The digest does mention men later but could be clearer that the executive function link was particularly pronounced for men.
- **Suggested fix:** "Psychological resilience was associated with better executive function, particularly in men, and helped buffer the impact of trauma on working memory, with stronger protective effects observed in men."

Overall: Minor wording issue; core findings are accurate.

## Study 10: Amyloid and Tau Pathology Drive Future White Matter Damage—Not the Reverse
**PMID:** 42274941 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "over 1,200 older adults"
- **Abstract says:** "1,241 older adults with Aβ and 636 with tau for cross-sectional analyses"
- **Problem:** The digest uses "over 1,200" which rounds accurately for the amyloid group but doesn't capture the smaller tau sample. This is a minor simplification.
- **Suggested fix:** "over 1,200 older adults (with smaller subsamples for tau analyses)"

Overall: Minor simplification; findings accurately represented.
