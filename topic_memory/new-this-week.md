# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

## Established findings
- Devices, supplements and add-on drugs marketed for brain health routinely outrun their human evidence: 40 Hz GENUS (42828668), NAC for Parkinson's (40988585), vitamin E for cerebral blood flow with zero human studies (41964896, 2026-10-05), and donepezil combinations failing to beat donepezil alone (42816922, 2026-10-05).
- Technology for older adults improves engagement and experience more reliably than the outcome it is sold on: self-guided memory app matching clinician coaching (42043435), exergaming easing anxiety but not cognition (42576230), VR Tai Chi more enjoyable but no better taught than 2D video (42814913, 2026-10-05).
- Short clinic tests and functional scores flatter the person taking them, or miss impairment entirely: clinic vs real-world gait speed (42296946), intraindividual variability (40847579), 28.6% cognitive impairment among CVT survivors rated as having no disability (41416684, 2026-10-05).
- Low-intensity, frequently repeated movement is the achievable exercise target in later life: exercise snacks preserving peak power at every age (42571752), accelerometer self-monitoring cutting long sitting bouts in frail day-care clients (42830457, 2026-10-05).
- Exercise benefit in very old, acutely ill patients holds regardless of medication status — statin users gained at least as much with no muscle problems (42814267, 2026-10-05).

## Emerging threads
- Loneliness is not one condition: purposeful activity (volunteering, work, video calls) tracks with less loneliness while outings, clubs and religious services do not (41934642); existential loneliness is most strongly tied to depression and the only type tied to anxiety (42590895); contact with adult children, especially by phone, tracks with mood improvement (41201818).
- Family expectations rather than proximity drive late-life distress: filial discrepancy and relationship quality (42029190), alongside culturally framed caregiving (41781013).
- Care site and care quality as separate problems: hospital closures concentrating harm on older residents (42343058), hospital-at-home matching inpatient outcomes with higher satisfaction (42816724), insurance predicting only whether an HbA1c got drawn (42380557).
- Sleep as a systemic variable across the care dyad: insomnia plus inflammation (42264275), U-shaped sleep duration and lung disease (42246285), weak but real evidence for behavioral caregiver sleep programs (42472449).
- Prognosis in dementia is thinly evidenced; baseline cognition, not age or living situation, is the one defensible planning anchor (42814268).
- Autistic adults over 50 as a newly studied population — large subjective cognitive complaints with only small objective gaps (42815817).
