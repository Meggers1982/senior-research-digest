# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- Biomarkers keep hitting a ceiling at the individual level: dementia blood-test PPV only 16–24% over 12 years (42913444), p-tau217 tracks but GFAP does not (42909513), epigenetic clocks show no consistent agreement with clinical outcomes (42926120, 2026-10-25), neuromelanin MRI prediction intervals crossing zero (42911266, 2026-10-25).
- Surrogate markers move without proof that health follows: telomeres unchanged by a stress program (42918505), cytokines lowered by exercise with no cognitive endpoint (42925286, 2026-10-25).
- Popular programs and devices are well-liked but underdeliver on primary endpoints: phone coaching (42895589), dementia rehab with caregiver support (42907949), voice-assistant pilot with marginal usability (42913442).
- Facility and system process beats amenities: care home UTI diagnostics (42887518), staffing continuity and family contact in LTC mental health (42909849).
- Social disconnection plus physical decline is a durable risk package: loneliness→depression→chair-rise→osteoarthritis (42911759), loneliness+inactivity as the top late-life dementia cluster (42911078, 2026-10-25), low mood+poor recall doubling disability risk (42925897, 2026-10-25).
- Screening tools get pruned as often as added, but not always: incontinence (42904834), frailty for cognition in HIV (42900505) — versus the RAI frailty score holding up after a decade (42902074, 2026-10-25).

## Emerging threads
- "Cognitive reserve" under pressure: bilingualism fails, measured earlier-life ability shrinks the apparent effect (42926022, 2026-10-25) — bears on how communities market enrichment programming.
- Parkinson's as a multi-domain management problem rather than a movement disorder: freezing not fixable by a second DBS target (42909654), hospital delirium recognition (42924868), doubled rehab discharge after hip fracture (42911098), genetic drivers of impulse control disorders and faster decline (42908235, 42905360) — all 2026-10-25.
- Everyday function in dementia as a research object: apraxia patterns differing by diagnosis (42925366), modest walking tied to ~45% lower pneumonia mortality (42925461) — 2026-10-25.
- Older women's metabolic and chronic-disease risk from routine data: eGDR predicting multimorbidity (42926119, 2026-10-25), prior oophorectomy and diabetes (42914867).
- Aging with HIV still has almost no geriatric evidence base: 3.41 years of epigenetic age acceleration, no study of adults over 65 (42921758, 2026-10-25), frailty not predicting cognition (42900505).
- Conditions assumed to belong to the young appearing in the oldest: lupus prevalence highest over 85 (42904345, 2026-10-25).
