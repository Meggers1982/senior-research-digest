# Topic Memory: cardiovascular disease
_Last updated: 2026-10-10_

## Established findings
- Heart and mind travel together: depression independently raises cardiovascular risk in diabetes beyond glycemic control, physical-plus-mental multimorbidity clusters carry the steepest mortality, and heart disease tracks with faster cognitive decline (PMIDs 42624218, 42821655, 42475970, 42767983; Aug–Dec 2026)
- Everyday function (gait speed, intrinsic capacity, daily-task independence) predicts future cardiovascular events across very different health systems, not just the reverse (PMIDs 42697165, 42612584, 42830734; Sep–Oct 2026)
- CVD's share of health burden in the 80+ population is falling (42.3%→28.6%) while disability's share rises; demand is shifting toward function-preserving and dementia-capable care (PMID 42594758; recurring Sep–Nov 2026)
- Cardiometabolic clusters outperform single frailty markers such as grip strength for predicting stroke and vascular events (PMIDs 42501509, 42447644, 42392540; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Supplements marketed for brain and vascular protection show null randomized biomarker results (multivitamin, cocoa, vitamin D, fish oil); premium phenol-enriched olive oil no better than standard (PMIDs 42775737, 42746141, 42521082; Jul–Oct 2026)
- Peripheral arterial disease is late-diagnosed with 30–50% five-year mortality, systematically under-treated relative to coronary and cerebral disease (PMID 42772316; recurring Sep–Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- The endpoint question in geriatric cardiology: STAREE cut events ~30% in healthy over-70s with no gain in dementia- and disability-free survival, alongside an ACC statement urging routine frailty and cognition assessment (PMIDs 42670961, 42663983; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Risk stratification targeted at the "gray zone": CAC scanning adds most in borderline PREVENT risk; cumulative rather than recent lipid exposure predicts events (PMIDs 42647022, 42705507; Oct 2026)
- Climate and heat as a formal cardiac risk factor with pre-season clinical planning (PMID 42843319; Oct 2026)
- Sex- and population-specific risk history entering cardiac charts: menopause before 45 after acute coronary syndrome; wide variation in diabetic retinopathy across Hispanic/Latino background groups (PMIDs 42837120, 42854854; Oct 2026)
- Psychiatric treatment and vascular aging: long-term lithium associated with far less aortic and coronary calcification a decade later; more mood episodes with more calcification (PMID 42674302; Dec 2026)
- Oral GLP-1 therapy as a hypoglycemia-sparing alternative to insulin in older diabetes patients, offset by high GI side-effect and discontinuation rates (PMID 42815506; Sep 2026)
- Basic control still failing in rural older adults: 76% of diagnosed hypertension uncontrolled, with self-reported activity a poor proxy for who is doing well (PMID 42720604; Oct 2026)
- Circadian and bedroom-environment exposures: sleep regularity and timing linked to blood pressure; nocturia as a possible cardiac signal in men with sleep apnea (PMIDs 42526099, 42600420; Nov–Dec 2026)
