# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

## Established findings
- Palliative referrals arrive too late; the fixes are structural — nurse-triggered screening with authority to act, co-location, named accountability, formal facility-to-specialist linkage (PMIDs 42904749, 42853191, 42929149, 42860763, 42580497; Oct 2026)
- A screen with no attached referral pathway changes nothing (PMID 42860763, Oct 2026)
- Specific written instructions and documented ACP conversations track with care that matches wishes; vague paperwork does not (PMIDs 42714187, 42586324; Oct–Dec 2026)
- Preventive medications linger and are then stopped abruptly rather than tapered; deprescribing stays reactive (PMIDs 42519959, 42731737; Oct–Dec 2026)
- Misunderstanding of what palliative care is — not availability — is a core barrier, especially where language, faith, or immigration history shape the conversation (PMIDs 42842360, 42833695, 42556759)
- Age-tailored lower opioid doses gave equal pain relief in specialist units; the oldest-old and non-cancer patients remain understudied (PMIDs 42853544, 42893776)
- The end-of-life pharmacologic evidence base is thin, and prognostic tools are only moderately accurate, worst in non-cancer dying (PMIDs 42660371, 42843988, 42536632)

## Emerging threads
- Delirium and terminal agitation cluster: no agreed definition, no validated tool, terminology drives whether anyone seeks a reversible cause, and lorazepam outperformed haloperidol on caregiver-defined comfort goals (PMIDs 42873521, 42910992, 42885674, 42586325)
- Family psychological outcomes as a measurable endpoint: PTSD symptoms after witnessing death rattle and agonal breathing (42727789); determinants of home-hospice caregiver well-being (42680076); transfer out of home reframed as relief, not failure (42617989)
- Autonomy and legal risk: hospital-initiated guardianship granted 99% of the time, with worse outcomes for Medicaid and less-educated patients (42929333)
- Existential distress, not pain, predicts a wish to hasten death (42918739); loneliness in the final year (42492599)
- Frailty as a missed trigger — frailer cancer patients first meet palliative care as inpatients (42607784); opioid switching with doubling doses is the normal trajectory (42607783)
- Clinician grief and burnout as a cheap structural fix rather than a resilience problem (42634408), alongside quantified workforce shortfalls (42546987)
