### Study 6: Online Self-Help Therapy Reduces Anxiety and Depression for Dementia Caregivers
**PMID:** 42472457 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline overstates findings** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "Online Self-Help Therapy Reduces Anxiety and Depression for Dementia Caregivers"
- **Abstract says:** "While the RCT evaluated its effects on anxiety, depression, and psychological flexibility, this study examined intervention implementation (fidelity and dose), contextual factors influencing engagement, and mechanisms of impact"
- **Problem:** The headline claims the therapy "reduces" anxiety and depression, but this paper is a process evaluation, not the primary efficacy outcomes paper. The abstract explicitly states this study examined implementation, not efficacy.
- **Suggested fix:** "Online Self-Help Therapy for Dementia Caregivers: Process Evaluation Shows Feasibility and Engagement"

**Issue B. Adherence figures not in abstract** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "adherence declined over time (50% at 6 months, 40% at 12 months)"
- **Abstract says:** No specific adherence percentages provided
- **Problem:** These figures cannot be verified from the abstract alone.
- **Suggested fix:** Flag as "Unverifiable from abstract only" or remove specific percentages.

### Study 10: Eight Practical Factors Predict Depression Risk in Hospitalized Older Adults
**PMID:** 42165005 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. AUC value discrepancy** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "achieved excellent discrimination (AUC = 0.93)"
- **Abstract says:** "The development set AUC was 0.930, and the corrected AUC after internal validation was 0.832"
- **Problem:** The digest reports the development set AUC (0.93) but omits the internally validated AUC (0.832), which is the more relevant figure for assessing generalizable performance.
- **Suggested fix:** "The resulting nomogram achieved good discrimination after internal validation (corrected AUC = 0.83)."
