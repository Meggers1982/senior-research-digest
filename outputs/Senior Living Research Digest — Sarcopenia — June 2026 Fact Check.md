## Study 4: Sarcopenia and Obesity Together Triple Dementia Risk
**PMID:** 42155951 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sarcopenic obesity risk misrepresented** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "When sarcopenia and obesity occurred together ('sarcopenic obesity'), dementia risk was 69% higher than in people with neither condition—worse than having either condition alone."
- **Abstract says:** "Individuals with sarcopenic obesity (hazard ratios = 1.69, 1.41-2.03) had the highest risk of all-cause dementia than those with obesity only, sarcopenia only or neither."
- **Problem:** The digest states 69% higher risk accurately (HR 1.69), but the claim that this is "worse than having either condition alone" requires comparison. The abstract states HR 1.44 for possible sarcopenia, 1.37 for sarcopenia, and 3.06 for severe sarcopenia. Severe sarcopenia alone (HR 3.06) is actually higher risk than sarcopenic obesity (HR 1.69). The headline claiming sarcopenia and obesity "triple" dementia risk is inaccurate—that applies to severe sarcopenia alone, not the combination.
- **Suggested fix:** "When sarcopenia and obesity occurred together ('sarcopenic obesity'), dementia risk was 69% higher than in people with neither condition. People with severe sarcopenia had over three times the risk."

**Issue B. Headline accuracy** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "Sarcopenia and Obesity Together Triple Dementia Risk"
- **Abstract says:** Sarcopenic obesity HR = 1.69 (69% higher); severe sarcopenia HR = 3.06 (over three times)
- **Problem:** The headline implies the combination triples risk, but sarcopenic obesity shows 69% increase. The tripling applies to severe sarcopenia alone.
- **Suggested fix:** "Severe Sarcopenia Triples Dementia Risk; Combination with Obesity Also Harmful"

## Study 8: Combined Resistance Training and Protein Supplementation Most Effective for Sarcopenia and Falls
**PMID:** 42208412 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study count representation** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "This umbrella review synthesized 41 systematic reviews covering approximately 4,475 unique studies"
- **Abstract says:** "41 systematic reviews for assessment (37 in main synthesis at JBI ≥7; 41 in sensitivity analysis at JBI ≥4), encompassing approximately 4475 unique primary studies"
- **Problem:** The digest implies all 41 were in the main synthesis, but only 37 met the quality threshold for main analysis.
- **Suggested fix:** "This umbrella review synthesized 37 high-quality systematic reviews (41 in sensitivity analysis) covering approximately 4,475 unique studies"

## Study 9: Sarcopenia Doubles Odds of Cognitive Problems in Latin American Older Adults
**PMID:** 42155950 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size precision** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "sarcopenia was associated with 2.3 times higher odds of cognitive impairment"
- **Abstract says:** "OR = 2.30; 95% CI: 1.68-3.15"
- **Problem:** Technically, 2.30 times higher odds means a 130% increase; the OR of 2.30 means 2.30 times the odds (not 2.3 times higher). However, this is a common phrasing convention and the headline correctly says "doubles."
- **Suggested fix:** No change needed; this is acceptable phrasing convention.

No significant issues found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

## Study 14: Simple Tests and Mediterranean Diet Adherence Predict Early Mobility Problems
**PMID:** 42114049 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Model accuracy description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "A prediction model...achieved 70% accuracy"
- **Abstract says:** "neural network performing the best (AUC 0.70, 95% CI 0.63-0.77)"
- **Problem:** AUC of 0.70 is not the same as "70% accuracy." AUC measures discrimination ability, not accuracy. The abstract doesn't report overall accuracy for the final model.
- **Suggested fix:** "A prediction model...achieved an AUC of 0.70 (a measure of predictive discrimination)"

## Study 20: Decline in Both Lung Function and Muscle Mass Predicts Multiple Chronic Diseases
**PMID:** 42275840 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Risk increase percentages** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "arthritis (95% higher), asthma (331% higher), chronic lung disease (127% higher), and hypertension (56% higher)"
- **Abstract says:** "arthritis (HR = 1.95), asthma (HR = 4.31), chronic lung disease (HR = 2.27), and hypertension (HR = 1.56)"
- **Problem:** The percentage calculations are correct (HR 1.95 = 95% increase, HR 4.31 = 331% increase, etc.), but HR 4.31 for asthma is more accurately described as "more than 4 times higher risk" rather than "331% higher" for clarity with general audiences.
- **Suggested fix:** Consider rephrasing asthma as "more than four times higher" for clarity, though current phrasing is technically accurate.
