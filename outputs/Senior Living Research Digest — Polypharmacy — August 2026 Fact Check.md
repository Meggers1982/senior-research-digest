# Fact-Check Report

### Study 1: Korea's Hospital-Based Medication Review Program Cut Readmissions and Costs for Older Adults
**PMID:** 42570168 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. The sample sizes (1,135 intervention, 1,125 control), 15% lower readmission risk (aHR 0.85), cost figures ($6,400 vs $6,740), benefit-to-cost ratio (3.8), 34 hospitals, and study design are all correctly reported. Caveats appropriately note the historical control and retrospective design.

### Study 3: Diuretics, Antipsychotics, and Multiple Medications Linked to Injurious Falls in Australian Seniors
**PMID:** 42489786 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Sample size (952), follow-up period (up to 12 years, median 8.0 years), and effect sizes are correctly reported: polypharmacy (aSHR 2.62, correctly stated as "more than doubled"), diuretics (aSHR 2.98, correctly stated as "nearly tripled"), antipsychotics (aSHR 2.23, correctly stated as "more than doubled"). The PAF for diuretics (23%) is correctly cited. Caveats appropriately note observational design.

### Study 4: Specific Drug Classes—Not Just Pill Counts—Predict Recurrent Falls in Well-Functioning Seniors
**PMID:** 42584792 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Prevalence figures slightly rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "74% used at least one fall-risk-increasing drug and 71% had polypharmacy"
- **Abstract says:** "Prevalences of polypharmacy and use of any FRID were 70.5% and 73.9%, respectively"
- **Problem:** The digest rounds 73.9% to 74% and 70.5% to 71%, which is acceptable rounding but worth noting for precision.
- **Suggested fix:** No change necessary; rounding is within acceptable limits.

**Issue B. Effect size description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "antiepileptics nearly quintupled it"
- **Abstract says:** "antiepileptics... hazard ratios... of... 4.78 (1.24-18.35)"
- **Problem:** HR of 4.78 is technically closer to "nearly quintupled" but the wide confidence interval (1.24-18.35) suggests substantial uncertainty. The digest accurately reports the point estimate.
- **Suggested fix:** None required; the characterization is accurate.

### Study 5: Fall-Risk Drugs Linked to Higher Hospitalization Rates in Italian Older Adults
**PMID:** 42343007 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect sizes for specific drug classes** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Opioids and antidepressants showed the strongest associations (40% and 45% increased risk, respectively)"
- **Abstract says:** "Opioids (aOR 1.40, 95% CI 1.31-1.50) and antidepressants (aOR 1.45, 95% CI 1.36-1.54)"
- **Problem:** The digest correctly reports the effect sizes but lists them in reverse order (opioids 40%, antidepressants 45% rather than antidepressants 45%, opioids 40%). This is technically accurate but could be clearer.
- **Suggested fix:** "Antidepressants and opioids showed the strongest associations (45% and 40% increased risk, respectively)"

- **Problem:** Minor rounding (73.8% to 74%, 44.1% to 44%). Acceptable but imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** None necessary; within acceptable rounding.

**Issue B. Health technology use figures** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Use of health technology increased from 14% to 68%"
- **Abstract says:** "Use of health technology increased from 13.6% to 67.6%"
- **Problem:** Minor rounding. Acceptable.
- **Suggested fix:** None necessary.

### Study 19: Polypharmacy and Poor Nutrition Weigh Heavily on Rural Thai Seniors' Health Burden
**PMID:** 42334761 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Population description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "nearly 588,000 Thai adults aged 60 and older from rural areas"
- **Abstract says:** "587,905 older adults (aged ≥ 60 years) from 46 secondary hospitals in the 8th Health Service Region of northeastern Thailand"
- **Problem:** The digest says "rural areas" but the abstract specifies these are patients from secondary hospitals (not the entire rural population). The abstract explicitly notes the sample "should not be generalized to the entire rural older adult population."
- **Suggested fix:** "nearly 588,000 Thai adults aged 60 and older accessing outpatient services at secondary hospitals in rural northeastern Thailand"

**Issue B. DALY figures** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "contributed over 4 million DALYs"
- **Abstract says:** "contributing 4,035,612 DALYs"
- **Problem:** Accurate characterization.
- **Suggested fix:** None needed.
