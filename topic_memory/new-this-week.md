# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

## Established findings
- Administrative and self-report screening systematically misses older adults it is meant to catch: claims dementia algorithms (42760257), claims frailty indexes labeling 10-71% as frail (42760259), PASRR catching 11% of serious mental illness at nursing home admission (42771910), self-reported exercise predicting none of 16 outcomes while device steps predicted 9 (42785603).
- Everyday function and cheap routine signals beat elaborate scores: chewing/swallowing items (42754543), BNP and intrinsic capacity (42764591), 72-hour morning accelerometry (42754817), abstract executive-function speed over staged clinic tasks (42765144).
- Marketed premium options keep failing head-to-head tests: testosterone as the midlife explanation (42763064), single-score epigenetic clocks (42755069), fecal transplant for IBS (42785338), adaptive vs conventional DBS (42765432), TIVA vs inhaled anesthesia (42584898).
- A diagnosis or label is a poor reason to withhold treatment: dementia and STEMI care (42754545), frailty and cardiac rehab (42772577), late-stage Parkinson's excluded from trials (42770427).
- Low-tech, non-drug programs carry real weight, but structure and dose are the active variables: post-neurosurgery breathing bundles (42754223), exergames for MCI (42773385) and Parkinson's hand function (42773001), supervised training lowering CRP (42786581) — with FEARFALL (42773257) showing a branded curriculum tying its own sham.
- Symptoms and priorities nobody asks about go unrecorded: "What Matters" (42760752), early satiety in cancer (42751765), medication harm at palliative discharge (42785578).

## Emerging threads
- Blood biomarkers shifting from diagnosis to treatment monitoring, with mixed signals — lecanemab plasma proteomics showing some markers normalizing and others worsening (42785333); biological aging measures used as mediators rather than scores (42763827).
- Vascular and sleep events as early dementia flags: TIA doubling first-year dementia risk and staying elevated 20 years (42777184); light-vs-deep sleep tied to small-vessel brain damage (42779207).
- Place and continuity as health variables: staying in the same district or housing type doubling odds of successful aging (42786686).
- Short proxy instruments getting anchored thresholds for hospital use (AD8 crosswalk, 42786504).
- Body regions research has ignored: upper-limb loading and wrist fracture prevention in women (42771253).
- Parkinson's expectation-setting as a cluster: DBS benefit fading by year 5 (42765824), adaptive DBS no better (42765432), NAC pilot circulating as a supplement claim (42776345).
