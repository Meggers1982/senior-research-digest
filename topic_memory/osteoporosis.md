# Topic Memory: osteoporosis
_Last updated: 2026-09-17_

## Established findings
- Post-fracture care collapses at the handoff: fewer than 20% of men treated after fragility fracture, wrist/forearm breaks rarely coded as osteoporosis, and two-thirds of patients lose follow-up after discharge (PMIDs 42735505, 42448012, 42579041; Aug–Nov 2026)
- Muscle, body composition, and bone decline together; grip strength and body-measurement aging track osteoporosis and weakness (PMIDs 42167463, 42380078, 42678368; May–Sep 2026)
- Multicomponent exercise plus adequate calcium and protein is the most consistent non-drug protection, including during intentional weight loss (PMIDs 42402085, 42001538, 42714789; Apr–Sep 2026)
- Specific drug classes, not raw pill counts, drive risk: diuretics and antipsychotics for injurious falls, systemic glucocorticoids for ~70% higher fracture rate even in short courses (PMIDs 42296796, 42489786, 42722898; Jun–Sep 2026)
- Osteoporosis screening is heavily sex-skewed: DXA ordered at 0.04% of eligible men's visits vs 5.1% of women's, with no improvement over eight years (PMID 42493193, Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Treatment personalization: bone turnover markers (β-CTX) to defer zoledronic acid redosing; denosumab still effective at advanced age; prior adherence patterns predict romosozumab dropout (PMIDs 42398845, 42661953, 42362099)
- Site-specific treatment mismatch — romosozumab's large spine/hip gains not mirrored at wrist and shin (PMID 42711379)
- Diagnostic limits of DXA: spinal degeneration and metabolic factors can mask poor bone quality; rare secondary causes missed for decades (PMIDs 42492717, 42741772)
- Osteoporosis as multi-system signal: links to incident hearing loss and to vascular calcification via shared estrogen/inflammation pathways (PMIDs 42574660, 42749103)
- AI opportunistic screening now modeled as cost-saving, but value hinges on referral and medication persistence, not the algorithm (PMIDs 42732841, 42339496)
- Digital delivery of self-management: voice-assistant coaching acceptable to older women, with cost and privacy the sticking points (PMID 42442524)
- De-implementation thread: routine post-hip-fracture X-rays changed no decisions in 1,303 patients (PMID 42732263)
- Safety-perception thread: MRONJ rare at osteoporosis doses, but pre-treatment dental work timing matters (PMID 42372806)
