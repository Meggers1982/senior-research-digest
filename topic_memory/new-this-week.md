# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

## Established findings
- Function and everyday observation outperform diagnostic labels and staging systems for prognosis: PPS vs complex models (42857916), poor appetite preceding mobility loss (42861605), Braak stage uncorrelated with Parkinson's disability at death (42848968, 2026-10-13).
- Single interventions marketed for brain or pain benefit keep missing their endpoints while bundled/vascular and exercise components hold: 40 Hz light (42847333), single vs three-set resistance (42678372), midlife exercise alone (42865774), post-stroke BP/lipid drugs (42863385), photobiomodulation added to back exercise (42864195, 2026-10-13).
- Care transitions and referral timing are the sharp edge: post-discharge home visits (42859151), interhospital transfer nearly doubling in-hospital death odds (42860638), first unplanned admission in pancreatic cancer (42863588), 'I NEED HELP' referral triggers in advanced heart failure (42862549, 2026-10-13).
- Sleep fragmentation, not duration, is the aging signal: continuity and night-to-night variability tied to cognition (42436643), arousal subtypes splitting by genetic risk (42171655), WASO rising up to 32% in women by 60-64 with total sleep nearly flat (42518248, 2026-10-13).
- Biomarker cutoffs and test accuracy mislead at the individual level: %p-tau217 levels differing by race/ethnicity (42862590), EEG complexity classifier missing half of MCI cases (42865788, 2026-10-13), blood biomarker validation gaps in Black women (42860747, 2026-10-13).
- "Just aging" framing blocks effective care: Lancet OA roadmap (42860133), telomere length not causal for spine degeneration (42863602), APOE risk operating partly through modifiable methylation (42864517, 2026-10-13).
- Caregivers and their needs are structurally invisible to health systems: unrecognized hearing loss and caregiver depressive symptoms (42861992), caregiving status absent from the EHR entirely (42861868, 2026-10-13).

## Emerging threads
- Anti-amyloid therapy precision: uneven regional clearance sparing memory regions (42857419), occipital amyloid pattern plus GFAP/p-tau as possible ARIA-E predictors (42857403) — both tiny, single-center, with Lilly/Biogen disclosures.
- Benefits and geography as health exposures: SNAP Emergency Allotment termination raising food insufficiency most among older adults living alone (42647175), rural-urban variation in Medicare hospice delivery (42635982). Links to the unpitched "zip code decides where your rehab happens" idea.
- Basic aging biology arriving ahead of any application, with consumer-marketing risk: sex differences in naive T-cell loss (42855600), lipid acyl-chain lengthening tied to frailty and heart disease (42839094).
- Post-stroke and cerebrovascular cluster broadening: ICH blood-pressure benefit confined to deep bleeds (42848972), vertebrobasilar dolichoectasia progression predicting posterior stroke (42836955), depression over anxiety for cognitive decline (42863797).
- Age itself as an independent driver of treatment harm: 36% higher hospital-treated infection risk over 60 on rituximab (42854181).
