# Topic Memory: broad senior living
_Last updated: 2026-08-26_

## Established findings
- Depression and physical inactivity together amplify frailty risk (PMIDs 42372268, 42633642, 42636431)
- Inflammation markers (CRP, CAR) linked to cognitive, navigational, and surgical outcomes (PMIDs 42378443, 42634733, 42635903)
- Diverse activity engagement across physical, cognitive, and social domains protects function (PMID 42377798)
- Blood-based biological aging clocks approaching clinical utility (PMIDs 42373948, 42631826)

## Emerging threads
- Early-life adversity and socioeconomic trajectories shape late-life health; adult circumstances can modify outcomes (PMIDs 42633642, 42632042)
- Non-human and parasocial relationships (TV, pets) as emotional support in late life (PMIDs 42634112, 42629518)
- Communication gaps around medication risks in care settings (PMID 42640817)
- Frailty as risk-stratification factor, not automatic exclusion, for new therapies (PMID 42641765)
