# Fact-Check Report

### Study 1: Eating More Flavonoid-Rich Foods Linked to Lower Risk of Hearing Loss
**PMID:** 42235109 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Mediation percentage rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "C-reactive protein mediating about 5% of the association"
- **Abstract says:** "accounting for 4.8% of the total effect"
- **Problem:** The digest rounded 4.8% to "about 5%," which is acceptable but slightly imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** "C-reactive protein mediating 4.8% of the association" (or retain "about 5%" as acceptable rounding)

**Issue B. Follow-up period stated as average rather than total** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "for an average of 11 years"
- **Abstract says:** "During 613,590 person-years of follow-up"
- **Problem:** The abstract reports total person-years, not average follow-up duration. The digest's "11 years" (613,590 ÷ 55,859 ≈ 11) is a reasonable calculation but is presented as directly stated when it's inferred.
- **Suggested fix:** "over approximately 614,000 person-years of follow-up (averaging about 11 years per participant)"

### Study 4: Gamified Brain-and-Hearing Training Shows Promise for Older Adults
**PMID:** 42302249 | **Verdict:** ❌ Significant issues

**Issue A. Industry funding misattributed** — Severity: Major

- **As written:** "industry funding from Sonova AG (hearing device manufacturer)"
- **Abstract says:** "JSG reports Regeneron: 1-time honorarium. Other authors declare no conflicts of interest."
- **Problem:** The digest incorrectly states industry funding from Sonova AG. The abstract mentions only a one-time honorarium from Regeneron for one author (JSG), with no mention of Sonova AG funding.
- **Suggested fix:** Remove "industry funding from Sonova AG (hearing device manufacturer)" from Caveats. If noting conflicts, state: "One author reported a one-time honorarium from Regeneron."

**Issue B. Gender composition reversed** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** Not explicitly stated in digest body, but abstract shows majority male
- **Abstract says:** "mean age 67.65, SD 4.78 years; 45/60, 75% male"
- **Problem:** The digest states "mean age 68" (acceptable rounding from 67.65) but should note the unusual gender distribution if relevant to generalizability.
- **Suggested fix:** Consider adding to Caveats: "predominantly male sample (75%)"

### Study 5: Osteoporosis Treatment May Protect Against Hearing Loss
**PMID:** 42574660 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size for zoledronic acid slightly misstated** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "hearing loss risk was 13% lower than untreated patients"
- **Abstract says:** "HR: 0.87, 95% CI: 0.81-0.93"
- **Problem:** HR 0.87 represents a 13% lower hazard, which is correctly stated. No error.

**Issue B. Denosumab effect size misstated** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "denosumab showed similar benefits in men (9% lower risk)"
- **Abstract says:** "denosumab (HR: 0.91, 95% CI: 0.85-0.97)"
- **Problem:** HR 0.91 represents a 9% lower risk overall, not specifically in men. The abstract specifies denosumab was only statistically significant in males, but the HR reported appears to be for the overall comparison. The digest conflates the overall effect size with the sex-specific significance.
- **Suggested fix:** "denosumab users had 9% lower risk overall (HR 0.91), though the association reached statistical significance only in male patients"

### Study 7: Self-Reported Hearing Loss Tied to More Sedentary Time
**PMID:** 42363407 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Odds ratio for sedentary behavior misstated** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "81% higher odds of sitting 8+ hours daily"
- **Abstract says:** "hearing loss was associated with high sedentary behavior (OR, 1.81; 95% CI, 1.16-2.82)"
- **Problem:** OR 1.81 does not equal 81% higher odds. An OR of 1.81 represents 81% higher odds only if interpreted as (1.81-1.00) × 100 = 81%, which is the correct interpretation. This is actually accurate.

No factual errors found upon re-review. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

**Verdict revised:** ✅ Accurate
