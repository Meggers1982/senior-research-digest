## Study 2: Living with a Spouse Linked to Better Protein Intake Trajectories in Older Adults
**PMID:** 42385655 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Slight odds ratio discrepancy** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Those living with a spouse had nearly twice the odds of following a high protein intake trajectory compared to those living alone."
- **Abstract says:** "spousal co-residence was associated with 1.95 (Coefficient = 0.67, 95% CI: 0.24-1.12) times the relative odds"
- **Problem:** 1.95 is accurately described as "nearly twice," but the digest says "nearly twice the odds" which could be interpreted as an approximation. This is acceptable.

**Issue B. Multigenerational household odds** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Multigenerational households also showed benefit, with 62% higher odds of high protein intake."
- **Abstract says:** "multigenerational households were associated with 1.62 (Coefficient = 0.48, 95% CI: 0.07-0.93) times the relative odds"
- **Problem:** 1.62 times the odds equals 62% higher odds—this is correctly stated.

No significant issues found. Framing appropriately identifies this as observational.

## Study 5: Oral Health Program Reduces Frailty Risk in Community-Dwelling Older Adults
**PMID:** 42128471 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "enrolled 518 community-dwelling adults"
- **Abstract says:** "Of 651 enrolled participants, 518...with complete paired data and were included"
- **Problem:** The digest describes 518 as enrolled, when actually 651 were enrolled and 518 had complete data for analysis. Minor distinction but technically imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** "enrolled 651 community-dwelling adults, of whom 518 (median age 72, 75% women) with complete data were analyzed"

**Issue B. Age eligibility** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "community-dwelling adults (median age 72, 75% women)"
- **Abstract says:** "Community-dwelling adults aged ≥50 years were recruited"
- **Problem:** The digest doesn't note this was adults 50+, not specifically older adults, though median age was 72. This is a minor omission.

The single-arm study design limitation is appropriately noted in caveats.

## Study 7: Gut-Friendly Diet Linked to Lower Mortality, Partially Through Slower Biological Aging
**PMID:** 42030815 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Follow-up period description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "median 12+ years of follow-up"
- **Abstract says:** "mortality data linked through 2019" (study period 2007-2018)
- **Problem:** The abstract does not specify median follow-up of "12+ years." This appears to be inferred or unverifiable from abstract alone.
- **Suggested fix:** Remove specific follow-up duration or flag as inferred: "with mortality data linked through 2019"

**Issue B. Effect size precision** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "9% lower premature death and 10% lower all-cause mortality per point increase"
- **Abstract says:** "each DI-GM point rise correlated with 9% fewer premature deaths and 10% lower total mortality"
- **Problem:** Accurately stated.

Overall findings are correctly represented.

## Study 8: Periodontal Disease Linked to Reduced Physical and Sensory Function in Older Adults
**PMID:** 40971116 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Odds ratio for locomotion** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "more than doubled odds of reduced locomotion ability"
- **Abstract says:** "Severe PD was associated with reduced locomotion [odds ratio(OR):2.30, 95% confidence interval(CI):1.27-4.16)"
- **Problem:** OR of 2.30 is accurately described as "more than doubled."

**Issue B. Vision odds ratio** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "137% higher odds of vision problems"
- **Abstract says:** "sensory function (vision) (OR:2.37, 95%CI:1.24-4.56)"
- **Problem:** OR of 2.37 equals 137% higher odds—correctly calculated.

**Issue C. Psychological symptoms framing** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "79% higher odds of depressive symptoms"
- **Abstract says:** "impaired psychological well-being (depressive symptoms) (OR:1.79, 95%CI:1.01-3.16)"
- **Problem:** OR of 1.79 equals 79% higher odds—correctly stated.

No significant issues. Framing appropriately acknowledges cross-sectional design limitations.

## Study 14: High Frailty Screening Rates Found in Older Cameroonians Using WHO Tool
**PMID:** 42334923 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline framing** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "High Frailty Screening Rates Found in Older Cameroonians"
- **Abstract says:** Study measured "intrinsic capacity impairment" not frailty per se
- **Problem:** The headline uses "frailty" while the study specifically assessed intrinsic capacity (IC) impairment using ICOPE Step 1. While related concepts, they are distinct. The body text correctly describes "intrinsic capacity impairment."
- **Suggested fix:** "High Intrinsic Capacity Impairment Rates Found in Older Cameroonians Using WHO Tool"

Sample size (597), prevalence (96.8% with at least one impaired domain), and most affected domains (locomotion 81.4%, psychological well-being 73.4%, vision 60%) are accurately reported.
