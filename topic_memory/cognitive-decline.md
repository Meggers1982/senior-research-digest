# Topic Memory: cognitive decline
_Last updated: 2026-07-11_

## Established findings
- Elevated resting heart rate (≥80 bpm) associated with faster cognitive decline (ASPREE; PMID 42329536, June 2026)
- Moderate-to-vigorous exercise (≥150 min/week) may buffer amyloid-related decline (PMID 42185590, May 2026)
- Frailty triples cognitive decline odds and quadruples mortality (Brazilian cohort; PMID 42268499, June 2026)
- Hearing loss is a modifiable dementia risk factor; screening and hearing aids recommended (PMID 42252380, June 2026)
- Antioxidant-rich diet combined with 7–8 h sleep linked to lower cognitive impairment risk (PMID 42241923, June 2026)
- Pulse pressure predicts cognitive decline only in those with elevated blood pressure (PMID 42178211, July 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Multimodal/combinatory interventions (exercise + cognitive games, decision-support-coordinated care) showing real-world traction (PMIDs 42398018, 42393905, 42287562)
- Wearable step-count data feasible for monitoring cognitive status in dementia spectrum (PMID 42340575)
- Depressive symptom trajectories in pre-dementia adults vary; social isolation, pain, vision impairment are modifiable predictors (PMID 42312838)
- Lifestyle-messaging framing may inadvertently stigmatize people who develop dementia (PMID 42223952)
