# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## Established findings
- Chronological age is a poor decision variable; frailty, baseline function and disease biology do the predictive work: pre-chemo frailty (42987730), breast cancer in the 80s (42993545), lumbar microdiscectomy at 70+ (42993959), glioblastoma surgery (42975712).
- Marketed aging products and devices underperform their claims: rapamycin with no survival benefit (42989006), untested "hallmarks of aging" biomarkers (42980902), smart home dementia tech with zero caregiver-burden effect (42992512), robots that eased loneliness but cut daily steps (42987450).
- Nutrition and metabolic control are the durable modifiable layer: GNRI and cognitive decline (42992391), diabetes/sleep/diet explaining ~19% of MCI (42991179), insulin resistance and muscle loss (42980902), seasonal intake drops in care homes (42993431), magnesium and depressive symptoms (42982236).
- Scores, checklists and algorithms fail at the individual level: intrinsic capacity as a population-only marker (42986748), sleep apnea questionnaires near chance after 65 (42993419), deep-learning MCI progression prediction not clinic-ready (42727648).
- Under-detection and misattribution remain the core care failure: dementia hiding behind treatment-resistant depression (42995052), iNPH patterns on Alzheimer's scans (42988576), earlier iNPH and Parkinson's detection gaps (41604771, 42759118).
- Decline is not uniformly one-way: frailty improvement within a year (42986749), post-LVAD recovery (42981223), preserved motor planning in healthy aging (42983147).
- Co-residence does not deliver connection; money, a confidant and removed barriers do (42988767, 42974821, 42974910).

## Emerging threads
- The care setting itself is a treatment variable: five-fold higher Parkinson's device-therapy discontinuation in institutional care (42993214), against home-based care proving less burdensome than feared (42986685, 42691806).
- Automation performs on measurement tasks with a ground truth (42988576) but not on prediction of uncertain futures (42727648) — worth tracking as the split hardens.
- Procedures with possible downstream cognitive benefits via restored mobility, hypothesis-generating only (42982275).
- Open, owner-reported citizen-science cohorts at scale as an aging-research model (42980899).
- Screening programs for the oldest old promoted but unproven (42691802); POCUS evidence still confined to acute hospitals (42987892).
- Conflict-of-interest density worth flagging routinely (42993214, 42988576, 42989006, 42987892).
