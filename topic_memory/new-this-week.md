# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-05_

## Established findings
- Heavily marketed add-ons, devices and supplements keep underperforming in controlled comparison while the shared baseline therapy does the work: trans-spinal stimulation plus treadmill (42664922), diet counseling vs monitoring (42815344), tavapadon's doubled adverse events (42822261), polyphenol supplements with five usable trials (42983146), cola and bone health at very low GRADE certainty (42980778).
- Exercise is the reliable active ingredient; combination and dose matter: exercise+nutrition for sarcopenia (42980314), the one polyphenol trial showing muscle-mass gain included exercise (42983146).
- Non-drug therapies hold up in Parkinson's where drugs are limited by side effects: singing (42975677), CBT for anxiety and depression (42983118).
- Evidence is routinely built without the people who will rely on it: scarce frailty-specific vaccine data (42976900), screened psychedelic samples (42975895), healthy ASPREE enrollees (42982348), younger LVAD recipients (42981223), unreported ages in a TBI trial (42532137).
- Under-detection and misattribution remain the core care failure: Parkinson's only 57% sensitive at first visit (42759118), iNPH mistaken for ordinary aging (41604771), mistreatment unscreened despite high-certainty links to hospitalization, disability and death (42981484).
- Late-life dementia is usually mixed pathology, and patients' own cognitive concerns span far more than memory (41885166, 42973460).

## Emerging threads
- Risk scores and categories are missing people: 29% of "robust" adults fell within a year (42982004), sensory/nutrition/mobility impairment predicted fracture beyond DXA (42980776), BMI a poor gatekeeper for cardiac devices (42981223).
- Frailty as reversible rather than fixed: 75.9% improved a class within six months post-LVAD with sharply lower mortality (42981223), against the reduced-reserve framing (42976900, 42976199).
- Computational methods scaling across existing records: LLM taxonomy of clinical notes (42973460), NLP across 1.9 million vaccinations (42981061), questionnaire-reading AI for Parkinson's (42983008), smartphone gait in iNPH (41604771) — all still cohort-validated, none clinic-ready.
- System and administrative variables as clinical outcomes: admitting service tied to one-year mortality after hip fracture (42981487); conservative kidney care matching dialysis on quality-adjusted survival (42982076).
- Deprescribing fears being audited and largely not confirmed (42985873).
- Self-report as a usable signal: subjective age predicting sleep four years later (42986003).
- Conflict-of-interest density worth flagging routinely (42980777 manufacturer-funded, 42981223 Abbott co-authors, 42543049 Takeda-funded).
