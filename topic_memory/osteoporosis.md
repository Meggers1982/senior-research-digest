# Topic Memory: osteoporosis
_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Established findings
- Multicomponent exercise (strength + balance + aerobic) outperforms single-modality loading for balance/mobility in women with low BMD (PMID 42402085, July 2026)
- Low handgrip strength predicts incident osteoporosis over 12+ years (PMID 42167463, May 2026)
- Sarcopenia and osteoporosis share genetic/biological pathways and bidirectionally increase each other's risk (PMID 42380078, July 2026)
- Hip abductor strength and diuretic use are key modifiable fall-injury predictors (PMIDs 42296796, 42489786, June–July 2026)
- Post-fracture patients report inadequate bone-health education, rehab access, and fear-of-falling support (PMID 42358089, July 2026)
- Long-term cancer survivors have ~2× osteoporosis risk and elevated functional limitations (PMID 42349049, June 2026)
- Secondary fracture-prevention incentives reduce re-fracture risk ~15%, but two-thirds of patients lack post-discharge continuity (PMID 42579041, Aug 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Sit-to-stand power may outperform grip strength as a bone-health screening proxy (PMID 42392462, July 2026)
- Gut microbiome dysbiosis in fragility-fracture patients; microbiome-targeted interventions under exploration (PMID 42269784, June 2026)
- AI-based osteoporosis screening shows promise but lacks standardization/validation (PMID 42339496, June 2026)
- Diabetes medication class may influence bone outcomes; clinical confirmation needed (PMID 42025820, April 2026)
- DEHP plasticizer exposure associated with higher osteoporosis odds; mechanistic studies ongoing (PMID 41990860, April 2026)
