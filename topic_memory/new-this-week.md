# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Established findings
- Medication burden in later life is the recurring practical theme: named prescribing cascades in 2.3M older Ontarians (PMID 42727946, Sep 2026), pharmacist-led deprescribing halving inappropriate meds in older cancer patients (PMID 42731364), and a workable benzodiazepine taper route via orexin antagonists with best results in those 65+ (PMID 42717137).
- Exercise and physical function evidence keeps converging on modest, achievable doses: 400-600 MET-min/week peak for bone density (PMID 42716561); dual-task training top-ranked among non-drug Alzheimer's interventions (PMID 42731086).
- Hip fracture recovery is incomplete long-term (~25% excess ADL difficulty at 6 years), with the 70s hardest hit and 85+ continuing to decline (PMID 42721595).
- Palliative care reaches people too late in usual pathways (median 23 days from referral to death, PMID 42713708), while dedicated longitudinal team clinics markedly raise hospice enrollment (69%, PMID 42727035).

## Emerging threads
- Sleep as a cross-system lever: tau disrupting slow-wave travel and overnight memory (PMID 42728376), sleep regularity tied to eye disease (PMID 42017838), differential sleep decline in Parkinson's (PMID 42726411). Watch for intervention trials.
- Representation gaps in Alzheimer's blood biomarkers (p-tau217 validated on 77% White, mostly research cohorts, PMID 42722299) as these tests enter primary care.
- Simple, chart-available risk flags competing with expensive ones: parental longevity vs. genetic scores (PMID 42726198), estimated basal metabolic rate predicting disability (PMID 42715815), frailty screening in oncology (PMID 42731927).
- Disability trajectory work (PMID 42720597) challenging the "long slow decline" assumption — worth watching for replication outside Sweden.
- Caregiver-support design mismatch for men (PMID 42716444); staff coaching improves satisfaction but not care confidence (PMID 42568190).
