# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-10_

## Established findings
- Products marketed for brain, muscle or longevity benefit keep failing their own primary endpoints: 40 Hz light therapy moved no EEG measure (42847333, 2026-10-10), nicotinamide riboside slightly worsened early Parkinson's (42848371), hyperbaric oxygen rests on 7 studies/502 people (42849526), PRP knee injections faded without gait gains (42847270).
- Measurement and standardization remain the weak link: brain MRI markers too heterogeneous to pool or use prognostically (42848092), DXA scanners differing up to 19% (42841935), loneliness results flipping by scale (42850158), gait-quality gains invisible to step counts (42849064). Counterweight this run: first validated US intrinsic-capacity reference curves (42853646).
- Function and functional reserve, not diagnosis counts or symptom scores, carry the prognostic signal: frailty plus sarcopenia tripled risk of no recovery after cardiac surgery (42854514), intrinsic capacity tracked disability (42853646), activity-limiting back pain predicted frailty (42844230), opioid deficits largest in physical functioning (42853040).
- Group-level averages are not personal risk, and trait-level results can reverse within persons: conscientiousness trait vs state (42663588), benzodiazepine-dementia 43%→15% after adjustment (42853249), APOE effects only visible longitudinally (42853241).
- Geography, logistics and documentation decide who gets care: memory concerns under-documented in deprived neighborhoods (42842937), rehab setting driven by transportation/housing (42667652), rural and Hispanic gaps in back pain care (42852584), out-of-pocket eyewear costs (42848386).
- Palliative and end-of-life support is a delivery-system problem — uptake depends on being asked, staffing model and plain-language framing (42849667, 42853125, 42850936, 42842360, 42842242); advance directives without conversation are the weaker half (42647180).
- Low-cost, lay-deliverable life-story/reminiscence work produces durable or dual benefit: telephone narrative therapy at 12 months (42850158), digital collage life review improving patient self-efficacy and caregiver burden (42853972).

## Emerging threads
- Beliefs about aging as a modifiable clinical variable: negative views predicted under-recovery in rehab while positive views did nothing (42625386); positive self-perceptions predicted pairing documents with conversation but did not reach non-planners (42647180).
- Risk-stratification biomarkers proliferating ahead of clinical use: perivascular spaces predicting amyloid-related bleeding (42855813), CRP and epigenetic inflammation (42841728), GCase in Parkinson's (42843141), post-stroke BDNF with Val66Met null (42852574), flat cortisol slope plus chronic worry (42671305), aging discordance index (42847329).
- Environment and brain health, source-specific: traffic, industrial and household PM2.5 each raising dementia risk, amplified by genetic and lifestyle risk (42853264); heat stress under-reported by the oldest adults (42841726).
- Caregiving demography: unpaid caregiving onset peaks at 56, women twice as likely, sharply different timing for US-born vs foreign-born Hispanic adults (42610683).
- Under-researched populations and documentation gaps: elder abuse among LGB veterans recorded without context (42844572).
- Medication review as the highest-leverage later-life conversation: fracture risk by sleep-drug class (42850802), dose-by-dementia-severity (42847294), why deprescribing trials stall (42848531).
- Cost and necessity of surgical add-ons: lumbar fusion without rhBMP-2 at 91.9% fusion over five years (42854246).
