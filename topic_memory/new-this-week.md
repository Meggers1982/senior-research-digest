# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

## Established findings
- Supplements, lab shortcuts and marketed anti-aging metrics keep failing randomized or adjusted tests: vitamin D/omega-3 on AD biomarkers (42746141), NAD+/frailty attenuated by hematocrit (42744962), AST/ALT adding nothing to dementia prediction (42741023); branched-chain amino acids and mood remain inconclusive (42740686, Sep 2026).
- Large, well-powered analyses keep undercutting assumed benefit in older patients: carotid revascularization and cognition (42747834), 3-week prehabilitation (42743945), expanded-criteria kidneys in 75-year-olds (42759976), ascites reinfusion and survival (42759903).
- Everyday function and mobility predict hard outcomes better than test scores: IADL decline and mortality (42753032), documented mobility impairment and ED/hospital use (42760637), cooking/medication difficulty and dementia (42720743).
- Medication burden is a durable practical lever: deprescribing and prescribing cascades (42727946, 42731364, 42717137), self-purchased supplements in reconciliation (42742071), non-drug group programs cutting controlled pain medication and, through it, falls (42755166).
- Behavior, motivation and mood changes are informative early or prognostic signals: apathy and MCI conversion (42749536), p-tau181 and behavioral impairment (42745934), depression mediating functional-decline mortality (42753032).
- Digital delivery works modestly and specifically, usually with industry ties to disclose: cognitive apps (42754558), online caregiver support (42745561), prism-plus-tablet stroke rehab (42759357), live online group exercise improving mood but not memory (42754613).
- Social, structural and now environmental conditions behave like clinical variables: loneliness tied to income/tenure/internet access (42742397), neighborhood and dementia risk (42730922), heat, cold and PM2.5 driving nursing home ED visits (42760629).

## Emerging threads
- Care-delivery research inside hospitals: "What Matters" is the 4M most often skipped (42760752), communication beats wait time as the driver of bad ED experiences (42762629), group PT extends therapist reach without new staffing (42760622).
- Triage and prediction tools being graded honestly by their own authors: digital screen before Alzheimer's blood testing (42757183), NCAS 100-day survival score judged unfit to guide treatment alone (42759253).
- Parkinson's personalization on both ends — pre-surgical imaging predicting DBS response (42753347), 7% diagnostic yield from genetic testing with GBA1 dominant (42762359), DaTscan revising a third of uncertain diagnoses (42753528).
- Evidence-base equity gaps: falling female enrollment in stroke rehab trials with flat methodological quality (42759356), RSV vaccine data missing for immunosuppressed patients (42755043).
- Signals hidden in routine data: atypical questionnaire response patterns predicting dementia (42760255), mobility buried in free-text notes (42760637).
- Age itself is a poor decision rule — thrombolysis beyond 24 hours in over-70s (42749350) versus marginal kidneys in the same age band (42759976).
