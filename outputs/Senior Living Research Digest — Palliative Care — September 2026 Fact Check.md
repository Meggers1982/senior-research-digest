## Study 7: Advance Care Planning Works—But Needs Tailoring for Diverse Communities
**PMID:** 42085825 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Missing terminology precision** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Researchers synthesized 61 systematic reviews on advance care planning (ACP) interventions"
- **Abstract says:** "Of the deduplicated 7513 records, 61 reviews met the inclusion criteria"
- **Problem:** The abstract describes this as an "umbrella review" that synthesized systematic reviews. The digest correctly identifies this in the caveats but could be clearer in the main text that these were reviews of reviews, not primary studies. However, the digest does state "synthesized 61 systematic reviews" which is accurate.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; the description is accurate.

**Issue B. Slight imprecision on minority barriers** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "minority communities and those with stigmatized illnesses face additional barriers to ACP completion"
- **Abstract says:** "efforts to address stigmatized conversations, especially in minority communities and severe illnesses"
- **Problem:** The abstract mentions "severe illnesses" not "stigmatized illnesses." The stigmatization refers to the conversations, not the illnesses themselves.
- **Suggested fix:** "minority communities and those facing stigmatized conversations around severe illnesses face additional barriers to ACP completion"

## Study 9: Family Caregivers Value Palliative Care but Want Earlier, Better-Coordinated Support for Hospitalized Dementia Patients
**PMID:** 41247831 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Interview completion numbers** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Researchers interviewed 32 racially and ethnically diverse family caregivers"
- **Abstract says:** "Thirty-two caregivers were recruited from two hospitals; 18 completed interviews and surveys, and 14 completed surveys only"
- **Problem:** The digest says "interviewed 32" but only 18 completed interviews. The caveats correctly note "18 completed interviews" but the main text overstates.

The themes and findings are accurately represented.

## Study 13: Functional Ability at Admission Predicts Survival and Discharge Home from Palliative Care Units
**PMID:** 42660629 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Survival and discharge statistics** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Patients with higher functional scores lived significantly longer (median 40 vs. 15 days) and were more likely to be discharged home (62% vs. 28%)"
- **Abstract says:** "Median OS was significantly longer in the high (40 days) and middle (23 days) groups than in the low (15 days) group" and "Discharge-to-home rates were significantly higher in the high group (62.1%) than in the middle (24.7%) and low (27.5%) groups"
- **Problem:** The digest compares high vs. low groups only, which is accurate but omits the middle group. The discharge comparison of "62% vs. 28%" conflates middle (24.7%) and low (27.5%) groups. The 28% figure doesn't appear in the abstract.
- **Suggested fix:** "Patients with higher functional scores lived significantly longer (median 40 days for high-scoring patients vs. 15 days for low-scoring) and were more likely to be discharged home (62% vs. 25-28% for lower-scoring groups)."

**Issue B. FIM score cut-off** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "A total functional score of 87 or above (out of 126) best predicted the possibility of going home"
- **Abstract says:** "The cut-off total FIM score for predicting discharge to home was 87.0"
- **Problem:** The digest adds "(out of 126)" for context, which is helpful and matches the FIM scale range mentioned in methods. This is accurate.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required.

## Study 14: Cancer Cachexia Present in Over 90% of Patients with Advanced Disease, Contributing to Most Deaths
**PMID:** 42674857 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Omitted conflict of interest** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** No mention of industry relationships in caveats
- **Abstract says:** "RJS has received personal fees for consulting from Artelo, Actimed, Faraday, Helsinn, Pfizer, Abbott and Nutricia. BJL has received personal fees for consulting from Artelo, Actimed, Faraday, Kyona Kirin, Toray Pfizer, Abbott and Nutricia."
- **Problem:** Multiple authors have consulting relationships with pharmaceutical and nutrition companies, which is relevant given the topic of cachexia (a condition these companies develop treatments for). This should be disclosed in caveats.
- **Suggested fix:** Add to Caveats: "Some authors have consulting relationships with pharmaceutical and nutrition companies."

The statistics (513 deaths, 3 years, 68-92% cachexia prevalence, 70% contribution to deaths) are accurately reported.
