# Topic Memory: cardiovascular disease
_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

## Established findings
- Mood is a cardiovascular variable: depression independently raises CVD events and death in diabetes beyond glycemic control, a lifetime depression history raises cardiometabolic risk 15 years out with no distinguishing symptom subtype, and depression–frailty–osteoporosis clusters carry the steepest mortality (PMIDs 42624218, 42826766, 42821655; Oct–Dec 2026)
- Cardiovascular risk scores track brain health: PREVENT/ASCVD scores predict cognitive decline and correlate with amyloid, tau, cortical thinning and white matter damage (PMIDs 42561811, 42898366, 42821750; Oct–Nov 2026)
- CVD's share of health burden in the 80+ population is falling (42.3%→28.6%) while disability's share rises; demand shifts toward function-preserving and dementia-capable care (PMID 42594758; recurring Sep 2026–Oct 2026)
- Peripheral arterial disease is late-diagnosed with 30–50% five-year mortality and is undertreated relative to coronary and cerebral disease; it also contributes disproportionately to women's CVD burden (PMIDs 42772316, 42912409; Oct 2026)
- Basic hypertension control still fails in rural older adults — 76% of diagnosed cases uncontrolled; self-reported activity is a poor proxy for who is doing well (PMID 42720604; recurring Oct 2026)
- CAC scanning adds meaningful information mainly in the borderline PREVENT risk band, not across the board (PMID 42647022; recurring Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Risk signals hiding in data already collected: sleep-study single-lead ECG predicts 10-year AF, stroke, heart failure and death; retinal photographs flag later depression and CVD; nocturia flags risk in men with apnea (PMIDs 42852996, 42551563, 42600420; Oct–Dec 2026)
- Social and environmental exposures as measurable cardiovascular biology: financial strain and discrimination linked to inflammatory protein shifts in older Black adults with existing heart disease; air pollution linked to accelerated biological aging with partial reversal after air-quality improvement (PMIDs 42843435, 42617978; Nov 2026)
- Reproductive and sex-specific history entering cardiac charts: menopause before 45 after acute coronary syndrome; maternal hypertensive disorders accounting for 25% of incident CVD in European women (PMIDs 42837120, 42912409; Oct 2026)
- Sensory and literacy barriers to cardiac self-care: difficulty reading small print predicted failure to progress in cardiac rehab behavior change and more rehospitalization (PMID 42887362; Oct 2026)
- Diet scored by inflammatory potential linked to stroke specifically (64% higher) but not heart attack or CV death (PMID 42892123; Oct 2026)
- Psychiatric treatment and vascular aging: long-term lithium associated with far less aortic and coronary calcification; more mood episodes with more calcification (PMID 42674302; Dec 2026)
- Oral GLP-1 therapy as a hypoglycemia-sparing alternative to insulin in older diabetes patients, offset by high GI side-effect and discontinuation rates (PMID 42815506; Oct 2026)
