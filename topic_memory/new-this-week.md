# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

## Established findings
- Screening and measurement systems routinely miss what they exist to catch: claims dementia algorithms (42760257), frailty indexes labeling 10-71% as frail (42760259), PASRR catching 11% of serious mental illness (42771910), 545 different outcomes in frail-inpatient research with only 26% patient-reported (42791643, 2026-09-26).
- Self-report loses to objective measurement, but objective tools need calibration: device steps vs self-reported exercise (42785603), felt age driving self-rated cognition while test scores diverge (42791633), plasma p-tau217 accuracy hinging on the lab's chosen cutoff (42787809).
- Marketed premium options keep failing head-to-head or within-family tests: fecal transplant for IBS (42785338), adaptive vs conventional DBS (42765432), TIVA vs inhaled anesthesia (42584898), diet-and-epigenetic-clock associations vanishing between identical twins (42791452).
- A diagnosis or label is a poor reason to withhold treatment: dementia and STEMI care (42754545), frailty and cardiac rehab (42772577), late-stage Parkinson's excluded from trials (42770427), cancer history and mitral valve repair (42786380).
- Non-drug programs hold up where drug evidence is thin, but structure, dose and task specificity are the active variables: exergames (42773385, 42773001), supervised training lowering CRP (42786581), FEARFALL tying its sham (42773257), gait-initiation practice working where general walking training did not (42789437) against weak sleep-drug evidence in Parkinson's (42782386).
- Vascular damage is a recurring dementia signal: TIA (42777184), microvascular disease of eye/kidney/nerve (42787302), stroke during follow-up in the 85+ (42791168).

## Emerging threads
- Sleep as a clinical signal rather than a symptom to medicate: light-vs-deep sleep and small-vessel damage (42779207), sleep disturbance in dementia linked to suicide risk independent of depression (42790647).
- Blood biomarkers spreading from detection to treatment monitoring, eligibility gatekeeping, and new populations: lecanemab proteomics (42785333), memory-clinic cutoffs (42787809), Down syndrome trajectories (42786385).
- Technology and algorithms entering aging care ahead of standards and validation: AgeTech standards gap (42779484), hip-fracture AI models not bedside-ready (42771090), sensor/VR fall screening still in progress (42789327).
- Healthy-aging metrics pushed into midlife, with mobility declining first (42790659); lean mass outperforming grip strength as a functional marker (42790802).
- Prediction before symptoms appear: quadriceps strength and tendon stiffness preceding knee osteoarthritis (42784800).
- Populations still largely unstudied: Asian and Asian American dementia programming (42788840), adults with Down syndrome (42786385), bereaved older parents (42791223).
