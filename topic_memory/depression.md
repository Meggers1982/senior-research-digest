# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

## Established findings
- Loneliness (not objective isolation) mediates social connection → depression, especially for family support and women (PMID 42667132, Aug 2026; PMID 42480060, Jul 2026)
- Anti-inflammatory diet associated with 8–21% lower depression risk in adults 70+ (PMID 42154116, May 2026)
- Missing personally important social activities dramatically increases caregiver depression (PMID 42249768, Jun 2026)
- Childhood adversity predicts late-life depression and physical health via sustained depression (PMID 42633642, Aug 2026; PMID 42366693, Jun 2026)
- Physical activity buffers depression risk even with high childhood trauma exposure (PMID 42578940, Aug 2026)
- Pet therapy meta-evidence now strong for depression (SMD = 0.73; PMID 42400739, Jul 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Heart-brain-emotion feedback loop framework linking cardiovascular, mood, and cognitive decline (PMID 42628846, Aug 2026)
- Activity variety (not frequency) may protect cognition—needs replication beyond single cohort (PMID 42658656, Aug 2026)
- Caregiver self-stigma and entrapment as intervention targets for depression/suicidality (PMID 42658649, Aug 2026)
- Arthritis as underappreciated driver of depression in multimorbidity clusters (PMID 42636431, Aug 2026)
