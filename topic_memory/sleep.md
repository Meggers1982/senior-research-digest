# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

## Established findings
- U-shaped sleep duration risk in older adults: very short and especially very long sleep track with mortality, cognitive impairment, depression persistence, and reduced kidney function (PMIDs 42648048, 42690762, 42628578, 42686460, Sept 2026; earlier 42241923, 42623973)
- Poor sleep and depressed mood are tightly linked across settings — community, home care, nursing home, and cognitive impairment (PMIDs 42628578, 42727201, 42711172, Sept 2026; 41705738, 42265577)
- CPAP benefits concentrate in those with high nocturnal hypoxia burden, and may stabilize telomere length (PMIDs 42403561, 41840472)
- Sleep quality mediates pathways from chronic pain to frailty and from fragmented sleep to gait instability/falls (PMIDs 42423848, 42302301)
- Sleep regularity and sleep architecture, measured by wearables, matter independently of total hours (PMIDs 42649471, 42686460)

## Emerging threads
- Pharmacology of senior sleep: prescribing cascades that add a sleep aid to treat a drug side effect (PMID 42727946), cannabis as an unreported sleep aid with high use-disorder rates (PMID 42712247), first orexin agonist for narcolepsy with majority side effects (PMID 42714024)
- Sleep EEG as an Alzheimer's marker moving from subtyping to mechanism — infraslow spindle rhythm reduced in AD (PMID 42708477; earlier 42249255, glymphatic review 42264186)
- Napping is contested: no mortality signal (42648048) but associations with slower processing speed and lower cognition (42596580, 42690762)
- Subjective sleep quality vs measured hours — which predicts what remains unresolved (42711172 vs 42648048)
- Clustered assessment models putting sleep alongside metabolic, mood, nutrition and strength measures (circadian syndrome 42567211; perioperative aging burden index 42600483)
- Nutrient status and sleep: vitamin D tied to objectively measured sleep, stronger in Black participants (PMID 42696426) — needs replication
- CBT-I and epigenetic aging (PMID 42586102) — still awaiting replication
- Low-cost, low-tech self-guided programs: printed lifestyle booklet outperformed an app on insomnia (PMID 42503319); weighted blankets safe but ineffective in dementia care (PMID 42679410)
- Formal consensus guidance arriving for Parkinson's sleep and bladder symptoms (PMIDs 42731925, 42716874)

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Sleep as Anti-Aging Medicine" (Aug 2026): CBT-I trial + CPAP-telomere + glymphatic research as longevity medicine
- "The Gateway Target" (Sept 1 2026): sleep as modifiable mediator connecting pain, falls, cognition
- "Long sleep as an under-read vital sign" (Sept 15 2026): 9-10+ hours and heavy napping as signals to investigate, not reassurance
