## Topic Memory: sarcopenia
_Last updated: 2026-10-30_

## Established findings
- Muscle quality (fat infiltration/density) outperforms muscle size for predicting power loss, surgical recovery, cancer recurrence and mortality (PMIDs 42753450, 42779183, 42648596; contested in TAVI, 42567492)
- Grip strength is most useful as a raw, repeated number; weak/not-weak labels swing dramatically by criteria set, and strict cut-offs flag people too late (PMIDs 42697178, 42556116)
- Frailty predicts disability, falls and lost independence better than muscle mass alone; transitions are asymmetric, making pre-frailty the actionable stage (PMIDs 42669283, 42600484, 42537596)
- Low strength or sarcopenia before surgery, dialysis, stroke, heart attack or cancer treatment predicts mortality, complications and failure to regain independence; now extended to abdominal and thoracic surgery (PMIDs 42946212, 42906881, 42782098, 42660496, 42806518)
- Weakness and body composition, not body weight, drive associations with mood, cognition and metabolic risk; normal BMI routinely hides sarcopenia and sarcopenic obesity (PMIDs 42928822, 42741804, 42822208)
- Resistance training is the intervention with the clearest evidence for muscle-specific strength; supplements, aerobic work and combined programs did not show significant benefit (PMID 42570706); timing of day doesn't matter (42791208)
- Later-life weight loss, especially GLP-1-assisted, needs explicit muscle-preservation planning — protein, resistance work, functional monitoring rather than weight alone (PMIDs 42952307, 42772087)

## Emerging threads
- Osteosarcopenia as a single clinical entity: double mortality versus either condition alone, common in type 2 diabetes, and possibly modifiable by bone drug choice (PMIDs 41818462, 42828564, 42904865); lean mass also protective for the spine (42790802)
- Medications as muscle levers in both directions — GLP-1s, azilsartan, denosumab, and the sedative/antihypertensive/antivertigo classes tied to frailty progression (PMIDs 42952307, 42872079, 42904865, 42669283)
- Cheap routine lab proxies for muscle keep appearing but are hitting validation limits; the blood sarcopenia index failed to predict cardiometabolic multimorbidity (PMIDs 42777518, 42822209, 42802037)
- Grip asymmetry between hands may carry information that a single reading misses, while weakness alone did not (PMID 42753932)
- Multimorbidity as an upstream driver of incident sarcopenia, 17-19% population attributable fraction (PMID 42764080)
- AI-read muscle ultrasound is advancing fast but is not clinic-ready: in-house datasets, operator dependence, no outcome data (PMID 42893969)
- Gait speed, not grip, tracked with brain gray matter in mild cognitive impairment — possible divergence within sarcopenia measures (PMID 42920257)
- Care-delivery gap: nutrition plans collapse at transitions between hospital, rehab, facility and home (PMID 42807943)
