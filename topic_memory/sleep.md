# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-09-01_

## Established findings
- CPAP cardiovascular protection concentrated in those with higher nocturnal hypoxia burden (PMID 42403561, June–July 2026)
- CPAP may stabilize telomere length via inflammation reduction—pilot RCT (PMID 41840472, August 2026)
- Poor sleep quality linked to gait instability and fall risk (PMID 42302301, June 2026)
- Sleep quality mediates chronic pain–frailty pathway (PMID 42423848, July 2026)
- Short sleep and multimorbidity risk in longitudinal cohort (PMID 42623973, August 2026)
- Dementia caregivers benefit from app-based mindfulness for stress and sleep (PMID 42247635, June 2026)

## Emerging threads
- CBT-I may slow epigenetic aging—first RCT evidence (PMID 42586102, July 2026); watch for replication
- Deep sleep, REM, and sleep regularity linked to aging hallmarks via UK Biobank accelerometry (PMID 42649471, September 2026)
- Glymphatic system as mechanistic link between sleep and brain aging (PMID 42264186, August 2026)
- Racial disparities in caregiver sleep vulnerability—Black caregivers more affected by role overload (PMID 42121379, July 2026)
- Climate change impacts on older adult mental health mediated by sleep disruption (PMID 41782276, March 2026)
- Maternal sleep deprivation and intergenerational brain aging—emerging narrative review (PMID 42287331, June 2026)
