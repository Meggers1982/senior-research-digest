# Topic Memory: polypharmacy
_Last updated: 2026-07-14_

## Established findings
- Opioid-benzodiazepine co-prescription associated with ~3× frailty risk (PMID 42285234, Jun 2026)
- Fall-risk-increasing drugs (opioids, antidepressants, sedatives) drive fall hospitalizations; antihypertensives alone do not (PMID 42343007, Jun 2026)
- Benzodiazepine deprescribing is safe with gradual, supported tapering (PMID 42089738, May 2026)
- ~50% of older adults globally have polypharmacy; each added medication raises mortality ~5% (PMID 42412515, Jul 2026)
- Hospital stays inconsistently optimize medications without structured pharmacist involvement (PMID 42047996, Jun 2026)

## Emerging threads
- ARBs may protect against sarcopenia—needs replication (PMID 42414787, Jul 2026)
- Prescribing cascades increasingly catalogued; antidepressants, sedatives, dementia drugs top triggers (PMID 41949780, Apr 2026)
- Informal caregivers largely untrained for medication management role (PMID 42434829, Jul 2026)
- Pharmacy-based frailty screening tools being validated for community use (PMID 42295695, Jun 2026)
