## Study 5: Behavioral Problems and Sedentary Time Linked to Poorer Quality of Life in Severe Dementia
**PMID:** 42627761 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sedentary time direction reversed** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "behavioral problems and more sedentary time were significantly associated with poorer overall quality of life"
- **Abstract says:** "Behavioural problems and non-sedentary time were significantly associated with overall QoL"
- **Problem:** The abstract reports that *non-sedentary* time (not sedentary time) was associated with QoL. The digest reverses the variable name. While the interpretation ("reducing agitation and encouraging movement") is directionally consistent with the abstract's conclusion ("increasing non-sedentary time may enhance the overall QoL"), the specific variable name is incorrect.
- **Suggested fix:** "behavioral problems and less non-sedentary time (i.e., more time spent sedentary) were significantly associated with poorer overall quality of life"

## Study 10: Stopping Statins at 75 and Older Did Not Increase Death Risk in Large Trial
**PMID:** 42580354 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size discrepancy** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "1,160 adults aged 75 and older"
- **Abstract says:** "1180 participants were randomly assigned, and 1160 participants were included in the analysis of the primary estimand"
- **Problem:** The digest uses 1,160, which is the primary estimand analysis sample, not the randomized sample (1,180). This is defensible as the primary analysis population, but could be clearer.
- **Suggested fix:** "In this French randomized trial, 1,180 adults aged 75 and older taking statins for primary prevention were randomly assigned, with 1,160 included in the primary analysis."

**Issue B. Mortality percentages slightly differ** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Mortality was 7.2% in the discontinuation group versus 7.9% in the continuation group"
- **Abstract says:** "48 (7·9%) of 604 participants in the statin continuation group and 35 (7·2%) of 484 participants in the statin discontinuation group had died"
- **Problem:** The digest has the numbers correct but attributes them to the opposite groups. The abstract says 7.9% in continuation and 7.2% in discontinuation, which matches the digest. No error upon re-reading.

No factual errors found on closer inspection. The framing is appropriate.

**Verdict revised:** ✅ Accurate

## Study 11: Dementia Risk at 90+ Still Higher for Women and Black Americans
**PMID:** 42551464 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Risk comparison framing** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Women had 89% higher risk than men"
- **Abstract says:** "dementia risk was higher in female than in male participants (subdistribution hazard ratio [sHR] 1·89, 95% CI 1·30-2·76)"
- **Problem:** An sHR of 1.89 means 89% higher risk, which is correct. No error.

**Issue B. Characterization of APOE ε4 finding** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "the ε4 variant showed a trend toward higher risk that was not statistically significant"
- **Abstract says:** "not significantly higher in APOE ε4 carriers than in non-carriers (sHR 1·51, 0·92-2·47)"
- **Problem:** The term "trend" is a reasonable interpretation of a non-significant HR of 1.51, though the abstract doesn't use this word. Acceptable framing.

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

**Verdict revised:** ✅ Accurate

## Study 13: Brain White Matter Changes May Help Predict Dementia Beyond Standard Risk Factors
**PMID:** 42568005 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sensitivity/specificity figures** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "The model achieved over 90% sensitivity and specificity"
- **Abstract says:** "sensitivity = 90.9%; specificity = 93.2%"
- **Problem:** "Over 90%" is accurate for both figures. No error.

**Issue B. Study population description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "176 adults with dementia, 176 age-matched cognitively normal older adults, and 176 younger healthy adults"
- **Abstract says:** "individuals with dementia (n = 176), age-matched cognitively normal older adults (n = 176), and a young healthy reference cohort (n = 176)"
- **Problem:** Accurate match. No error.

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract.

**Verdict revised:** ✅ Accurate
