# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

## Established findings
- Depression commonly precedes and accompanies neurodegenerative disease: it clusters with other complaints in the 1–5 years before an Alzheimer's diagnosis, rises steadily over a decade in Parkinson's, and roughly doubles odds of probable dementia in national data (PMIDs 42847312, 42839627, 42766893, 42731172; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Family caregivers carry independent, clinically meaningful depression that patient-focused services do not resolve; grief- and activity-based interventions help adjacent outcomes but leave depression scores unmoved (PMIDs 42844914, 42830600, 42853972, 42749467, 42791221; Sep–Oct 2026)
- The treatment gap is at the handoff, not the screen: a third of older Americans with major depression untreated, 65+ least likely to get outpatient care, "I can handle it myself" the top barrier; pharmacists and frontline carers are willing to screen but lack payment, time and referral pathways (PMIDs 42624700, 42313335, 42788366; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Reachable, varied, socially engaging activity tracks with better mood and lower dementia risk; program counts and frequency alone do not (PMIDs 42721779, 42658656, 42789932, 42858616; Aug–Oct 2026)
- Depression and physical/functional decline move together — frailty, liver disease risk, loss of function (PMIDs 42599324, 42667890; Jul–Oct 2026)
- Neurostimulation hype outpaces evidence: personalized accelerated TMS failed to beat sham; 40 Hz tACS improved sleep in mild Alzheimer's but not mood (PMIDs 42749167, 42858052; Oct–Dec 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Depression may only track with Alzheimer's pathology in APOE ɛ4 carriers — a possible explanation for years of conflicting findings; untreated duration also mattered (PMID 42857391, Oct 2026)
- Place and environment as mood exposures: air pollution reaching the brain partly via vascular and mood/sleep pathways; community sanitation linked to depression (PMIDs 42632461, 42842074; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Documentation equity — memory concerns recorded for only 13.8% of older patients and least often in deprived neighborhoods (PMID 42842937, Oct 2026)
- Subjective experience as an early marker: post-stroke self-discrepancy predicting later depression, depression tilting word meaning, teenage psychological health predicting self-rated memory at 67 (PMIDs 42744143, 42744145, 42658651; Oct–Dec 2026)
- Psychedelics and biomarker disclosure both testing the "will this harm them emotionally?" assumption and not finding harm (PMIDs 42546985, 42832237; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Companion chatbots reduce loneliness more reliably than depression; evidence base still thin (PMID 42623930, Oct 2026)
