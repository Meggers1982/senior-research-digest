# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

## Established findings
- Anti-amyloid and symptomatic Alzheimer's drugs underdeliver against expectation: genotype- and microbleed-driven ARIA risk (42907679, 42908770, 2026-10-23) and donepezil's front-loaded, modest structural benefit over 36 months (42911381, 2026-10-24).
- Popular programs keep missing primary endpoints while participants rate them highly: caregiver text referral (42869650), robots/VR/apps (42867198), personalized vs generic phone coaching (42895589), dementia rehab with caregiver support (42907949), and now marginal usability in a voice-assistant pilot despite 97% retention (42913442, 2026-10-24).
- Facility and system process — not amenities or labels — determines outcomes: care home UTI diagnostics and 60-day mortality (42887518), hospice flags driven by one input and patient mix (42895138), staffing continuity and family contact as protective in LTC mental health during COVID (42909849, 2026-10-24).
- Screening tools are being pruned as often as added: incontinence too weak for fall screening after 75 (42904834), frailty failing to predict cognitive progression in HIV (42900505, 2026-10-24), ordinary risk factors matching blood biomarkers for dementia prediction (42913444, 2026-10-24).
- Low-impact exercise has real clinical standing but real boundaries: aquatic and mind-body exercise for blood pressure (42887271), versus no bone benefit from step counts after menopause (42909484, 2026-10-24).

## Emerging threads
- Blood biomarkers hitting their ceiling: positive predictive value only 16–24% over 12 years in a community cohort (42913444), p-tau217 trajectories tracking decline while GFAP does not (42909513) — both 2026-10-24, against last week's diagnosis-revision and logistics findings (42899313, 42906069, 42908786).
- Imaging shifting toward risk stratification and trial efficiency: brain age gap predicting memory decline (42911347), neuromelanin MRI for Parkinson's (42913074), MSA volumetrics cutting required trial size ~60% (42897443) — all 2026-10-24.
- Bone and fracture risk as a cluster: GLP-1 bone density signal at high weight loss (42920024), steps protective only premenopausally (42909484), rising anxiety trajectory and hip fracture (42909516) — all 2026-10-24.
- Travel and access burden being engineered out of specialty neurology care: remote DBS programming noninferior at 90 days (42920097), pallidothalamic focused ultrasound widening candidacy (42900661) — 2026-10-24.
- Decision aids and goals-of-care documentation as an intervention target: a 12-minute video shifting both documented conversations and treatment preference in advanced CKD (42911803, 2026-10-24).
- Mechanism-chain framing of loneliness: no direct effect on osteoarthritis, entire pathway through depression, inactivity and chair-rise difficulty (42911759, 2026-10-24).
