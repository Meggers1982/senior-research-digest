# Topic Memory: osteoporosis
_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

## Established findings
- Post-fracture care collapses at the handoff across health systems: wrist/forearm breaks rarely coded as osteoporosis, <20% of men treated after fragility fracture, and only 29.5% of Japanese hip fracture patients starting medication by 2023 (PMIDs 42448012, 42735505, 42288172; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Screening and awareness are heavily sex-skewed: DXA ordered at 0.04% of eligible men's visits vs 5.1% of women's; men also scored worst on osteoporosis knowledge and had the higher silent vertebral fracture rate (PMIDs 42493193, 42600719, 42624233)
- Bone, muscle, and fat decline as one system; visceral fat, not body weight, tracks with lower bone density, and sex-specific timing clusters around menopause (PMIDs 42678368, 42811242, 42641825)
- Exercise is the most consistent non-drug lever, with small-to-moderate density gains strongest at the femoral neck across 162 RCTs; chair-based or purely aerobic programing is unlikely to move bone (PMID 42575297, Dec 2026)
- Specific drug classes drive risk and harm more than pill counts: systemic glucocorticoids (~70% higher fracture rate), and oral bisphosphonates administered to people who cannot swallow them safely (PMIDs 42722898, 42776717)
- Risk scoring is sensitive to recent falls and fracture recency: FRAXplus reclassified 16% upward into treatment eligibility (PMID 42689245)

## Emerging threads
- High-dose vitamin D in already-sufficient older adults did not prevent fractures over 7.3 years — a candidate for deprescribing, alongside routine post-hip-fracture X-rays that changed no decisions (PMIDs 42704968, 42732263)
- Treatment personalization and persistence: β-CTX-guided zoledronic acid deferral, five-year repeat romosozumab courses with ~28% dropout, and prior adherence predicting romosozumab discontinuation (PMIDs 42398845, 42769300, 42362099)
- Diagnostic limits of DXA, and practical workarounds: MRI-derived VBQ plus metabolic markers, and Vertebral Fracture Assessment finding silent fractures in 15.9% of a primary care sample (PMIDs 42492717, 42624233)
- Osteoporosis as a multi-system signal: hearing loss, vascular calcification, oral function predicting frailty, and compounded bone/heart risk in women with epilepsy at menopause (PMIDs 42574660, 42749103, 41331404, 42810186)
- Diet pattern over single nutrients: Mediterranean adherence linked to lower fracture risk; fracture-healing supplements still unproven (PMIDs 42641790, 42476484)
- Digital self-management acceptable to older women, with cost and privacy as the sticking points (PMID 42442524)
