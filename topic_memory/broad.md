# Topic Memory: broad senior living
_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

## Established findings
- Pain, depression and physical decline move together in later life; screening for one should prompt screening for the other (PMIDs 42636431, 42633642, Aug 2026; 42749164, Oct 2026)
- Communication failures — not drug choice — are the recurring weak point in older adults' care: antipsychotic risks unexplained to families (42640817), hospital deprescribing that unravels after discharge (42785158), and inadequate treatment information as the top driver of bad ER visits (42762629)
- Routine lab values are being repurposed as prognostic tools, consistently with only moderate accuracy (42634733, 42631826, Aug 2026; 42777518, 42766789, Oct 2026)
- Progressive lower-body strength work remains the reliable route to better balance; add-on neurotechnology did not beat sham (42753671, Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Fall risk is being relocated to overlooked places: forefoot deformity (42766945), Charles Bonnet hallucinations (42833804), gaze fixation on the next step (42753670), and gait transitions rather than straight-line walking (42762698)
- De-implementation of low-value aging care as a formal quality discipline, starting with hospital bed alarms (42833806)
- Negative trials for marketed "personalized" neurotechnology in older adults (42749167, 42753671) — watch whether marketing claims adjust
- Blood and CSF biomarkers now shaping dementia diagnosis logistics and treatment eligibility, including mixed pathology in Lewy body dementia (42841041, 42832237); builds on the earlier frailty-as-stratification argument (42641765)
- Long-term care operations and financing entering the digest: multi-year Medicaid HCBS enrollment durations (42834564), flawed calorie equations in nursing homes (42759166)
- Early-stage ("pre-frail") social isolation carries measurable mortality risk, with sex and education differences (42762630); extends the earlier loneliness/companionship thread (42634112, 42629518)

## Feature ideas already pitched

- Falls prevention evidence audit: bed alarms and balance gadgets fail while feet, vision, lighting and transitions go unchecked (Oct 2026 run)
