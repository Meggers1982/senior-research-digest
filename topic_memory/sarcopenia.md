# Topic Memory: sarcopenia
_Last updated: 2026-08-07_

## Established findings
- Combined exercise + protein (≥1.2 g/kg/day) + vitamin D (≥700 IU/day) outperforms single interventions for sarcopenia/fall prevention (PMID 42208412, May 2026)
- Sarcopenia linked to higher dementia risk (44–300% depending on severity); sarcopenic obesity shows highest risk (PMID 42155951, May 2026; PMID 42155950, May 2026)
- Sarcopenia associated with respiratory disease risk (COPD 37%, asthma 35%, ILD 74% higher) via inflammatory pathways (PMID 42391675, July 2026)
- Osteoporosis and sarcopenia share ~25% genetic correlation; same lifestyle factors (exercise, sleep, not smoking) protect both (PMID 42380078, July 2026)
- Long-term statin use shows no adverse muscle effects (PMID 42373884, June 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Blood-based biological aging markers may identify sarcopenia risk in adults under 40 (PMID 42449042, July 2026)—needs replication
- ARBs may protect against sarcopenia (PMID 42414787) vs. modest lean-mass reduction in women (PMID 42373884)—conflicting signals, watch for clarification
- Sensory impairment (vision/hearing) as gateway to broader functional decline including sarcopenia (PMID 42492227, July 2026)
- Housing/environmental quality (water, fuel, building type) as modifiable sarcopenia risk factor (PMID 42349091, June 2026)
