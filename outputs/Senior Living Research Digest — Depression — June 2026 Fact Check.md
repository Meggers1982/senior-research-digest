### Study 8: Smartphone App Plus Community Care Reduces Depression in Rural Older Adults
**PMID:** 42085615 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study design terminology** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "294 adults aged 65+ were divided into an intervention group (N=148)"
- **Abstract says:** "A quasi-experimental, nonequivalent control group pretest-posttest design was conducted with 294 participants"
- **Problem:** The digest says participants were "divided," which could imply randomization. The abstract specifies "nonequivalent control group" design where "Participants were allocated based on their scheduled appointment times."
- **Suggested fix:** "294 adults aged 65+ were allocated based on appointment times to an intervention group (N=148)"

**Issue B. Headline framing** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Smartphone App Plus Community Care"
- **Abstract says:** "8-week intervention featured a community-based music storytelling program incorporating local cultural elements and digital education"
- **Problem:** The headline emphasizes "smartphone app" but the intervention was primarily a music storytelling program with digital education components, not primarily app-based.
- **Suggested fix:** "Community Music Program With Digital Education Reduces Depression in Rural Older Adults"

### Study 11: Slow Walking Speed Plus Depression Doubles Fracture Risk
**PMID:** 42067696 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline overstatement** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Slow Walking Speed Plus Depression Doubles Fracture Risk"
- **Abstract says:** 46% higher risk of any fracture, 64% higher risk of MOF for slow gait + depressive symptoms
- **Problem:** "Doubles" implies 100% increase; actual increase was 46% for any fracture. The 64% increase for MOF is closer but still not "doubles."
- **Suggested fix:** "Slow Walking Speed Plus Depression Increases Fracture Risk by Nearly 50%"

The body text correctly states 46% and 64% figures, so only the headline is problematic.
