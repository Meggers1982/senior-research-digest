# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

## Established findings
- Palliative referrals arrive far too late; the fixes identified are structural — embedded referral triggers, co-location, renaming the service, systematic case-finding (PMID 42504041, 42714185, Oct 2026; PMID 42734951, 42713708, Sept 2026)
- Only specific written instructions (DNR/do-not-hospitalize) track with fewer hospital transfers, and residents are still moved against documented wishes (PMID 42714187, Oct 2026)
- Family caregiver burden and unmet information/practical needs shape distress and how families remember a death, and are rarely screened (PMID 42804262, Sept 2026; PMID 42728769, 42697036, Sept 2026)
- Cheap routine measures predict survival in advanced cancer better than intuition, but only modestly (calf circumference + albumin/CRP, AUC ~0.63; PMID 42674364, Oct 2026)
- Preventive drugs linger past usefulness at end of life; deprescribing remains reactive and depends on an owned, scheduled conversation (PMID 42519959, 42533730, Oct 2026; PMID 42730948, Sept 2026)
- The pharmacologic evidence base at end of life is thin: null lidocaine trial for opioid-refractory pain (PMID 42536632), low/very low certainty for propofol sedation (PMID 42533745), no proven benefit of routine urine drug testing (PMID 42362165)

## Emerging threads
- Prognostic model accuracy is population-dependent: 0.90 in nursing home intake data (PMID 42748398) vs 0.55–0.76 and poor external validation in advanced dementia (PMID 42533729) — while physicians remain broadly favorable toward AI prognosis despite low AI literacy (PMID 42463061)
- Patients preferred AI-written empathy 72.4% of the time; positive-valence language ("trust," "joy") drove preference (PMID 42599269)
- Decision aids inform without resolving decisional conflict, satisfaction or regret (PMID 42517466)
- Access and counting gaps: intellectual disability linked to far less cancer treatment though palliative access held (PMID 42490651); solo APRNs carrying all end-of-life conversations in rural critical access hospitals (PMID 42406410); place-of-death data missing hospice and nursing home categories (PMID 42544535); China's framework-first policy sequence (PMID 42592757)
- Palliative care expanding beyond oncology into progressive neurological disease (PMID 42714185)
- Long-tail bereavement: ~12% of parents carry high-intensity grief years later, with poverty the strongest predictor (PMID 42603866)
- Consent for palliative sedation often undocumented — a governance, not pharmacology, problem (PMID 42533745)
