# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Established findings
- Medication burden remains the most reliable practical thread: named prescribing cascades (PMID 42727946), pharmacist-led deprescribing (PMID 42731364), benzodiazepine-to-orexin-antagonist taper best in 65+ (PMID 42717137), and now the tapering gap in palliative care where beta-blockers, PPIs and antidepressants are stopped outright (PMID 42731737, Sep 2026).
- Sleep is a repeatedly implicated, treatable lever: tau and slow-wave disruption (PMID 42728376), sleep regularity and eye disease (PMID 42017838), Parkinson's sleep decline (PMID 42726411), restless/short sleep preceding motoric cognitive risk syndrome (PMID 42721722), and near-universal underdiagnosis of restless legs in Parkinson's (PMID 42721723).
- Cheap, chart-available markers keep predicting decline as well as costly ones: parental longevity (PMID 42726198), estimated BMR (PMID 42715815), frailty in oncology (PMID 42731927), five-domain intrinsic capacity across three national cohorts (PMID 42727174).
- Palliative care arrives late in usual pathways (PMID 42713708) while dedicated longitudinal team clinics markedly raise hospice enrollment (PMID 42727035).
- Hip fracture leaves lasting functional loss, worst in the 70s, with continued decline after 85 (PMID 42721595); most people otherwise stay independent until the final two years (PMID 42720597).

## Emerging threads
- Non-memory early signals of brain decline: mild behavioral impairment (PMID 42704084), psychological functioning as strongest intrinsic-capacity domain (PMID 42727174), everyday-function trouble in cognitively normal adults (PMID 42730663), posture-linked cognition in orthostatic hypotension (PMID 42732657).
- Equity and representation in Alzheimer's blood testing: p-tau217 validation cohorts skewed White and research-based (PMID 42722299); failed blood draws concentrated in advanced dementia and possibly minority participants (PMID 42732163); MethylCog as an added but tiny signal (PMID 42706996).
- Boundaries of non-drug care: cannabinoids null for dementia agitation (PMID 42704497), mindfulness helps mood but not falls in Parkinson's (PMID 42704301), play therapy promising but loosely defined (PMID 42705005) — against dual-task training's earlier positive ranking (PMID 42731086).
- Caregivers as unrecognized team members: European research-priority gaps (PMID 42708430), narrow formal role for relatives in end-of-life decisions (PMID 42708630), rigidity and apathy as the top burden drivers (PMID 42731983), men's preference for practical support (PMID 42716444).
- Health-system technology and cost: hospital AI tool type determines whether patients benefit or pay more (PMID 42734057) — first appearance, watch for replication.
- Acute-care sentinel events predicting loss of independence: hospitalization for simple cystitis (PMID 42731025); supplements pointing opposite directions by body composition (PMID 42727175).
