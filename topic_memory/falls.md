# Topic Memory: falls
_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## Established findings
- Frailty phenotype (exhaustion, slowness, weakness, low activity, weight loss) predicts falls, disability and mobility loss better than muscle-mass/sarcopenia or equipment-based measures (PMIDs 42600484 Nov 2026; 42347914 Jun 2026; 42696807 Sep 2026)
- Self-reported items — worry about falling, self-rated health, balance confidence, SARC-F, condition counts — add predictive value beyond timed mobility tests, which have modest sensitivity (PMIDs 42708074, 42666693, 42288171 2026; 41538278, 42320833 2026)
- Multidomain impairment (mobility + cognition + vision/hearing) compounds fall and fracture risk; single-domain screening underperforms (PMIDs 42575258 Oct 2026; 42492227, 42235161, 42401083 2026)
- Balance and multicomponent exercise reduces fall rates 23–34%; exercise + protein (≥1.2 g/kg/day) + vitamin D (≥700 IU/day) supports muscle (PMID 42208412, May 2026)
- Medication burden — anticholinergics, fall-risk-increasing drugs, intensive glucose lowering in frail patients — is a recurring modifiable contributor (PMIDs 42542092, 42436877, 41937436, 2026)
- Falls accelerate frailty progression and mortality; a fall should trigger full reassessment, not just injury care (PMID 42361731, Jun 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Falls resilience framework gaining operational content: safe-landing instruction and perturbation training with a dose signal (1 month = reactive saves; 3 months = general balance) (PMIDs 42508443, 42615522, 42716502)
- Digital fall prediction under scrutiny: AI/sensor tools validated as models but untested for actually reducing falls; user acceptance hinges on discretion, cost, privacy (PMIDs 42696799, 42677268, Sep 2026)
- High-tech training has not beaten simpler comparators (VR dual-task vs treadmill; mindfulness added to instability training) (PMIDs 42475977, 42677025)
- Bone health and falls services run in silos — under-coding of osteoporosis after wrist/upper-limb fractures, missed screening in Parkinson's, and a seven-organization call for integration (PMIDs 42448012, 42723140, 42720850)
- EMS contacts as an early-warning and volume-burden signal, in the community and in aged care (PMIDs 42714914, 42489144)
- Setting assumptions challenged: community-dwelling older adults fall more than facility residents; hospital "mobilize unless contraindicated" cut falls 34% (PMIDs 42497575, 42689465)
- Fall trajectories begin in midlife with multimorbidity; fatigability, chronic pain plus slowed processing, and white matter integrity emerging as mechanisms (PMIDs 42288171, 42605068, 42687648, 42679689)
- Policy economics and information quality entering the field: community elder-care services outperforming cash transfers; nearly half of popular fall-prevention videos poor quality with engagement uncorrelated to accuracy (PMIDs 42717430, 42531750)

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Beyond Balance Class: Why Preventing Falls Takes a Village" — systems-level resilience and community infrastructure (Aug 2026)
- "Falls Start in the Mind" — loneliness, aging attitudes, sleep as overlooked risk factors (Aug 2026)
- "Simple Questions Predict Falls Better Than the Timed Walking Test" — self-report and reaction-based screening outperforming standard mobility tests (Sep 2026)
