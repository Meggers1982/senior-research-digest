# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

## Established findings
- Under-detection and misattribution is the recurring care failure: unmeasured swallowing in bvFTD (41989822), incontinence absorbed into laundry routines (41947693), Parkinson's attention loss read as inattentiveness (42492675), oral care treated as an optional extra (42971073, 2026-11-03), specialist palliative involvement invisible in primary care records (41980783, 2026-11-03).
- Popular or heavily marketed interventions keep failing controlled tests: cannabinoids in Parkinson's (42966680), erector spinae block vs physical therapy (42131955), transcranial pulse stimulation vs sham (42975199), higher-frequency DBS at equal energy (42972941), routine paracetamol alongside strong opioids (42309698), methylphenidate for cancer fatigue (42971491).
- Exercise works beyond its target outcome, and combination plus dose is the active ingredient: resistance+balance in Parkinson's (41144919), combined cardio+strength ranked first for sleep (42970245), structured exercise in type 2 diabetes (42973264), VR only at adequate dose and only as an add-on (41819338), tai chi helping balance but not grip or gait speed (42971076).
- Depression, social isolation and physical illness in later life are one intertwined problem: childhood violence predicting both (42248533), depressive symptoms and isolation ranked the top two population-level dementia risk factors (42971073), social life shrinking ahead of decline in high mortality-risk adults (42974667).
- Cheap bedside measures keep outperforming sophisticated scores: grip/chair-stand/gait speed over unvalidated sarcopenia calculators (42790129), dual-task TUG (41201919), grip + metabolic score (42956513).
- What you choose to measure determines what you find: frailty subtypes appear only if cognition and mood are tested (42537597), agitation scales counting outbursts not distress (42425806), functional gains invisible on body-composition scans (42973264).

## Emerging threads
- Subtyping who declines fastest is moving from genetics to co-pathology: GBA1 trajectories (42969188), alpha-synuclein co-pathology with steepest p-tau217 rise (42970748) — and the new blood tests don't detect the second protein.
- Alzheimer's pipeline shifting from detection to decision and disclosure: relational consent for anti-amyloid drugs (42969459), limits of what a blood result tells a family (42970748).
- End-of-life research moving from geography to visibility: home visits beat family willingness (42966200), county palliative physician density (42965848), routine records too sparse to monitor quality (41980783).
- Evidence built without the people who will use it: telerehab after hip replacement untested in older patients (42307257), sarcopenia calculators externally validated twice (42790129), single-country cohorts dominating (42971073, 42974888).
- Aging with HIV as a metabolic and geriatric problem, not an infectious-disease one (42974888).
- Freezing of gait as an attention problem with a reproducible brain correlate, supporting cueing over medication adjustment alone (42957570, with 42492675).
- Low-cost prehabilitation and multidomain bundles proving feasible: breathing-muscle training before cardiac surgery (42401308), 100% retention in a four-component MCI program (42966490).
