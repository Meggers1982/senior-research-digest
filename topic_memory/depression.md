# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-09-13_

## Established findings
- Loneliness — not contact frequency — is the pathway from social connectedness to later depression, strongest via family support and in women; holds into the oldest old (PMIDs 42667132, 42678139, 42480060; Jul–Sep 2026)
- Depression clusters with hearing loss, vision loss, low education and cognitive decline in large population samples, with sharp racial/ethnic disparities (PMIDs 42731172, 42645764, 42437564; Jun–Sep 2026)
- Depression and poor physical function/frailty track together; function loss predicts distress better than pain does (PMIDs 42599324, 42713765, 42549000; Jul–Oct 2026)
- Family caregivers carry independent, screenable depression risk driven by unmet practical/informational needs and internalized stigma (PMIDs 42728769, 42658649, 42472457; Jul–Sep 2026)
- Childhood adversity shapes late-life depression and physical health across 50–60 years; physical activity buffers it (PMIDs 42633642, 42658651, 42578940; Aug 2026)
- Generic exercise reliably improves physical function but not mood in older adults with MCI; social, choreographed movement (dance) is the exception (PMIDs 42545663, 42709503, 42641961; Aug–Sep 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Depression may be a reversible contributor to MCI, not only a risk marker — treated patients showed cognitive gains and some reverted to normal cognition (PMID 42726936, Sep 2026)
- Emotional suffering outranks pain in serious illness, and the most impaired patients cannot self-report it (PMIDs 42724296, 42362166, 42716350; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Service-delivery models moving depression care into non-psychiatric settings: perioperative program, home care model, psychotherapy in dementia (PMIDs 42714910, 42593009, 42714086; Sep 2026)
- Gender-specific risk by living arrangement — solo-living men vs. married women (PMID 42715499, Sep 2026); women also higher distress in Parkinson's ideation and caregiving (PMIDs 42727509, 42728769)
- Depression as a barrier to digital health access rather than only an outcome of technology use (PMID 42731363, Sep 2026)
- Hearing aid refusal driven by social loneliness, not audiogram severity — uptake, not prevalence, is the lever (PMID 42707027, Sep 2026)
- Genetic risk disclosure with trained counseling does not appear to worsen mood, including in Latino participants (PMID 42712082, Sep 2026)
