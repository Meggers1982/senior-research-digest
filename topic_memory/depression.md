# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

## Established findings
- Physical activity is the most consistently supported mood lever in later life, across pooled cohorts, randomized trials and long-term follow-up; social engagement is the best substitute when activity is not possible, and adding movement matters more than reducing sitting (PMIDs 42721779, 42868040, 42892150, 42897872, 42843433, 42658656; Aug–Dec 2026)
- The treatment gap is attitudinal and handoff-related, not purely logistical: ~4.6% of US adults 50+ have a yearly major depressive episode, 65+ least likely to get outpatient care, "I can handle it myself" the top barrier; willing screeners (pharmacists, frontline carers) lack payment, time and referral routes (PMIDs 42624700, 42313335, 42788366; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Depression commonly precedes and accompanies neurodegenerative disease, and distinguishing it from early dementia is now partly biomarker-mapped: amyloid and neurodegeneration markers point to Alzheimer's, vascular and connectivity changes toward depression (PMIDs 42904492, 42847312, 42839627, 42731172; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Testing the "will knowing harm them emotionally?" assumption keeps returning no harm — early blood biomarker disclosure and psilocybin during active cancer treatment both held up (PMIDs 42832237, 42546985; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Depression and physical/functional decline move together, and mood is now being modeled as the mediating link rather than a parallel problem (PMIDs 42873644, 42911759, 42849853, 42599324; Jul 2026–Jan 2027)
- Caregivers carry independent, clinically meaningful depression that patient-focused services do not resolve (PMIDs 42844914, 42830600, 42853972; Sep–Oct 2026)
- Neurostimulation hype outpaces evidence: personalized accelerated TMS failed to beat sham; 40 Hz tACS improved sleep but not mood (PMIDs 42749167, 42858052; Oct–Dec 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Mood tracks with biological aging markers — loneliness, depression and stress linked to epigenetic age acceleration, though an 8-week mind-body trial moved depression without moving telomeres (PMIDs 42566822, 42918505; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Mood and the musculoskeletal system: rising anxiety and hip fracture risk, loneliness-to-osteoarthritis via chair-rise weakness, back pain programs lifting mood (PMIDs 42909516, 42911759, 42892150; Oct 2026)
- Observable, non-clinical early signals — lost appetite before mobility decline, driving-pattern drift before MCI (the latter not explained by depression), skipped outpatient visits before hospitalization (PMIDs 42861605, 42898004, 42849853; Oct 2026–Jan 2027)
- Institutional care as its own mental health setting, largely excluded from national surveys: ~55% depression prevalence in long-term care during COVID-19, with staffing continuity and family contact protective (PMIDs 42909849, 42843433; Oct–Nov 2026)
- Place, environment and lifetime social circumstance as mood and brain exposures — air pollution via vascular/mood/sleep routes, midlife social disadvantage showing in later brain imaging, community sanitation (PMIDs 42632461, 42574936, 42842074; Oct–Nov 2026)
- APOE ε4 as a modifier of the depression–Alzheimer's link, now extending to sleep, alcohol and mood trajectories (PMIDs 42857391, 42877708; Oct 2026)
