# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

## Established findings
- CPAP cardiovascular protection is concentrated in those with higher nocturnal hypoxia burden (PMID 42403561, July 2026)
- Poor sleep quality linked to gait instability and fall risk (PMID 42302301, June 2026)
- Sleep, diet, and circadian rhythms interact to influence cognitive aging (PMIDs 42241923, 42612710)
- Dementia caregivers benefit from app-based mindfulness for stress and sleep (PMID 42247635, June 2026)
- Sleep trajectory instability (inverted-U pattern) predicts stroke risk (PMID 42208063, May 2026)

## Emerging threads
- CBT-I may slow epigenetic aging—first RCT evidence (PMID 42586102, July 2026); watch for replication
- CPAP may stabilize telomere length via inflammation reduction—pilot RCT (PMID 41840472, August 2026)
- Glymphatic system as mechanistic link between sleep and brain aging (PMIDs 42264186, 42579359, 42372236)
- "Chronogeroprotection" framework proposes circadian-aligned interventions for healthy aging (PMID 42372239)
- Racial disparities in caregiver sleep vulnerability—Black caregivers more affected by role overload (PMID 42121379)
- Climate change impacts on older adult mental health mediated by sleep disruption (PMID 41782276)
