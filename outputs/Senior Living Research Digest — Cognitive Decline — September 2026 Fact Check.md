## Study 1: Medicare Annual Wellness Visits linked to earlier detection of mild cognitive impairment
**PMID:** 42456217 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Publication date** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Published: November 2026"
- **Abstract says:** "Epub 2026 Jul 10" (no November date specified; journal volume listed as "2026 Nov;150")
- **Problem:** The journal issue date is November 2026, but the article was actually published (Epub) on July 10, 2026. This is a minor ambiguity in how "published" is defined.
- **Suggested fix:** "**Published:** July 2026 (print issue November 2026)" or simply note the epub date.

**Issue B. Sample size not fully specified** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "4,828 adults aged 65 and older"
- **Abstract says:** The abstract does not explicitly state the sample size of 4,828.
- **Problem:** This specific number cannot be verified from the abstract alone.
- **Suggested fix:** Flag as "Unverifiable from abstract only" or retain if sourced from full text with appropriate notation.

No other factual errors found. Framing is appropriate—observational design is flagged in caveats.

## Study 4: Sleep disturbance patterns over eight years predict Alzheimer's disease risk and cognitive decline
**PMID:** 42542107 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Trajectory group percentages** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "'low-stable' (37%), 'high-peaked' (21%), and 'moderate-increasing' (42%)"
- **Abstract says:** "'Low-stable' (37.2%), 'High-peaked' (21.0%), and 'Moderate-increasing' (41.8%)"
- **Problem:** The digest rounds the percentages, which is acceptable, but 41.8% rounds to 42% while 37.2% could be shown as 37%. This is a trivial rounding difference that doesn't change meaning.
- **Suggested fix:** No change required; rounding is reasonable.

**Issue B. Description of CSF biomarker** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "a cerebrospinal fluid marker of amyloid pathology"
- **Abstract says:** "a steeper reduction in CSF Aβ1-42 levels"
- **Problem:** The digest describes the CSF Aβ1-42 reduction correctly in direction and nature. No substantive error.
- **Suggested fix:** None required.

No significant issues. Framing is appropriate.

## Study 6: Resistance exercise improves executive function, working memory, and processing speed in healthy adults
**PMID:** 42508500 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study population age not specified** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "healthy adults"
- **Abstract says:** "healthy adults" (no age specification)
- **Problem:** The headline and body imply older adults given the digest context, but the abstract explicitly states "healthy adults" without age restriction. The digest doesn't misrepresent this, but the audience framing ("To older adults and seniors") may slightly overreach given the abstract doesn't confirm the meta-analysis focused on older populations.
- **Suggested fix:** Add clarification: "This meta-analysis included studies of healthy adults across various ages; applicability to older populations specifically is supported but not exclusive."

No other factual errors found. The domain-specific findings (global cognition, executive function, working memory, processing speed improved; episodic memory did not) are accurately reported.

## Study 8: A simple blood test for brain-derived tau shows promise for tracking Alzheimer's progression
**PMID:** 42677463 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Outcome variable description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "best predicted decline in everyday functioning over time"
- **Abstract says:** "best predicted Clinical Dementia Rating Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB decline)"
- **Problem:** The digest simplifies "CDR-SB decline" to "decline in everyday functioning," which is a reasonable lay translation but loses specificity. CDR-SB includes both cognitive and functional components.
- **Suggested fix:** "best predicted decline on a clinical dementia rating scale (CDR-SB) over time"

No other factual errors. The single-site limitation is appropriately noted.

## Study 9: Sociocultural diversity shapes how people experience and report subjective cognitive decline
**PMID:** 42683545 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Study type characterization** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "This expert consensus review, produced by an international collaboration of 60 researchers"
- **Abstract says:** The abstract does not specify "60 researchers" or use the term "expert consensus review." The author list shows 60 authors, but the abstract describes a review of literature rather than a formal consensus process.
- **Problem:** "60 researchers" is inferred from the author list, which is verifiable. "Expert consensus review" may slightly overstate if it was a narrative review rather than a formal Delphi or consensus process.
- **Suggested fix:** "This narrative review, produced by an international collaboration of researchers from the Alzheimer's Association ISTAART working groups"

The caveat correctly notes this is a narrative review, not a meta-analysis.
