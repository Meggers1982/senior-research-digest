# Fact-Check Report: Senior Living Research Digest (Fall Prevention)

## Study 1: Village doctors cut falls by 40% among rural older adults with heart disease
**PMID:** 42580695 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. The 40% reduction correctly reflects the OR of 0.60 (95% CI 0.43-0.84). Fall percentages (31.6% vs 42.7%), injury rates (15.9% vs 24.9%), and study details (128 villages, 12-month intervention, adults 60+) are all accurate.

## Study 2: Exergames may reduce falls in older adults, but more evidence needed
**PMID:** 42536979 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size reporting** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "pooled data from 9 randomized controlled trials (N=1,385)"
- **Abstract says:** "Nine studies (N=1385) met the inclusion criteria"
- **Problem:** Accurate, no issue here.

**Issue B. Effect size characterization** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "25% relative reduction"
- **Abstract says:** "risk ratio 0.75, 95% CI 0.61-0.92"
- **Problem:** The digest correctly interprets RR 0.75 as 25% reduction. Accurate.

**Issue C. Conflict of interest disclosure** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "one author had a prior commercial relationship with an exergame company"
- **Abstract says:** "EDB was a cofounder of Dividat, an ETH Zurich (Switzerland) spin-off company that developed an exergame platform and was associated to the company as an external advisor."
- **Problem:** "Prior commercial relationship" understates "cofounder" status. The abstract explicitly states the author was a cofounder and external advisor of an exergame company, which is a more significant relationship than implied.
- **Suggested fix:** "one author was a cofounder and external advisor of an exergame company"

## Study 5: Pickleball improves balance and social connection in pre-frail and older women
**PMID:** 42049158 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline inaccuracy** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "Pickleball improves balance and social connection in pre-frail and older women"
- **Abstract says:** Study was on "pre-frail older adults" (61 participants, median age 67); no mention of "social connection" outcomes
- **Problem:** The headline conflates two different studies. PMID 42049158 studied pre-frail adults (not specifically women) and measured inter-joint coordination, not social connection. Social connection was measured in PMID 41161302 (Study 8).
- **Suggested fix:** "Pickleball improves lower-limb coordination during dual-task walking in pre-frail older adults"

**Issue B. Outcome description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "improved lower-limb coordination during walking tasks that required simultaneous attention to movement or thinking"
- **Abstract says:** "improved inter-joint coordination" specifically during "motoric dual-task (MDT), and cognitive dual-task (CDT) walking"
- **Problem:** Accurate simplification for lay audience.
