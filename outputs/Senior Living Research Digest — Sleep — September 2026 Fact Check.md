### Study 1: Treating Insomnia May Slow Biological Aging in Older Adults
**PMID:** 42586102 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. ### Study 4: Chronic Pain Increases Frailty Risk, Partly Through Poor Sleep
**PMID:** 42423848 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Effect size magnitude** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "chronic pain (lasting at least two months) was associated with a 79% higher risk of frailty progression"
- **Abstract says:** "Chronic pain was significantly associated with frailty progression (odds ratio [OR]: 1.79; 95% confidence interval [CI]: 1.08-2.97)"
- **Problem:** An OR of 1.79 represents 79% higher odds, not 79% higher risk. For common outcomes (27.3% had frailty progression), odds ratios and risk ratios diverge. This is a minor technical distinction but worth noting for precision.
- **Suggested fix:** "chronic pain (lasting at least two months) was associated with 79% higher odds of frailty progression" or simply "was significantly associated with frailty progression (OR 1.79)"

### Study 10: Role Overload Disrupts Sleep More for Black Dementia Caregivers Than White Caregivers
**PMID:** 42121379 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Caregiver age specification** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "288 Black and non-Hispanic White family caregivers of people with dementia (aged 64+)"
- **Abstract says:** "288 Black (Mage = 59.91) and non-Hispanic White (Mage = 62.67) caregivers of persons living with dementia (>64 years old)"
- **Problem:** The age criterion (>64 years old) in the abstract refers to the care recipients (persons living with dementia), not the caregivers. The caregivers' mean ages were approximately 60-63.
- **Suggested fix:** "288 Black and non-Hispanic White family caregivers of people with dementia (care recipients aged 64+)"
