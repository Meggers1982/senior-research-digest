# Topic Memory: falls
_Last updated: 2026-08-28_

## Established findings
- Exercise + protein (≥1.2 g/kg/day) + vitamin D (≥700 IU/day) reduces sarcopenia-related falls (PMID 42208412, May 2026)
- Balance and multicomponent exercise reduces fall rates by 23–34% (high-certainty evidence, PMID 42208412)
- Dynapenic abdominal obesity elevates fall risk, especially in men with diabetes (PMID 42223007, Jun 2026)
- Frailty and malnutrition together multiply fall risk nearly 16-fold vs. robust/well-nourished (PMID 42453170, Jul 2026)
- Falls accelerate frailty progression and raise mortality (PMID 42361731, Jun 2026)
- Cumulative sensory impairment (vision + hearing) raises fall and hip fracture risk dose-dependently (PMID 42235161, Jun 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Falls resilience framework: shift from risk reduction to resilience building (PMID 42508443, Jul 2026)
- Exergaming as engaging fall prevention alternative—moderate-certainty evidence for reduced fallers at 12 months (PMID 42536979, Jul 2026)
- Psychosocial pathways: negative aging perceptions → fear of falling; social isolation → depression/cognition → falls (PMIDs 42265590, 42249766, Jun 2026)
- Sleep fragmentation linked to lateral gait instability; sleep disorders raise fall risk (PMIDs 42302301, 41825783, 2026)
- Post-anesthesia delirium predicts in-hospital falls; hypoactive type often missed (PMID 42614798, Aug 2026)
- Gait speed threshold of 1.0 m/s may capture more at-risk adults than traditional 0.8 m/s (PMID 42320833, Jun 2026)
