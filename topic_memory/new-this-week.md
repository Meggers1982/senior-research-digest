# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

## Established findings
- Short, cheap measures already available in a clinic carry real prognostic weight: five-item physiological aging index with trajectory beating snapshot (41351716), blood biomarkers predicting 15-year smell loss (41405795), VES-13 in older cancer patients (42628443), severity of fall-related fear (42628444), grip strength over muscle mass (41369831).
- Diagnosis and frailty labels, not need, keep deciding what care people get: hospice underuse in nursing home residents with dementia (41176320), shunt surgery in "complex" iNPH (42385569), anticoagulant withholding in frail AF patients (41345290), ablation withheld after stroke (42821842), unnecessary pureed/thickened diets reversed once a swallow was actually examined (42641501).
- Exercise modality, timing and load barely matter; doing something, with specificity for balance and a concrete goal, does: 42791208, 42551773, 42600321, 42456792, 42439187, and resistance training tying with all other exercise for cognition (42623933).
- Longevity supplements and "anti-aging" nutrition products keep failing: polyphenol null at the muscle-tissue level (42788414), 27-trial review finding no change in senescent-cell or telomere markers (42401265). Exceptions remain narrow (anthocyanins 41351717, one probiotic trial 42663491).
- What fails older adults after an acute event is organizational, not clinical: home palliative nursing barriers (40844247), near-absent post-delirium recovery research (42612611), stroke navigation with no benefit for anxiety or quality of life and no rural or caregiver data (42140823).
- Subjective and self-reported experience captures what instruments miss: breathlessness as social loss (42372996), fear of falling as a clinical profile (42628444).

## Emerging threads
- Mood as a physical risk factor in later life: persistent (not episodic) depression preceding frailty across four cohorts (42822543), depression clustering with frailty and osteoporosis as a high-mortality combination (42821655), pre-surgery mood burden predicting post-DBS gait recovery (42823526).
- Conversational AI as an intervention in its own right, not an accessory: automated caregiver platform matching in-person counseling effect sizes (41644333), chatbots reducing loneliness but not quality of life (42623930) — a partial counterpoint to "devices only work with a human attached" (42442508).
- Muscle quality over muscle quantity: intramuscular fat tracking strength loss over 18 years while muscle area did not (41369831), non-responders to load changes (42551773).
- Sex-specific risk patterns worth building services around: sex-divergent multimorbidity clusters (42821655), women living alone with lower multimorbidity and earlier peak risk age (41243063), greenery effect stronger in women (42805935).
- Biomarkers shifting from predicting disease to predicting treatment response: CSF tau and early lecanemab trajectory (42819539), CHIP and epigenetic age acceleration not yet clinically usable (42463064).
- Dementia subtype as a forecast: slow but highly variable early DLB decline versus Alzheimer's and mixed disease (41249712).
- Environmental and occupational exposure as a brain-aging variable: near-home greenery (42805935), 9/11 dust and cortical thinning (42805945).
