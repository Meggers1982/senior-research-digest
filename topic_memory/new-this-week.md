# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

## Established findings
- Chronological age is a poor decision variable; frailty, baseline function and disease biology do the predictive work (42987730, 42993545, 42993959, 42975712).
- Products and programs marketed for aging routinely underperform their claims: smart home tech (42992512), robots (42987450), rapamycin (42989006), fall-prevention insoles (42998665), VR/exergames (42998936), bundled "integrated" frailty programs (42995697).
- Risk scores, checklists and algorithms fail at the individual level: intrinsic capacity (42986748), sleep apnea questionnaires after 65 (42993419), deep-learning MCI prediction (42727648), dementia risk calculators in educated/affluent cohorts (42995191); brief verbal fluency screens flag dementia but cannot subtype it (42998135).
- Nutrition and eating behavior are the durable modifiable layer, and appetite/weight history outperforms lab indices: 42992391, 42982236, 42993431, 42998187, 42998707.
- Under-detection and misattribution remain the core care failure: dementia behind treatment-resistant depression (42995052), iNPH on Alzheimer's scans (42988576), cognitive decline after "minor" stroke (42998120), untreated late-life anxiety (42996801).
- Biomarker movement does not equal patient benefit — anti-tau drugs lowered CSF tau without slowing decline (42998661), echoing the prediction-vs-measurement split in AI/imaging (42727648, 42988576); diagnostic accuracy is where the field is delivering (42996798).

## Emerging threads
- Exercise structure, not exercise existence, carries the signal: instability-based resistance training (42998615) and perturbation/stumble training with schedule flexibility (42998663).
- The care setting as a treatment variable: institutional care predicting Parkinson's device-therapy discontinuation (42993214) against home-based care proving less burdensome than feared (42986685, 42691806).
- Large matched health-record cohorts (TriNetX) producing striking but hypothesis-only dementia associations (42982275, 42998120).
- Senolytics arriving on supplement shelves ahead of human data, with patent and equity conflicts attached (42995257).
- Preventive screening still below pre-pandemic baselines years later (42995348).
- Conflict-of-interest density worth flagging routinely (42998665 footwear manufacturer co-author, 42995191 commercial sponsor, 42995257, 42993214, 42988576).
