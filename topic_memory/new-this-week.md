# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

## Established findings
- Cheap measures already in the chart carry real prognostic signal: self-rated near/distance vision and IADL loss (42810001), CRP-TyG frailty trajectory (42805127), five-item physiological aging index where trajectory beat snapshot (41351716, 2026-10-01), blood biomarkers predicting 15-year smell decline (41405795, 2026-10-01).
- The label a person carries, not their need, keeps deciding what care they get: dementia and cancer-specific mortality (42810378), income gradient in meningioma radiation (42809476), lower hospice enrollment for nursing home residents with cognitive disability, with racial and sex disparities (41176320, 2026-10-01).
- Reflexive de-escalation in older or complicated patients is not supported: DOACs still net-beneficial with microbleeds and anemia (42807144), procedure-driven mortality in unruptured small aneurysms (42809132), shunt surgery in "complex" iNPH not clearly worse (42385569, 2026-10-01).
- Caregiver burden and organizational process, not clinician skill, are the modifiable levers in serious illness: burden as predictor of a good death (42804262), psychoeducation cutting Zarit burden (42811273), home palliative nursing barriers all organizational (40844247, 2026-10-01).
- Devices and supplements marketed to older adults consistently underperform without a human or a behavior change attached: neuromodulation cluster (42809063, 42808496, 42803727), trackers alone vs tracker-plus-coaching (42442508, 2026-10-01), polyphenol null at the muscle-tissue level (42788414, 2026-10-01).
- Subjective report captures what instruments miss: felt loneliness vs counted isolation (42805125), arm symptoms vs bioimpedance (42778350), the social and identity burden of breathlessness (42372996, 2026-10-01).

## Emerging threads
- Exercise research has shifted from "does it work" to "which kind, and for whom" — balance modality ranking across 423 trials (42600321), mind-body exercise ranking (42439187), null results for training time of day (42791208) and load intensity (42551773), safe supervised barbell work in older women (42486377), step targets outperforming vague advice (42456792).
- Environmental and occupational exposure as a brain-aging variable: near-home greenery (42805935), 9/11 dust and cortical thinning (42805945), air pollution and stroke (42809522).
- Ancestry-specific failure of standard risk tools: APOE ε4 not predictive in Hispanic/Latino adults, alternative clusters outperforming (42805940).
- Sleep timing and hypnotic choice as distinct, separable risks: circadian misalignment in insulin resistance (42424773), drug- and dose-specific nocturnal balance impairment (41946519).
- Incidental brain aneurysms as a routine older-adult decision point — rising prevalence (42679840) plus treat-vs-watch mortality numbers (42809132).
- Aging with well-treated HIV may not follow the accelerated-immune-aging script (42775653).
