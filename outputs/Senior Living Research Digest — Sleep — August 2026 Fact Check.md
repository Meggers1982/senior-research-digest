# Fact-Check Report

### Study 1: Treating Insomnia May Slow Biological Aging, Landmark Trial Suggests
**PMID:** 42586102 | **Verdict:** ✅ Accurate

No factual errors found. Framing is consistent with the abstract. The digest correctly reports this as a secondary analysis of an RCT, accurately describes the sample (N=92, adults 60+), the comparison groups (CBT-I vs. sleep education therapy), the follow-up period (20–39 months), the primary finding (slower pace of biological aging measured by DunedinPACE in CBT-I group), and notes remission rates. Caveats appropriately flag single-center and demographic limitations.

### Study 2: Short Sleep and Poor Sleep Quality Linked to Faster Physical and Mental Decline
**PMID:** 42623973 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Slight imprecision in risk statistic** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "up to 52% higher risk of developing 'physical, psychological, and cognitive multimorbidity'"
- **Abstract says:** "HR = 1.52, 95% CI: 1.37-1.70" for sleeping <5 h/night; "HR = 1.47, 95% CI: 1.32-1.62" for restless sleep 5-7 d/week
- **Problem:** The "52% higher risk" accurately reflects the HR of 1.52, but this was specifically for <5 hours sleep, not a general "up to" figure combining sleep duration and quality. The framing is acceptable but slightly imprecise.
- **Suggested fix:** "Those who slept fewer than 5 hours per night had 52% higher risk, and those with restless sleep most nights had 47% higher risk..."

### Study 12: Sleep Education as Low as It Gets? Sleep Disturbances Widespread Among Black Dementia Caregivers
**PMID:** 42121379 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Headline accuracy** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** Headline "Sleep Education as Low as It Gets?" 
- **Abstract says:** Study examined caregiving stressors and sleep outcomes; "sleep education" is not a topic of this study
- **Problem:** The headline appears to be a play on words that doesn't clearly relate to the study content. The study is about role overload and sleep disturbance, not sleep education.
- **Suggested fix:** Revise headline to reflect actual study content, e.g., "Role Overload Hits Black Dementia Caregivers' Sleep Harder"

**Issue B. Sample composition** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "288 family caregivers of people with dementia (half Black, half non-Hispanic White)"
- **Abstract says:** "288 Black...and non-Hispanic White...caregivers" but doesn't specify exact split
- **Problem:** "Half" may not be precisely accurate; abstract doesn't confirm exact proportions.
- **Suggested fix:** "288 Black and non-Hispanic White family caregivers..."
