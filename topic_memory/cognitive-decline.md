# Topic Memory: cognitive decline
_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

## Established findings
- Resistance training improves executive function, working memory and processing speed, but not episodic memory (PMID 42508500; recurred Aug, Sep 2026 and Sep 19 2026)
- Multidomain lifestyle programs work when they address capability, opportunity and motivation together, run 6+ months and include social support and practical enablement; cognitive benefit itself remains low certainty (PMID 42586247; recurred across three runs)
- Sleep disturbance trajectories over years — not single snapshots — predict Alzheimer's risk (PMID 42542107; recurred Sep 2026 twice); circadian/metabolic/mood clustering tracks with subjective decline (PMID 42567211)
- Medicare Annual Wellness Visits surface more MCI diagnoses but have not shown earlier dementia diagnosis or slower decline (PMID 42456217; recurred Sep 2026 twice)
- Plasma p-tau217 predicts progression in cognitively unimpaired adults, now with absolute 5-year risk estimates (~38% in very high group) and stronger effect in APOE-ε4 carriers (PMIDs 42449500, 42716078; earlier assay work 42677463)
- Neighborhood environment matters for late-life mood and cognition: mature tree canopy and nearby amenities linked to fewer depressive symptoms; relocation itself is a stressor (PMID 42442669, recurred)

## Emerging threads
- Cheap tests hold up against expensive ones: cued recall staging (PMID 42561582) and short self-administered digital batteries (PMID 42741980) add prediction beyond p-tau217 and PET
- Beyond amyloid: hippocampal asymmetry predicts decline in amyloid-negative people (PMID 42407430); automated MRI marker for the LATE mimic (PMID 42745216); cholinergic degeneration index for individualized prognosis (PMID 42756499)
- Perioperative brain health consolidating as a distinct thread — delirium as "acute brain failure" (PMID 42756469), post-cardiac-surgery dementia risk (PMID 42740336), earlier preoperative sleepiness finding (PMID 42668427)
- Basic modifiable risk factors go unmanaged even in patients pursuing anti-amyloid drugs (PMID 42430964) — a care-gap storyline for residential settings
- Culture, sex and place shape self-reported cognition: Hispanic/Latino sex differences moderated by cardiovascular risk and acculturation (PMID 42424748); neighborhood moderates the cost of family isolation for Black older adults (PMID 42755056); earlier diversity consensus (PMID 42683545)
- Care setting as a variable: institutional care linked to better daily function, mediated partly by neuropsychiatric symptom management, not social support (PMID 42758043) — needs replication outside China
- Vascular route to dementia gaining mechanistic detail (lacunar stroke metabolites, PMID 42721413)
