## Study 1: More Than Half of Hospitalized Older Adults and Nearly Half of Nursing Home Residents Need Palliative Care — Most Don't Have Cancer
**PMID:** 42366281 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Cancer prevalence in hospitals slightly rounded** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Prevalence was highest among those with cancer (77% in hospitals)"
- **Abstract says:** "Prevalence was higher among patients with cancer (77.2% in hospital; 61.5% in LTCF)"
- **Problem:** Minor rounding (77.2% → 77%) is acceptable, but the digest omits the LTCF cancer figure (61.5%) which provides fuller context.
- **Suggested fix:** "Prevalence was highest among those with cancer (77.2% in hospitals, 61.5% in nursing homes)"

## Study 8: Living Alone and Dementia Linked to Unmet Social, Emotional, and Spiritual Needs at End of Life
**PMID:** 42407095 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Age range** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "N=507 deceased adults aged 55+"
- **Abstract says:** "deceased participants aged 55 years and older" and "Individuals died at a mean age of 83 years (range 55-105)"
- **Problem:** No error—accurately stated.

**Issue B. Percentages for needs met** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Most individuals had these needs met (74–82%)"
- **Abstract says:** "Social and psychological needs were met for most individuals (81.8% and 77.8%); among those with spiritual needs (n = 227), 74.4% had these needs met"
- **Problem:** The range "74–82%" is a reasonable summary of 74.4%, 77.8%, and 81.8%. Accurate.

No significant factual errors found.

## Study 12: Polypharmacy Persists Even in Late-Stage Palliative Care — Potentially Inappropriate Medications Remain Common
**PMID:** 41950773 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "294 hospitalized older adults with palliative care needs"
- **Abstract says:** "Of 558 patients initially screened, 294 were administered the NECPAL tool, of whom 254 (45.5% of the sample screened) had PC needs"
- **Problem:** The digest implies all 294 had palliative care needs, but only 254 of the 294 screened positive. The polypharmacy and PIM statistics appear to apply to the 294 administered the tool, not specifically the 254 with PC needs.
- **Suggested fix:** "In a Ligurian (Italy) point-prevalence study, 294 hospitalized older adults were screened using the NECPAL tool, with 254 identified as having palliative care needs. Among those screened, 93.9% were taking five or more medications..."
