# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

## Established findings
- Marketed aging products underperform their claims across categories: polyphenol supplements (42983146), cola and bone health (42980778), rapamycin with no survival benefit over a median 11 years (42989006), untested "hallmarks of aging" biomarkers (42980902), smart home dementia tech with zero effect on caregiver burden (42992512), robots that helped loneliness but cut daily steps (42987450).
- Exercise, nutrition and metabolic control are the durable active ingredients: exercise+nutrition for sarcopenia (42980314), insulin resistance as the one consistent muscle-loss marker (42980902), GNRI and cognitive decline (42992391), diabetes/sleep/diet explaining ~19% of MCI (42991179), activity protective against worsening frailty (42986749).
- Frailty is dynamic, not a verdict: 75.9% improved post-LVAD (42981223), 18.3% improved within one year in a community cohort (42986749) — both argue for repeat measurement over intake-only scoring.
- Evidence is routinely built without the people who will rely on it: healthy ASPREE enrollees (42982348), transplant recipients standing in for healthy agers (42989006), trials enrolling from age 18 (42987453), volunteer cohorts in the oldest old (42986748).
- Structured, facilitated, non-drug programs beat self-directed or device-based substitutes: CBT in Parkinson's (42983118), group reminiscence vs a workbook (42987468).
- Under-detection and misattribution remain the core care failure: Parkinson's first-visit sensitivity (42759118), iNPH mistaken for aging (41604771), unscreened mistreatment (42981484).

## Emerging threads
- Risk scores and categories miss people and overreach at the individual level: 29% of "robust" adults fell (42982004), sensory/nutrition/mobility beyond DXA (42980776), intrinsic capacity useful as a population marker only (42986748).
- Screening and check-up programs for the oldest old are promoted but unproven (42691802); POCUS evidence sits almost entirely in acute hospitals, not where older adults live (42987892).
- Education delays dementia onset but does not soften its course — more life-years lost among the highly educated (42989503).
- Care moved into the home is proving less burdensome than feared when communication is strong (42986685, 42691806), while home technology has not shown burden relief (42992512).
- Recruitment and participation as bottlenecks: only 24.4% willing to join a prevention trial, with the study-partner requirement a sticking point (42992342).
- Social participation sustained by financial security and a confidant rather than co-residence (42988767).
- Conflict-of-interest density worth flagging routinely (42989006, 42987892, 42980777, 42981223).
