## Study 5: Home-Based Vision and Hearing Support Helps People with Dementia—But Requires Caregiver Involvement
**PMID:** 42212629 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Trial description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "In a European clinical trial, 124 people with dementia..."
- **Abstract says:** "The trial enrolled 252 dyads (PwD + study partner). In the active arm (n = 124)..."
- **Problem:** The digest correctly identifies 124 in the active arm but doesn't clarify this was the intervention group within a larger trial of 252 dyads.
- **Suggested fix:** "In a European clinical trial of 252 participant pairs (person with dementia plus care partner), 124 people with dementia in the intervention group received..."

**Issue B. Hearing aid prescription rate denominator** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Most participants with hearing loss (81%) were prescribed hearing aids"
- **Abstract says:** "Of 124 assessed for hearing loss, 101 (81%) were prescribed hearing aids"
- **Problem:** The abstract states 124 were *assessed* for hearing loss, not that 124 *had* hearing loss. The denominator is those assessed.
- **Suggested fix:** "Of those assessed for hearing loss, 81% were prescribed hearing aids"

## Study 9: Having Multiple Age-Related Eye Diseases Dramatically Raises the Risk of Severe Vision Loss
**PMID:** 41249002 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Sample size precision** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "over 500,000 South Korean adults"
- **Abstract says:** "Among 0.5 million participants"
- **Problem:** "0.5 million" is exactly 500,000, not "over" 500,000. This is a minor overstatement.
- **Suggested fix:** "Using data from 500,000 South Korean adults..."

**Issue B. Risk increase description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "having two or more of these conditions nearly doubled the risk"
- **Abstract says:** "the risk of severe VI (aHR 4.96; 95% CI 4.33 to 5.68) increased 1.93-fold with a second AROD (aHR 9.54)"
- **Problem:** The 1.93-fold increase refers to the increase *from* having one AROD *to* having two, not doubling from baseline. The framing is slightly imprecise but the direction is correct.
- **Suggested fix:** "having a second condition increased the risk nearly twofold compared to having just one"

## Study 12: New Therapy Restores Eye Shape and Vision in Chronic Low Eye Pressure
**PMID:** 41521029 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Adverse event description** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Two adverse events occurred in eyes with pre-existing severe thinning."
- **Abstract says:** "Two adverse events occurred: uveitis flare-ups leading to loss of HPMC clarity in two eyes... and two cases of temporary vision loss post-injection"
- **Problem:** The abstract describes four adverse events (two uveitis flare-ups, two temporary vision losses), not two. The abstract does not specify these occurred in eyes with "severe thinning."
- **Suggested fix:** "Four adverse events occurred: two uveitis flare-ups and two cases of temporary vision loss post-injection, which resolved with treatment."
