# Topic Memory: cardiovascular disease
_Last updated: 2026-09-12_

## Established findings
- Statins: no clear cognitive harm, and discontinuation after 75 may be safe in primary prevention — while secondary prevention evidence pushes toward deeper LDL reduction (PMIDs 42010230, 42580354, 42623575; Apr 2026–Sep 2026)
- Cardiovascular and cardiometabolic disease predict frailty, disability and function loss, not just death; CVD's share of health burden in the 80+ population is falling as disability's share rises (PMIDs 42334801, 42400980, 42594758; Jun–Sep 2026)
- Chronic inflammation is a shared driver of cardiovascular and cognitive aging, and is measurably reducible by structured lifestyle programs (PMIDs 42425421, 42387181, 42687462; Jul–Sep 2026)
- Heart and mind are bidirectionally linked; combined physical-plus-mental multimorbidity carries the steepest mortality risk (PMIDs 42628846, 42475970, 42567210; Aug–Sep 2026)
- Cardiometabolic clusters (metabolic syndrome, visceral fat, waist-to-height) outperform single frailty markers such as grip strength for predicting vascular events (PMIDs 42501509, 42447644, 42607606; Sep 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Lipoprotein(a) moving from untreatable trait to actionable target: aspirin signal in high-Lp(a) aortic stenosis, plus a single-dose siRNA holding 96-97% reduction at 48 weeks (PMIDs 41721439, 42664979; Sep 2026)
- Circadian and bedroom-environment exposures as cardiovascular risk: nighttime light linked to cardiac remodeling and events; sleep regularity linked to blood pressure; CPAP benefit unsettled in non-sleepy apnea (PMIDs 42715935, 42526099, 42349065; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Externally validated heart failure risk prediction entering practice (SCORE2-HF, SMART2-HF), answering the earlier critique that ML/risk models lacked validation (PMIDs 41810943, 41810961 vs 41941969; Sep 2026)
- Sex-specific lifelong cardiovascular history — pregnancy complications and PCOS/PMOS as durable, routinely unrecorded risk flags in older women (PMIDs 42669304, 42727321; Sep 2026)
- Unused cardiovascular information already in the chart: liver index scores and retinal imaging as candidate risk signals (PMIDs 42702521, 42551563; Sep–Dec 2026)
- GLP-1 mechanism claims unsettled — endothelial function is not a clean explanation for proven outcome benefits (PMID 42679726 vs 42608657; Aug–Oct 2026)
- Care-coordination failures in cardiac care: cardiac self-management deprioritized during cancer treatment; unscreened dysphagia in cardiac rehab (PMIDs 42049274, 41716004; Aug–Sep 2026)
