# Topic Memory: cognitive decline
_Last updated: 2026-08-08_

## Established findings
- Elevated resting heart rate (≥80 bpm) linked to faster cognitive decline (ASPREE; PMID 42329536, June 2026)
- Moderate-to-vigorous exercise (≥150 min/week) buffers amyloid-related decline (PMID 42185590, May 2026)
- Combined physical-cognitive interventions (Zumba + games, resistance training) improve cognition; attendance matters (PMIDs 42398018, 42508500)
- Multidomain decision-support care stabilizes cognition in 85% of memory-concern patients (PMID 42393905)
- Persistent/worsening loneliness accelerates cognitive decline; intermittent confidants more protective than daily contacts (PMIDs 42480060, 42478745)
- Hearing loss is modifiable dementia risk; screening and aids recommended (PMID 42252380)

## Emerging threads
- Plant-rich keto + intermittent fasting shows early RCT promise in Alzheimer's (PMID 42497811)
- Bilingualism slows episodic memory decline, effects vary by ethnicity (PMID 42500984)
- Metabolomic aging clock predicts MCI risk; nearing clinical translation (PMID 42481860)
- Serious video games feasible for home cognitive monitoring (PMID 42477234)
- Muscle mitochondrial function linked to cognition in men, not women (PMID 42479929)
