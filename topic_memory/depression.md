# Topic Memory: depression
_Last updated: 2026-08-02_

## Established findings
- Music therapy (individual or group) reduces depression in cognitively healthy older adults (PMID 42161569, May 2026)
- Anti-inflammatory diet associated with 8-21% lower depression risk in adults 70+ (PMID 42154116, May 2026)
- Missing personally important social activities dramatically increases caregiver depression (PMID 42249768, Jun 2026)
- Elder abuse erodes trust, worsening depression; social support buffers both pathways (PMID 42289040, Jun 2026)
- Preference for solitude predicts smaller networks and worse mental health over time (PMID 42231145, Jun 2026)
- Depression partially mediates socioeconomic disadvantage → subjective cognitive decline (PMID 42289977, Jul 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Pet therapy meta-evidence now strong (SMD=0.73; PMID 42400739)—ready for implementation guidance
- Legacy/ethical will writing groups show promise for depression (PMID 42472454)—needs replication
- Problematic smartphone use affecting 27% of older adults; depression is risk factor (PMID 42508009)
- Childhood adversity shapes depression networks decades later (PMID 42366693)—trauma-informed care implications
