# Topic Memory: hearing loss
_Last updated: 2026-08-23_

## Established findings
- Hearing loss consistently associated with depression across multiple countries (PMIDs 42119796, 42226155; replicated August 2026)
- Hearing aid use linked to reduced frailty risk (PMID 42423142; replicated August 2026)
- Flavonoid-rich diet associated with lower incident hearing loss (PMID 42235109; replicated August 2026)
- Hearing loss as marker of accelerated biological aging tied to dementia, CVD, diabetes (PMID 42419408; replicated August 2026)
- Hearing loss identified as global modifiable dementia risk factor (PMID 42437564; replicated August 2026)
- Public awareness of hearing-dementia link remains low (PMID 41966599; replicated August 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Hearing loss part of modifiable Parkinson's disease risk clusters (PMID 42581680)
- People with dementia can benefit from hearing aids with appropriate support (SENSE-Cog, PMID 42212629)
- Integrated hearing-cognition screening tools validated (PMID 42630084)
- Central auditory processing and executive function predict communication beyond audibility (PMIDs 42463613, 41525395)
