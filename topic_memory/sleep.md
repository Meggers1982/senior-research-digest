# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

## Established findings
- U-shaped/inverted-U sleep duration risk in older adults: very short and especially very long sleep track with mortality, weaker cognition, persistent depression, and reduced kidney function (PMIDs 42648048, 42690762, 42755049, 42628578, 42686460; Sept 2026 and earlier 42241923)
- Poor sleep and depressed mood are tightly coupled across community, home care, institutional, and cognitively impaired populations; sleep is the residual symptom most likely to persist when depression is treated (PMIDs 42665164, 42628578, 42648548, 42727201, 42711172)
- Sleep and circadian regulation belong in pre-surgery risk assessment for older adults: composite burden index and objectively measured presurgical sleep both predict delirium and worse recovery (PMIDs 42600483, 42665464)
- Prescribing cascades are common: ~10% of new cholinesterase inhibitor users are later given a sleep agent, a class that raises fall/fracture/confusion risk (PMID 42727946)
- Mechanistic evidence ties disrupted sleep to brain clearance, glial activation and Alzheimer's pathology, bidirectionally, but no interventional proof that fixing sleep changes disease course (PMIDs 42780441, 42798625, 42708477)

## Emerging threads
- Non-drug insomnia care now has dosing data: ~6 weeks of thrice-weekly acupuncture as the point of diminishing returns, 8-week MBCT effective but fading by 3 months, 12-week ashwagandha RCT positive but small (PMIDs 42697250, 42648548, 42742071)
- Patient and carer preferences favor avoiding side effects over maximizing effectiveness — deprescribing may meet less resistance than clinicians assume (PMID 42746961)
- Napping remains contested: no mortality or couples-cognition signal (42648048, 42755049) vs slower processing speed and lower cognition elsewhere (42596580, 42690762)
- Menopause as the origin point of long-running sleep disruption, largely undiscussed in clinical visits; cooler bedrooms supported, broader heat-vulnerability claims not (PMIDs 42770459, 42742071, 42772086)
- Sleep/non-motor symptom patterns as early clues to dementia subtype, with Lewy body disease showing the heaviest burden (PMID 42768715)
- Life events and sleep: bereavement after a child's death registers in sleep, cognition and self-rated health (PMID 42791223)
- Night-shift care staff as an occupational health question, with brain and metabolic correlates of low mood (PMID 42624217)
- Even highly motivated dementia patients meet almost none of the 15 modifiable brain health targets, and key labs are missing from charts (PMID 42430964)

## Feature ideas already pitched
- "Sleep as Anti-Aging Medicine" (Aug 2026): CBT-I, CPAP-telomere, glymphatic research as longevity medicine
- "The Gateway Target" (Sept 1 2026): sleep as modifiable mediator connecting pain, falls, cognition
- "Long sleep as an under-read vital sign" (Sept 15 2026): 9-10+ hours and heavy napping as signals to investigate
- "The non-drug insomnia menu, with numbers" (Sept 29 2026): acupuncture/MBCT/ashwagandha dosing and duration plus patient preference for avoiding side effects
