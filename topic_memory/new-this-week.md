# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-30_

## Established findings
- Under-detection and misattribution to "normal aging" remain the dominant care failure: autoimmune encephalitis mistaken for dementia (42951877), depression read as Parkinson's progression (42949295), fluctuating alertness in advanced DLB read as inconsistency (42953268, 2026-10-30), decades-old head injury left off the chart (42958736), hearing loss filed as cosmetic (41916749).
- Cheap functional measures repeatedly outpredict expensive ones: osteosarcopenia doubling mortality (41818462), grip and bone risk (42946838), intrinsic capacity index predicting death/dementia/disability fourfold (42955660), clinical fall-prediction models beating imaging and fluid biomarkers in Parkinson's (42960478), discharge physical function predicting six-month life-space after stroke (42957010).
- Low-tech, system-level fixes pay for themselves: vaccination workflow changes (42947551), home-based rehab matching inpatient (42945046), post-discharge nurse calls (42946540), zoledronate at every risk level (41968301), fracture liaison services (42955080).
- Appealing self-directed or technology-based interventions often fail clean tests: food aroma (42933112), probiotics with iron (42933603), daily low-dose aspirin for capacity (42955660), exergaming vs a conventional class (42954170), smartphone sway for fall prediction (42956683), internet frequency and mood in frail elders (42954453). Industry/developer authorship is common and worth flagging.
- Policy and service delivery run ahead of evaluation and infrastructure: aging-in-place reforms unmeasured (42933452), rural palliative care with two RCTs since 2000 (42951088), caregiver financial support with no financial outcomes (41371759), anti-amyloid drugs approved into systems reaching <10% of patients (42953259).
- Depression is a modifiable driver of outcomes usually treated as purely physical or degenerative: Parkinson's cognition (42949295), Parkinson's falls (42960478).

## Emerging threads
- Follow-up horizons are too short: hip fracture decline peaking in years 2-3 (42956453), fracture liaison benefit only appearing after year 1 (42955080), community re-entry after minor stroke (42957010).
- Muscle and strength as the unrecorded vital sign, now including GLP-1 lean mass loss in the 70s and 80s (41818462, 42946838, 42950710, 42952307).
- Frailty as a financial risk factor, not only a clinical one (42950708) — pairs with caregiver financial burden (41371759).
- Delivery format as an active ingredient: group beating individual delivery for cognitive outcomes (42954294).
- Measurement inconsistency deciding real-world eligibility: MRI sequence shifting microbleed risk category in 38% of patients (42959393); two DLB fluctuation scales barely correlating (42953268).
- Cultural and social participation acquiring a biological rationale (42957065); retirement increasing help given to others (42956395); older drivers self-regulating effectively (42958638) — a counter-ageism cluster worth watching.
- Non-attendance at diagnostic appointments proposed as a neglect indicator (42954618) — very small sample, watch for replication outside Turkey.
