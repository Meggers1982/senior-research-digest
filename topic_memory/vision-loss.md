## Topic Memory: vision loss
_Last updated: 2026-11-02_

## Established findings
- Vision loss is a modifiable dementia risk factor that clusters with cardiometabolic, mood and social risks; cumulative burden tracks with worse cognition and MRI changes including hippocampal atrophy (PMIDs 42645764, 42371784, 42716809; recurring Aug–Nov 2026)
- Near vision impairment — the cheapest to correct — is the association that survives full adjustment, while distance vision fades out (PMID 42716809, recurring); MMSE scores can be distorted by uncorrected reading vision
- Low-contrast obstacles reduce foot clearance in all older walkers, not just those with diagnosed vision loss; contrast marking belongs in fall-prevention audits (PMID 42501620 — recurring)
- Pausing anti-VEGF treatment when the eye looks dry is not the end of monitoring: ~50% of regressed polypoidal eyes recur within two years, some with 10+ letter loss (PMID 42752539 — recurring)
- Baseline outer retinal volume on routine OCT independently predicts two-year diabetic retinopathy progression; inner retinal volume does not (PMID 42752554 — recurring)
- Two or more age-related eye diseases compound the risk of severe visual impairment far beyond any single diagnosis (PMID 41249002 — last seen Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Medication- and treatment-induced eye damage in older adults, now with two data points: tamoxifen retinal toxicity with near-zero monitoring (PMID 42955022) and ocular surface/corneal nerve damage in ~86% on HER2-targeted ADCs (PMID 42331364) — watch for a third drug class or any guideline movement
- Emotional and social dimensions of vision loss: loneliness in AMD follows distinct pathways with depression overriding protective factors (PMID 42847076); anti-VEGF patients cycle between hope and dread (PMID 42008767)
- Function beyond the eye chart: dim-light (scotopic) sensitivity declines before standard measures in a dry-AMD-like dystrophy (PMID 42734305), alongside the contrast/gait work (PMID 42501620)
- Supplement evidence base stays thin — AREDS only, in selected AMD patients; NAD+ boosters, citicoline, pyruvate unproven (PMID 42936152)
- How glaucoma is treated, not just whether, may track with dementia risk (PMID 42693831) — confounding by indication unresolved
- Lens-sparing angle-based combined surgery cuts drop burden roughly two-thirds in advanced angle-closure glaucoma (PMID 42833871) — single-country, no control arm
- Long-horizon survivorship: 20-year vision outcomes after plaque brachytherapy for choroidal melanoma (PMID 42874810); new PRKCZ gene in adult-onset retinitis pigmentosa with hearing loss (PMID 42955015)
- Dropped from active watch unless new data appear: GBD analyses of women's blindness burden (42521465) and diabetic retinopathy trends (42476825), audiobooks (42500929), VEXAS (41535098), hypotony gel (41521029), American Indian dementia risk hierarchy (42635108)
