# Topic Memory: New this week
_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

## Established findings
- Under-detection and misattribution to "normal aging" remain the dominant care failure: apathy misread as personality (42949266), late-onset muscle disease (42934973), autoimmune encephalitis mistaken for dementia with ninefold worse outcomes after 30-day delay in atypical cases (42951877, 2026-10-29), depression mistaken for Parkinson's progression (42949295, 2026-10-29).
- Age alone is a weak basis for withholding chronic treatment: anticoagulation after 80 (42946923), SGLT2 inhibitors in 75+ (42937517), restraint minimization in ICU (42944623). Exception holds for acute catastrophic events (42946922).
- Low-tech, system-level fixes repeatedly pay for themselves: stacked vaccination workflow changes (42947551), home-based rehabilitation matching inpatient outcomes (42945046), one nurse making post-discharge calls freeing 55.5 bed-days (42946540, 2026-10-29), zoledronate fracture prevention netting savings at every risk level (41968301, 2026-10-29).
- Biomarkers and risk scores work at group level, stall at the individual: dementia calculators (42934475), p-tau217 with 8% category shift (42947225), neuromelanin MRI without clinical threshold (42949087), CHIP null for bone density (42949023, 2026-10-29). Industry authorship is common and worth flagging.
- Popular intuitive interventions often fail clean tests — food aroma and intake (42933112), probiotics with iron (42933603) — though a chatbot cognitive training trial with imaging endpoints bucked this (42950244, 2026-10-29), with a no-treatment control and a commercial affiliation.
- Policy and service delivery run far ahead of evaluation: aging-in-place reforms unmeasured (42933452), rural palliative care with only two RCTs since 2000 (42951088, 2026-10-29), caregiver financial support with zero studies measuring caregiver finances (41371759, 2026-10-29).

## Emerging threads
- Muscle strength as the unrecorded vital sign: osteosarcopenia roughly doubling mortality (41818462), grip predicting bone risk category (42946838), strength-first vs memory-first decline patterns (42950710), post-surgical acute wasting (42946212), GLP-1 lean mass loss in older adults (42952307).
- GLP-1 and dual incretin drugs reaching the 70s and 80s without age-stratified trial evidence — monitoring of function, protein and resistance training as the proposed counterweight (42952307).
- Fracture risk tools underestimating specific histories: FRAX correction multipliers reclassifying 13.3% of people with vertebral or multiple prior fractures (41810867).
- Mental speed as the early signal, distinct from memory (42946888, 42948203, 42946145) — watch for follow-up.
- Sedating-drug stacking in long-term care: 52% of nursing home opioid users also on a potentiator, with possible undertreatment of pain in dementia (42951674).
- First randomized evidence in elder abuse and self-neglect (42951664) — watch for replication outside Ontario/Maine.
- Facility-level variation swamping national averages in economic models of geriatric programs (42946540).
