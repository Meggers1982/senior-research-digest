# Topic Memory: palliative care
_Last updated: 2026-09-16_

## Established findings
- Palliative referrals arrive far too late across systems: 4 days from consult to death/discharge for 90+ (PMID 42580255, Aug 2026); median 23 days from referral to death with 95% already metastatic (PMID 42713708, Sept 2026)
- Palliative needs extend well beyond cancer and are common in hospitals and nursing homes (PMID 42366281, June 2026); clinicians recognize frailty more confidently than the palliative need inside it (PMID 42700391, Sept 2026)
- Family caregivers carry measurable, routinely unassessed distress; unmet information and practical-help needs track with anxiety and depression (PMID 42728769, Sept 2026; PMID 42697036, Sept 2026; PMID 42407095, July 2026)
- Advance care planning reduces unwanted aggressive care, but only specific written instructions (DNR/do-not-hospitalize) track with fewer hospital transfers — and are sometimes overridden (PMID 42714187, Oct 2026; PMID 42085825, April 2026)
- Simple bedside measures of muscle, weight and function predict survival better than intuition: calf circumference plus albumin/CRP (PMID 42674364, Aug 2026); cachexia prevalence (PMID 42674857, Aug 2026); admission function (PMID 42660629, Aug 2026)
- Home-based and non-physician delivery models (NP, pharmacy technician, community health worker) surface unmet needs and reduce acute use in small studies (PMID 42343547, June 2026; PMID 42730948, Sept 2026; PMID 42681826, Sept 2026)

## Emerging threads
- AI entering prognosis and empathic communication: patients preferred AI-written empathy 72.4% of the time (PMID 42599269, Oct 2026); physicians favorable toward AI prognostication despite low AI literacy, including for hospice eligibility (PMID 42463061, Oct 2026)
- Relationships, not protocols, drive program performance — trust and proximity between palliative and oncology teams separated high from low hospice use (PMID 42734951, Sept 2026)
- Quality metrics miss what staff and families value from consultations, including staff morale and moral distress (PMID 42742117, Sept 2026)
- Stigma as a modifiable symptom driver: cirrhosis shame linked to worse distress and symptoms (PMID 42362166, Oct 2026); routine urine drug testing carries stigma without proven benefit (PMID 42362165, Oct 2026)
- Deprescribing near end of life remains reactive rather than planned (PMID 42519959, Oct 2026)
- Plausible interventions failing rigorous testing — null lidocaine trial for opioid-refractory pain (PMID 42536632, Oct 2026)
- Assisted dying requests as long-developing, control-driven deliberation rather than crisis response (PMID 42744336, Sept 2026)
- Shifting recurring procedures out of the hospital (outpatient paracentesis clinic, PMID 42722989, Sept 2026)
