# Topic Memory: sleep
_Last updated: 2026-10-27_

## Established findings
- U-shaped sleep duration risk holds across cohorts: very short and especially 9+ hours track with mortality and weaker cognition; napping repeatedly shows no signal (PMIDs 42648048, 42755049, 42821756)
- Sleep and daytime movement travel together, and activity appears to buffer some of the biological cost of poor sleep; activity's protective link to continence runs partly through sleep (PMIDs 42891778, 42748569, 42821756)
- Presurgical sleep — efficiency, rest-activity rhythm, apnea severity, or bundled into a multi-domain vulnerability index — predicts postoperative delirium and recovery (PMIDs 42665464, 42600483)
- Disrupted sleep is biologically entangled with Alzheimer's pathology via amyloid trajectory, vascular burden, APOE ε4, and now neurovascular coupling; still no interventional proof that fixing sleep changes disease course (PMIDs 42851166, 42857391, 42861684, 42742525)
- Sedative prescribing is risky and demonstrably changeable: benzodiazepines after hip fracture fell 33.7%→13.3% over 16 years while opioids stayed near 45%; older adults themselves cite side-effect fear as their top concern (PMIDs 42852585, 42746961)
- Sleep and mood are tightly coupled in later life, and sleep is often the residual symptom after depression is treated (PMIDs 42648548, 42850158, 42800142, 42790647)

## Emerging threads
- REM sleep behavior disorder as the gateway prodrome: dream enactment plus late-onset depression, smell loss and autonomic signs predicts Parkinson's/Lewy body disease, and diet and gut bacteria now track conversion risk (PMIDs 42907235, 42888868, 42768715, 42840315)
- Delivery and access, not efficacy, are the live question for non-drug insomnia care: BBTI sustained and grew in VA primary care after support withdrawal; patient demand exists but local supply does not (PMIDs 42920076, 42746961)
- Apnea treatment broadening beyond CPAP — triple-agonist injection cut events ~half in phase 3 — alongside a detection gap (half of fatigued Long COVID patients had undiagnosed apnea) (PMIDs 42904878, 42897409)
- Corrective thread: sleep is not the universal mediator. Null or modest sleep mediation in pain→depression (~9%), grip asymmetry→cardiometabolic risk, and apnea→Long COVID cognition (PMIDs 42749164, 42753932, 42897409)
- Circadian biology enters the frailty conversation: morning chronotype linked to less insomnia, and reduced CLOCK expression independently tied to pre-frailty (PMID 42877636)
- Environment and sleep as a route to brain aging: pollution-to-cognition pathways run partly through sleep, mood and vascular health (PMIDs 42632461, 42860785)
- Menopause: sleep quality is the dominant lifestyle correlate of hot flash burden, and digital programs offer modest, very-low-certainty sleep benefit where care access is thin (PMIDs 42899800, 42822207, 42832800)
