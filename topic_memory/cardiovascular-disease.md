# Topic Memory: cardiovascular disease
_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

## Established findings
- Heart and mind are bidirectionally linked: combined physical-plus-mental multimorbidity carries the steepest mortality risk, depression independently raises cardiovascular risk in diabetes beyond glycemic control, and heart disease tracks with faster cognitive decline (PMIDs 42475970, 42624218, 42767983, 42567210; Aug–Dec 2026)
- Cardiovascular disease predicts frailty, disability and function loss, not just death; CVD's share of health burden in the 80+ population is falling (42.3%→28.6%) as disability's share rises (PMIDs 42400980, 42594758, 42612584; Jun–Nov 2026)
- Cardiometabolic clusters (metabolic syndrome, visceral adiposity, waist-to-height, insulin resistance plus inflammation) outperform single frailty markers such as grip strength for predicting vascular events (PMIDs 42501509, 42447644, 42607606, 42392540; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Secondary-prevention lipid evidence keeps pushing toward deeper LDL reduction, now formalized in the 2026 VA/DoD guideline with combination therapy and a one-time Lp(a) test (PMIDs 42623575, 42735406; Sep 2026)
- Supplements marketed for brain and vascular protection do not hold up: null randomized biomarker results for multivitamin, cocoa extract, vitamin D and fish oil; safety questions for L-arginine in advanced CVD (PMIDs 42775737, 42746141, 42534924; Jul–Sep 2026)
- Dysphagia is common and under-detected in older cardiac patients; risk does not follow admission diagnosis, supporting universal bedside screening (PMIDs 41716004, 42570796; Sep–Oct 2026)

## Emerging threads
- Peripheral arterial disease as the neglected vascular bed — late diagnosis, 30–50% five-year mortality, symptoms misattributed to aging (PMID 42772316; Sep 2026)
- Fear of movement (kinesiophobia) as a treatable barrier to cardiac rehabilitation participation, distinct from apathy or frailty (PMID 42762436; Sep 2026)
- Lipoprotein(a) moving from untreatable trait to actionable target: aspirin signal in high-Lp(a) aortic stenosis, single-dose siRNA holding 96–97% reduction at 48 weeks, now a guideline-endorsed one-time test (PMIDs 41721439, 42664979, 42735406; Sep 2026)
- Circadian and bedroom-environment cardiovascular exposures: nighttime light and cardiac remodeling; sleep regularity and blood pressure (PMIDs 42715935, 42526099; Sep–Nov 2026)
- Unused cardiovascular information already in the chart: liver index scores, retinal imaging, multidomain function measures (PMIDs 42702521, 42551563, 42612584; Sep–Dec 2026)
- Treatment trade-offs in the oldest patients — marginal-quality donor kidneys may not beat dialysis after 75, with three times higher early mortality (PMID 42759976; Sep 2026)
- Quality of the nutrition evidence base itself under scrutiny: Dietary Inflammatory Index reviews rated low/critically low, premium phenol-enriched olive oil no better than standard, diet's cardiovascular benefit largely mediated through conventional risk factors (PMIDs 42435895, 42521082, 42617399; Oct 2026)
- Sex-specific and population-specific lifelong risk history: pregnancy complications, PCOS/PMOS, and cohort-dependent heart-brain associations that do not transfer across countries (PMIDs 42669304, 42727321, 42424748, 42767983; Sep–Oct 2026)
